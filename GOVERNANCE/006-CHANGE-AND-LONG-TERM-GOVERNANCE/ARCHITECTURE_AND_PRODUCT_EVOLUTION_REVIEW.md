# FEMC Architecture and Product Evolution Review

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Review Questions

- Does the proposed change improve family value?
- Does it preserve canonical meaning?
- Does it strengthen or weaken privacy?
- Does it introduce vendor dependence?
- Does it increase unnecessary complexity?
- Can existing families migrate safely?
- Can the capability be removed or replaced?
- What evidence supports the change?

## Principle

Growth is successful when FEMC becomes more capable without becoming less understandable, portable, or trustworthy.
