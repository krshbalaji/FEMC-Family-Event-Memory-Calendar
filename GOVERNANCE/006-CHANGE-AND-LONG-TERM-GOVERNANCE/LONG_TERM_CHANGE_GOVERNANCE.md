# FEMC Long-Term Change Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Change Horizons

### Immediate
Operational and implementation changes.

### Evolutionary
Capability, architecture, and product improvements.

### Foundational
Changes affecting FEMC's enduring identity, trust model, or constitutional principles.

## Rules

Long-term changes should consider:

- family value;
- compatibility;
- migration;
- portability;
- cost;
- complexity;
- security;
- privacy;
- future maintainability.

## Principle

Ten-year thinking means preserving the ability to change safely, not predicting every future technology.
