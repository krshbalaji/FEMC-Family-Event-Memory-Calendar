# FEMC Governance Continuity and Succession

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Purpose

Ensure FEMC's governing knowledge survives changes in people, teams, providers, and generations.

## Preserve

- constitutional intent;
- material decisions;
- architecture rationale;
- risk history;
- governance boundaries;
- major exceptions;
- succession responsibilities.

## Rules

No critical governance authority should depend solely on one person's undocumented memory.

## Principle

A ten-year architecture requires ten-year institutional memory.
