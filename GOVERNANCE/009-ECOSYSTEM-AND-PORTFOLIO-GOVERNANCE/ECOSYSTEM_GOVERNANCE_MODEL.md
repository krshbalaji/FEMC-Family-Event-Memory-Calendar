# FEMC Ecosystem Governance Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Ecosystem Participants

```text
FEMC CORE
  ├── FAMILY CAPABILITIES
  ├── AI CAPABILITIES
  ├── EXTERNAL SERVICES
  ├── PARTNERS
  └── FUTURE EXTENSIONS
```

## Rules

Every ecosystem participant requires a defined:

- purpose;
- boundary;
- permission model;
- dependency relationship;
- lifecycle;
- replacement/exit path.

## Principle

The ecosystem may grow around FEMC without displacing the canonical family core.
