# FEMC Platform Evolution Guardrails

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Guardrails

Future capabilities should preserve:

1. family-first product meaning;
2. privacy;
3. data integrity;
4. vendor independence;
5. human control;
6. understandable architecture;
7. portability;
8. production readiness;
9. maintainability;
10. long-term family value.

## Challenge

When a proposed capability conflicts with these guardrails, the burden is on the proposal to demonstrate why the conflict is justified and how the risk is controlled.

## Principle

Future expansion is welcome; uncontrolled architectural drift is not.
