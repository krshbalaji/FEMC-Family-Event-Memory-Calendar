# FEMC Post-Go-Live Assurance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Early Production Review

After significant production activation, review:

- actual family journeys;
- incidents;
- privacy/security events;
- data integrity;
- performance;
- capacity;
- support patterns;
- AI behavior;
- dependency stability;
- operational burden.

## Actions

Unexpected evidence should be routed to the responsible office.

Material systemic issues may require:

```text
Operations → Engineering → Architecture → Product → Constitution
```

as appropriate.

## Principle

Go-live starts the evidence cycle; it does not end governance.
