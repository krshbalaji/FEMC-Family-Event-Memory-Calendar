# FEMC Production Trust Assurance Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Assurance Areas

- privacy;
- security;
- canonical data integrity;
- family isolation;
- AI governance;
- portability;
- resilience;
- operational readiness;
- support safety.

## Evidence

Assurance should be based on:

- architecture decisions;
- engineering verification;
- operational evidence;
- security evidence;
- recovery exercises;
- incident learning;
- production metrics.

## Principle

Trust assurance is a continuing evidence process, not a one-time launch certificate.
