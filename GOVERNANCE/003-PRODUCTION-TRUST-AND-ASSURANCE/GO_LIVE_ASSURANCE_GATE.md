# FEMC Go-Live Assurance Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Gate Inputs

- Product readiness;
- Architecture validation;
- Engineering exit evidence;
- Security/privacy evidence;
- Operations readiness;
- Recovery evidence;
- Support readiness;
- Known risk register.

## Gate Outcomes

**APPROVED**

Evidence supports controlled production progression.

**CONDITIONAL**

Production may proceed only within explicitly bounded conditions and owned mitigations.

**BLOCKED**

Material trust, integrity, security, privacy, or readiness gaps remain.

## Principle

Go-live approval is a trust decision supported by evidence, not a celebration of feature completion.
