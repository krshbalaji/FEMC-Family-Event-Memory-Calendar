# FEMC Decision Traceability Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance Foundation
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Decision Chain

```text
QUESTION
 ↓
CONTEXT
 ↓
OPTIONS
 ↓
IMPACT
 ↓
AUTHORITY
 ↓
DECISION
 ↓
EVIDENCE
 ↓
REVIEW
```

## Material Decisions Should Record

- what was decided;
- why;
- alternatives considered;
- authority;
- risks;
- affected offices;
- reversibility;
- review trigger.

## Principle

Future FEMC teams should be able to understand why an important decision exists without reconstructing history from scattered conversations.
