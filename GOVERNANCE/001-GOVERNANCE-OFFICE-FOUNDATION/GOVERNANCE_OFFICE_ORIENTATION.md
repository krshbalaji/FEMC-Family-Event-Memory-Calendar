# FEMC Governance Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance Foundation
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Mission

Protect FEMC's long-term integrity by ensuring that product, architecture, engineering, operations, security, AI, and future ecosystem decisions remain aligned with the Constitution.

## Governance Owns

- cross-office decision integrity;
- constitutional compliance;
- material risk acceptance;
- escalation coordination;
- decision traceability;
- production trust assurance;
- change authority boundaries.

## Governance Does Not Own

Governance does not replace:

- Product authority;
- Architecture authority;
- Engineering authority;
- Operations authority;
- Security authority.

It ensures that those authorities remain within their defined boundaries.

## Principle

Governance protects the system of decision-making so that FEMC can evolve without losing its identity.
