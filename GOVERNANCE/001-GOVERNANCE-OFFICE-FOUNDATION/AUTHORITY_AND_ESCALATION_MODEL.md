# FEMC Authority and Escalation Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance Foundation
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Authority Flow

```text
OPERATIONS
ENGINEERING
ARCHITECTURE
PRODUCT
SECURITY
     │
     ▼
GOVERNANCE REVIEW
     │
     ▼
APPROPRIATE AUTHORITY
     │
     ▼
DECISION / ESCALATION
```

## Rules

A matter should be resolved at the lowest responsible authority that has sufficient mandate.

Escalate when a decision changes:

- constitutional meaning;
- product meaning;
- domain ownership;
- trust boundaries;
- material risk;
- long-term portability;
- major ecosystem dependency.

## Principle

Governance should prevent both unauthorized decisions and unnecessary bureaucracy.
