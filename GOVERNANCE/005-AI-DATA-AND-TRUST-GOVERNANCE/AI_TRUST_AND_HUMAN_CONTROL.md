# FEMC AI Trust and Human Control

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Human Control

Users and authorized operators must be able to understand material AI-assisted actions sufficiently to control, confirm, reject, or correct them where appropriate.

## High-Impact Areas

Stronger controls apply to:

- family relationship interpretation;
- historical facts;
- privacy-sensitive discovery;
- sharing;
- deletion;
- durable family records;
- consequential recommendations.

## Principle

AI can assist family intelligence, but humans remain responsible for meaningful family decisions.
