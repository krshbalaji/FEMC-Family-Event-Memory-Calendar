# FEMC AI Governance Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## AI Governance Areas

- purpose;
- authorized data;
- model/provider choice;
- output risk;
- human control;
- provenance;
- evaluation;
- privacy;
- security;
- portability;
- failure handling.

## Rules

AI must remain subordinate to FEMC's constitutional and domain rules.

AI output cannot silently become canonical family truth.

Material AI actions require controls appropriate to their consequences.

## Principle

FEMC governs AI as a capability inside the family platform, not as an authority above it.
