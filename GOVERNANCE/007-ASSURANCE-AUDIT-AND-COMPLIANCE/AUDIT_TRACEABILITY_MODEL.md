# FEMC Audit Traceability Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Traceability

Material claims should be traceable to appropriate evidence:

```text
PRINCIPLE
  ↓
POLICY
  ↓
ARCHITECTURE
  ↓
IMPLEMENTATION
  ↓
OPERATIONAL CONTROL
  ↓
EVIDENCE
```

## Rules

- Evidence must have appropriate ownership.
- Historical decisions should remain discoverable.
- Audit evidence must not unnecessarily expose family content.
- Gaps must remain visible until resolved or formally accepted.

## Principle

If a critical control cannot be demonstrated, its effectiveness should not be assumed.
