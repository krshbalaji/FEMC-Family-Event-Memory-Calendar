# FEMC Compliance and Change Readiness

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Objective

Ensure changing legal, contractual, platform, and industry obligations can be assessed without destabilizing FEMC's core architecture.

## Review Areas

- privacy obligations;
- data handling;
- security obligations;
- retention;
- portability;
- external-provider requirements;
- geographic considerations.

## Rules

External obligations must be translated into owned requirements and verified controls where applicable.

## Principle

Compliance should become an engineering and operational capability, not a last-minute legal checklist.
