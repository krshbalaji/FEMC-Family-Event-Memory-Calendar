# FEMC Assurance Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Purpose

Establish a continuing assurance model for privacy, security, integrity, reliability, AI, portability, and operational trust.

## Assurance Cycle

```text
DEFINE EXPECTATION
 ↓
COLLECT EVIDENCE
 ↓
ASSESS
 ↓
IDENTIFY GAP
 ↓
REMEDIATE / ACCEPT
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
REPORT
```

## Principle

Assurance is an evidence discipline, not a document-production exercise.
