# FEMC Knowledge Decay and Revalidation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Problem

Documentation can remain accurate in form while becoming wrong in practice.

## Revalidation Triggers

- major architecture change;
- production incident;
- provider change;
- regulatory change;
- significant product evolution;
- long periods without operational verification.

## Process

```text
OLD KNOWLEDGE
 ↓
COMPARE WITH EVIDENCE
 ↓
CONFIRM / CORRECT / RETIRE
 ↓
RECORD
```

## Principle

Knowledge is an operational asset only while it remains trustworthy.
