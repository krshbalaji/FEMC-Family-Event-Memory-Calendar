# FEMC Succession and Authority Continuity

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Purpose

Ensure critical responsibilities remain understandable and transferable as people and teams change.

## Requirements

For material responsibilities define:

- authority;
- scope;
- escalation path;
- required knowledge;
- operational dependencies;
- continuity owner.

## Rules

No critical authority should depend exclusively on undocumented personal knowledge.

## Principle

People may change; FEMC's institutional understanding must remain continuous.
