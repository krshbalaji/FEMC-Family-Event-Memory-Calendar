# FEMC Governance Metrics and Evidence

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Evidence Areas

Governance should maintain visibility into:

- unresolved material risks;
- exceptions;
- major incidents;
- privacy/security events;
- architecture changes;
- AI governance findings;
- portability readiness;
- operational readiness;
- capability portfolio health.

## Rules

Metrics are indicators, not substitutes for judgment.

A green metric must not override direct evidence of family harm or architectural failure.

## Principle

Governance measures what helps it make better decisions, not what merely looks good on a dashboard.
