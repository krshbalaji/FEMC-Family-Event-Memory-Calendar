# FEMC Material Decision Review

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Review Trigger

A material decision should be reviewed when:

- its assumptions materially change;
- production evidence contradicts expectations;
- risk increases;
- a dependency changes;
- family impact changes;
- a previously temporary decision becomes permanent.

## Review Questions

- Is the original rationale still valid?
- Is the decision still within authority?
- Has the risk changed?
- Has a simpler option emerged?
- Can the decision be reversed safely?

## Principle

A decision does not become correct merely because it is old.
