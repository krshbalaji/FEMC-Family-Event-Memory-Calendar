# FEMC Governance Review Cadence

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Purpose

Create a sustainable rhythm for reviewing FEMC decisions, risks, architecture evolution, AI behavior, operational evidence, and family trust.

## Review Horizons

### Continuous
Material incidents, exceptions, security/privacy events, and urgent trust concerns.

### Periodic
Risk registers, dependencies, AI governance, operational health, capability portfolio, and architecture drift.

### Strategic
Product direction, ecosystem evolution, portability, long-term maintainability, and constitutional alignment.

## Principle

Governance should review what evidence requires review—not generate meetings merely because a calendar says so.
