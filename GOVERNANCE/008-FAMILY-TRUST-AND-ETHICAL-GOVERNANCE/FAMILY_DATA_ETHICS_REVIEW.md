# FEMC Family Data Ethics Review

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Review Questions

Before introducing a material family-data capability ask:

1. Why is this information needed?
2. Who benefits?
3. Who could be harmed?
4. Who can access it?
5. Can the same value be achieved with less information?
6. Can the family understand the behavior?
7. Can the capability be reversed or removed?
8. Does it create unwanted dependence or surveillance?

## Principle

Just because FEMC can technically use family information does not mean FEMC should.
