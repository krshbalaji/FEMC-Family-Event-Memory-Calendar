# FEMC Family Trust Charter

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Purpose

Translate FEMC's commitment to family trust into durable governance expectations.

## Trust Commitments

FEMC should:

- respect family privacy;
- preserve data integrity;
- make meaningful system behavior understandable;
- avoid manipulative engagement;
- preserve human control;
- distinguish fact from AI interpretation;
- support family continuity and portability.

## Principle

Trust is a product property, an engineering property, and a governance responsibility.
