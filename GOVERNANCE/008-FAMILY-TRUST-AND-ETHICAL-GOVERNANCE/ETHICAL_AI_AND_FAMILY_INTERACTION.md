# FEMC Ethical AI and Family Interaction

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Governance Concerns

AI should not:

- fabricate family history;
- exploit emotional vulnerability;
- manipulate family relationships;
- expose private information;
- impersonate human family members deceptively;
- pressure users into unnecessary engagement.

AI should:

- assist appropriately;
- communicate uncertainty;
- respect authorization;
- preserve human choice;
- remain replaceable.

## Principle

FEMC's AI should deepen family connection without manufacturing dependency on FEMC itself.
