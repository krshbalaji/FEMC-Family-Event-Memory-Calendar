# FEMC Cross-Office Decision Protocol

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Trigger

Use this protocol when a decision materially crosses office boundaries.

## Flow

```text
INITIATING OFFICE
 ↓
IMPACT IDENTIFICATION
 ↓
AFFECTED OFFICES
 ↓
OPTIONS / TRADE-OFFS
 ↓
RESPONSIBLE AUTHORITY
 ↓
DECISION
 ↓
RECORD
 ↓
IMPLEMENT / VERIFY
```

## Rules

- Do not resolve cross-office conflicts through informal assumptions.
- Do not allow implementation convenience to override constitutional intent.
- Do not escalate routine local decisions unnecessarily.
- Record material dissent when it affects risk or future interpretation.

## Principle

Cross-office coordination exists to preserve system coherence, not to centralize every decision.
