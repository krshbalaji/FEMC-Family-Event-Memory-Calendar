# FEMC Product–Architecture–Engineering–Operations Alignment

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Alignment Chain

```text
PRODUCT
  ↓
ARCHITECTURE
  ↓
ENGINEERING
  ↓
OPERATIONS
  ↓
PRODUCTION EVIDENCE
  └──────────────→ PRODUCT
```

## Responsibilities

Product defines intended family value and meaning.

Architecture defines coherent system structure.

Engineering implements the approved architecture.

Operations protects production behavior.

Production evidence feeds learning back to the responsible authority.

## Principle

No office is complete in isolation. The lifecycle is intentionally circular.
