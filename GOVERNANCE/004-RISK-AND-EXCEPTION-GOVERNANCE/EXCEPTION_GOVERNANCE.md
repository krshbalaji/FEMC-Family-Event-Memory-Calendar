# FEMC Exception Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Purpose

Allow necessary deviations from established rules without allowing exceptions to become permanent hidden architecture.

## Every Material Exception Should Define

- rule being excepted;
- reason;
- scope;
- duration;
- risk;
- compensating controls;
- owner;
- review/expiry condition.

## Rules

- Security and privacy exceptions require appropriate specialist review.
- Constitutional principles are not casually waived through operational exception.
- Expired exceptions must be removed or deliberately renewed.
- Repeated exceptions indicate a possible architecture or product problem.

## Principle

An exception is a controlled deviation, not a new default.
