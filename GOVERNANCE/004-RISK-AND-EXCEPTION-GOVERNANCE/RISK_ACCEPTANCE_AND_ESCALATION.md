# FEMC Risk Acceptance and Escalation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Risk Flow

```text
IDENTIFY
 ↓
ASSESS
 ↓
MITIGATE
 ↓
REMAINING RISK
 ↓
ACCEPT / ESCALATE / BLOCK
```

## Acceptance

Risk acceptance requires an authority appropriate to the impact.

Risks affecting privacy, security, canonical integrity, or constitutional commitments require stronger review.

## Block Condition

If remaining risk violates a non-negotiable FEMC principle, it must not be accepted merely because delivery is convenient.

## Principle

Schedule pressure does not convert unacceptable risk into acceptable risk.
