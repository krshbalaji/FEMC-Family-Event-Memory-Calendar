# FEMC Risk Governance Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Purpose

Provide a common method for identifying, assessing, accepting, mitigating, escalating, and reviewing material FEMC risks.

## Risk Domains

- family trust;
- privacy;
- security;
- data integrity;
- product;
- architecture;
- engineering;
- operations;
- AI;
- dependency;
- portability;
- scalability;
- financial sustainability.

## Rule

Risk ownership follows the responsibility that can actually control or reduce the risk.

## Principle

A risk that is not owned is not governed.
