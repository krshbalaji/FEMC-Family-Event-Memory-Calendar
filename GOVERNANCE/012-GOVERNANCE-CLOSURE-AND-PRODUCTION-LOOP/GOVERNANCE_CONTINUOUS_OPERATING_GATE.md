# FEMC Governance Continuous Operating Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance Handoff
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Continuous Conditions

Governance should remain able to establish that:

- material decisions have appropriate authority;
- important risks have owners;
- exceptions remain bounded;
- family trust principles remain intact;
- AI remains governed;
- architecture remains coherent;
- production evidence can challenge assumptions;
- institutional memory remains current.

## Exit Meaning

This is not an exit from governance.

It is the transition from **building governance doctrine** to **operating governance through real evidence**.

## Principle

Once governance is mature, reality—not paperwork—becomes its primary teacher.
