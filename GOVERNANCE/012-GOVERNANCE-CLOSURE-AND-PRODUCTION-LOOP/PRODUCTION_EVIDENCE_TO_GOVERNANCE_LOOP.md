# FEMC Production Evidence to Governance Loop

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Governance Handoff
**Owner:** Governance Office

## Loop

```text
PRODUCTION
   ↓
EVIDENCE
   ↓
OPERATIONS / SECURITY / ENGINEERING
   ↓
GOVERNANCE REVIEW
   ↓
CLASSIFY
   ↓
RESPONSIBLE AUTHORITY
   ↓
DECISION
   ↓
IMPLEMENT
   ↓
VERIFY
   ↓
PRODUCTION
```

## Routing

Not every production lesson requires governance intervention.

Governance becomes active when evidence indicates material risk, cross-office conflict, authority ambiguity, or constitutional/product/architectural impact.

## Principle

Governance closes the loop without becoming the bottleneck.
