# FEMC Threat Modeling Framework

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Security Assurance
**Owner:** Security Office

## Threat Model Scope

Assess threats against:

- people;
- family contexts;
- canonical data;
- media;
- APIs;
- integrations;
- AI;
- infrastructure;
- operators;
- external providers.

## Questions

- What are we protecting?
- Who could attack or misuse it?
- What trust boundary could fail?
- What would the impact be?
- What control reduces the risk?
- How would we detect it?
- How would we recover?

## Principle

Threat modeling should begin with what matters to the family, not with a favorite security technology.
