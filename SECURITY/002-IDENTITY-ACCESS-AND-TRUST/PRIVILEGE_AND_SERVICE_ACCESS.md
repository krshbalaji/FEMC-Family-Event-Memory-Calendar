# FEMC Privilege and Service Access

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Security Architecture
**Owner:** Security Office

## Principles

- least privilege;
- explicit service identity;
- bounded permissions;
- short-lived credentials where appropriate;
- separation of duties;
- auditable privileged actions;
- rapid revocation.

## Service Access

Internal services must not assume unrestricted access merely because they operate inside FEMC infrastructure.

## Human Operations

Operational personnel should receive only the access required for their responsibilities.

## Principle

Being inside the platform is not equivalent to being trusted with everything inside it.
