## Authority

Security may require containment or escalation when credible evidence indicates material risk to:

- family privacy;
- authentication;
- authorization;
- canonical data integrity;
- platform security;
- trusted operations.

## Boundary

Security controls should be proportionate to risk.

## Principle

Security authority exists to protect trust, not to centralize unrelated decisions.
