# FEMC Family Membership and Relationship Context

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Architecture
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Context

Family membership may involve:

- biological relationships;
- legal relationships;
- chosen relationships;
- household relationships;
- historical relationships;
- evolving relationships.

## Rules

Relationship context must remain distinct from permission.

Relationship state may change over time and should preserve appropriate historical context.

## Principle

FEMC models family relationships with respect for real-world complexity rather than forcing every family into one rigid structure.
