# FEMC Family Person and Account Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Architecture
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Distinction

```text
PERSON
  ↓
FAMILY CONTEXT
  ↓
ACCOUNT
  ↓
AUTHENTICATED SESSION
```

A person may exist in family history without having an active account.

An account represents participation in FEMC; it is not the complete definition of the person.

## Principle

Family history must not disappear merely because an individual does not currently maintain an active FEMC account.
