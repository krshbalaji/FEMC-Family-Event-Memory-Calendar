# FEMC Identity Matching and Duplicate Resolution

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Governance
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Matching

Identity matching may use appropriate signals such as:

- explicit user confirmation;
- verified identifiers;
- family context;
- trusted source information.

Automated similarity is evidence, not proof.

## Duplicate Handling

Possible outcomes:

- confirmed same person;
- confirmed different people;
- unresolved;
- merge candidate;
- manual review.

## Principle

A false merge can permanently corrupt family history; uncertainty is safer than aggressive identity matching.
