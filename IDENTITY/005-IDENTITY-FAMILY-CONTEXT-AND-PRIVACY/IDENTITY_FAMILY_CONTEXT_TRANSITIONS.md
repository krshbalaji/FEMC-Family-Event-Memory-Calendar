# FEMC Identity Family Context Transitions

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Architecture
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Transitions

Family context can change through:

- joining a family space;
- invitation;
- relationship change;
- household change;
- separation;
- historical correction;
- account closure;
- family reorganization.

## Rules

Changes should preserve appropriate historical meaning while applying current authorization and privacy rules.

## Principle

Current family access and historical family context are different dimensions and must not be conflated.
