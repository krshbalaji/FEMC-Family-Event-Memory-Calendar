# FEMC Identity Privacy and Minimization

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Privacy
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Minimize

Identity systems should collect only information necessary for:

- account operation;
- authentication;
- recovery;
- family context;
- safety;
- legally or operationally necessary obligations.

## Rules

Do not expose identity attributes merely because they exist in the identity record.

Identity data must follow privacy classification, purpose limitation, and retention rules.

## Principle

Knowing more about a person does not automatically make FEMC better at serving that person.
