# FEMC Identity Closure and Continuous Assurance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Coverage

Identity foundations now address:

- identity authority;
- person/account distinction;
- family membership;
- relationships;
- matching and duplicates;
- lifecycle;
- correction;
- authentication;
- recovery;
- sessions/devices;
- privacy;
- family-context transitions;
- provider portability;
- takeover testing;
- reconciliation.

## Closure Rule

The Identity foundation is sufficiently defined when new identity capabilities can be evaluated against established principles for identity integrity, authentication, family context, privacy, recovery, and assurance.

## Continuous Loop

```text
REPRESENT
 ↓
AUTHENTICATE
 ↓
AUTHORIZE
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
CORRECT
 ↓
IMPROVE
 ↺
```

## Principle

Identity remains trustworthy when FEMC can evolve without losing track of who is represented, who is participating, and what historical context must be preserved.
