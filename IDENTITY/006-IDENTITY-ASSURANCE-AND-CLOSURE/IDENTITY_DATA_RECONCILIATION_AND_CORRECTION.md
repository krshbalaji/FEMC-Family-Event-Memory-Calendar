# FEMC Identity Data Reconciliation and Correction

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Assurance
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Reconcile

Identity reconciliation should compare:

- person representation;
- account state;
- family membership;
- relationship context;
- authentication state;
- authorization state.

## Rules

Conflicts must be surfaced and governed rather than silently resolved by whichever system was updated most recently.

## Principle

The latest technical record is not automatically the most correct family record.
