# FEMC Identity Account Recovery Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Assurance
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Recovery

Recovery should consider:

- claimant evidence;
- recovery channel trust;
- existing sessions;
- family context where appropriate;
- risk of takeover;
- notification of material changes;
- escalation for ambiguous cases.

## Rules

A successful recovery restores legitimate control without granting broader family access than the identity already possessed.

## Principle

Recovery restores identity control; it does not create new authority.
