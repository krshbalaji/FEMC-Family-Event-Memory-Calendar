# FEMC Identity Assurance Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Assurance
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Conditions

- [ ] Person and account concepts are distinct.
- [ ] Identity ownership is explicit.
- [ ] Family membership context is defined.
- [ ] Relationship context is defined separately from permission.
- [ ] Identity provenance is understood.
- [ ] Duplicate/matching behavior is governed.
- [ ] Lifecycle states are defined.
- [ ] Correction and recovery are controlled.
- [ ] Privacy and security implications are addressed.
- [ ] External identity dependencies are bounded.
- [ ] Material identity risks have owners.

## Outcomes

**READY** — identity behavior is sufficiently controlled.

**CONDITIONAL** — bounded identity risks have explicit safeguards.

**BLOCKED** — material identity ambiguity or integrity risk remains.

## Principle

Identity readiness means FEMC can reliably represent who is involved without confusing identity with access, relationship, or ownership.
