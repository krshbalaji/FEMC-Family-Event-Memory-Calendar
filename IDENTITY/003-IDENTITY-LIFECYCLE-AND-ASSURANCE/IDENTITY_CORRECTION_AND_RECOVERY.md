# FEMC Identity Correction and Recovery

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Assurance
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Correction

Identity corrections should preserve:

- reason;
- authority;
- previous state where historically relevant;
- affected relationships;
- downstream reconciliation.

## Recovery

Recovery should verify that the claimant is appropriately entitled to regain control of the relevant account.

## Principle

Identity correction repairs representation; it must not casually rewrite family history.
