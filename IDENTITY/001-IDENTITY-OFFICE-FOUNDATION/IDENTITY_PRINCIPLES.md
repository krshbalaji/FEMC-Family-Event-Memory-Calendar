# FEMC Identity Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Foundation
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Principles

1. Person identity is distinct from account identity.
2. Identity is distinct from authorization.
3. Family membership is contextual.
4. Identity provenance matters.
5. Identity changes must be traceable.
6. Duplicate identity should be prevented or governed.
7. Correction must not silently destroy historical meaning.
8. Identity data follows privacy boundaries.
9. External identity providers remain replaceable where practical.
10. Family relationships do not automatically grant access.
11. Uncertainty should not become fabricated identity.
12. Identity must remain understandable across generations.

## Principle

FEMC should know who is represented without pretending that identity, relationship, permission, and ownership are the same thing.
