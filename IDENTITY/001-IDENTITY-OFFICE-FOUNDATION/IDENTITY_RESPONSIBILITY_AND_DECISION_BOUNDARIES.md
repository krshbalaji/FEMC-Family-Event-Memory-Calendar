# FEMC Identity Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Foundation
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Identity Decisions

The Identity Office governs:

- identity representation;
- person/account linkage;
- identity lifecycle;
- identity provenance;
- membership context;
- identity correction;
- identity assurance requirements.

## Coordination

Identity coordinates with:

- Security for authentication and protection;
- Privacy for identity data handling;
- Data for canonical representation;
- Family domain for relationship meaning;
- Product for onboarding and family experience;
- Operations for account support.

## Principle

Identity establishes trustworthy representation of people; authorization decides what those represented people may do.
