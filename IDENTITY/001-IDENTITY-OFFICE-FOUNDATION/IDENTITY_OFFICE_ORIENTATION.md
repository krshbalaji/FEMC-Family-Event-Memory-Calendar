# FEMC Identity Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Identity Foundation
**Owner:** Identity Office

## Mission

Establish trustworthy identity and family-membership context so FEMC can understand who is participating, what relationships exist, and what identity state is authoritative without confusing identity with authorization.

## Identity Owns

- identity semantics;
- account/person linkage;
- family membership identity;
- identity lifecycle;
- identity verification context;
- identity provenance;
- identity quality and assurance.

## Identity Does Not Own

Identity does not redefine:

- constitutional principles;
- product strategy;
- family relationship meaning beyond identity representation;
- authorization policy;
- privacy authority;
- security implementation;
- engineering implementation.

## Principle

Knowing who someone is and knowing what they may do are related but distinct architectural concerns.
