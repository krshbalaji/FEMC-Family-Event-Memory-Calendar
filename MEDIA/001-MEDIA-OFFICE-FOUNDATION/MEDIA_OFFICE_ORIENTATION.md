# FEMC Media Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Media Foundation
**Owner:** Media Office

## Mission

Govern family media as durable family assets whose technical representation may evolve while their meaning, provenance, relationships, and authorized visibility remain trustworthy.

## Media Owns

- media-asset semantics;
- media metadata;
- asset relationships;
- media lifecycle;
- rendition policy;
- media provenance;
- media integrity and assurance.

## Media Does Not Own

The Media Office does not redefine:

- family identity;
- events;
- memories;
- privacy;
- security;
- product strategy;
- architecture;
- engineering implementation.

## Principle

A media file is an artifact. Its family meaning, provenance, and relationships are the durable asset.
