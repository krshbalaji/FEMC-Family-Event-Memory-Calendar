# FEMC Media Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Media Foundation
**Owner:** Media Office

## Media Decisions

The Media Office governs:

- asset representation;
- metadata;
- provenance;
- rendition relationships;
- media lifecycle;
- integrity;
- archival expectations;
- media-specific quality.

## Coordination

Media coordinates with:

- Memory for meaning and preservation;
- Events for event context;
- Identity for people context;
- Places for geographic context;
- Privacy for visibility;
- Data for canonical representation;
- Engineering for implementation.

## Principle

Media owns the integrity and lifecycle of family media assets without owning the family stories attached to them.
