# FEMC Family Media Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Media Foundation
**Owner:** Media Office

## Principles

1. Preserve original media where appropriate.
2. Preserve provenance.
3. Distinguish originals from derivatives.
4. Metadata is meaningful context.
5. Renditions are replaceable representations.
6. Media visibility follows family authorization.
7. Deletion and preservation are deliberate.
8. Integrity must be verifiable.
9. Provider storage must not become family meaning.
10. AI processing must preserve provenance.
11. Accessibility should improve media usefulness.
12. Future generations should be able to understand what an asset represents.

## Principle

FEMC should preserve the family artifact and enough context to understand why it matters.
