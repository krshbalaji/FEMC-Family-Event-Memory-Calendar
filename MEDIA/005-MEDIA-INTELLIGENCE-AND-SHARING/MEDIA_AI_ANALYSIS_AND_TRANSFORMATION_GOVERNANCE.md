# FEMC Media AI Analysis and Transformation Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Media Intelligence
**Owner:** Media Office

## AI May Assist With

- transcription;
- translation;
- tagging suggestions;
- object/scene description;
- duplicate detection;
- restoration suggestions;
- accessibility descriptions;
- authorized media summaries.

## AI Must Not Silently

- alter originals;
- invent people or events;
- remove provenance;
- expose restricted media;
- turn uncertain recognition into fact.

## Principle

AI-derived media information is derived intelligence unless explicitly confirmed and governed otherwise.
