# FEMC Media Closure and Continuous Evolution

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Media Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Media Office

## Coverage

Media foundations now address:

- asset authority;
- family media model;
- originals and derivatives;
- metadata;
- provenance;
- lifecycle;
- processing;
- transcoding;
- albums;
- collections;
- search;
- AI analysis;
- sharing;
- accessibility;
- scale;
- negative testing;
- provider migration;
- recovery.

## Closure Rule

The Media foundation is sufficiently defined when new media capabilities can be evaluated against established rules for preservation, provenance, transformation, discovery, accessibility, sharing, integrity, and provider independence.

## Principle

Media technology may evolve continuously; the family's claim to its meaningful artifacts remains the stable center.
