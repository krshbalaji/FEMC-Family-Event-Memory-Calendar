# FEMC Original Derivative and Rendition Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Media Architecture
**Owner:** Media Office

## Representation Chain

```text
ORIGINAL
   ↓
MASTER / PRESERVED REPRESENTATION
   ↓
DERIVATIVE
   ↓
RENDITION
   ↓
PRESENTATION
```

## Rules

Derivatives must not silently replace originals.

Material transformations should preserve source linkage and relevant processing provenance.

## Principle

A thumbnail, compressed video, transcript, or AI-enhanced image is a representation of the family asset, not automatically the asset itself.
