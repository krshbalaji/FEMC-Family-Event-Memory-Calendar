# FEMC Data Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Data Foundation
**Owner:** Data Office

## Data Decisions

The Data Office governs:

- canonical ownership;
- data definitions;
- quality rules;
- provenance;
- lifecycle;
- transformation standards;
- migration integrity;
- data-quality evidence.

## Escalate When

Changes materially affect:

- family-domain meaning;
- privacy;
- security;
- architecture;
- AI authority;
- constitutional commitments.

## Principle

Data governance protects meaning and integrity without becoming a substitute for Product, Architecture, Security, Privacy, or Engineering authority.
