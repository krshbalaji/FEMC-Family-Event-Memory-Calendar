# FEMC Data Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Data Foundation
**Owner:** Data Office

## Mission

Protect the meaning, integrity, availability, provenance, and long-term usefulness of FEMC's family information across its complete lifecycle.

## Data Owns

- data stewardship;
- canonical-data governance;
- data standards;
- data quality;
- provenance;
- lifecycle rules;
- migration/data integrity;
- data assurance.

## Data Does Not Own

The Data Office does not redefine:

- constitutional principles;
- family product meaning;
- architecture boundaries;
- security authority;
- privacy authority;
- engineering implementation;
- operational governance.

## Principle

FEMC data is not merely stored information. It is the durable representation of family meaning entrusted to the platform.
