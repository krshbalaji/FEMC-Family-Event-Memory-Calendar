# FEMC Analytics and AI Data Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Data Architecture
**Owner:** Data Office

## Data Classes

Analytics and AI may consume:

- canonical family data;
- authorized derived data;
- operational signals;
- external information;
- user-provided context.

## Requirements

AI and analytics pipelines should preserve:

- source provenance;
- authorization;
- purpose;
- transformation history where material;
- uncertainty;
- retention rules.

## Principle

The fact that data can be analyzed does not mean it should be analyzed, retained, or exposed.
