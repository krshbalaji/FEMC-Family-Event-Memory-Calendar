# FEMC Canonical Data Stewardship Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Data Governance
**Owner:** Data Office

## Stewardship

Every material canonical data domain should have:

- business meaning;
- authoritative owner;
- schema/definition;
- lifecycle;
- access policy;
- quality expectations;
- provenance;
- change process.

## Canonical Boundary

```text
CANONICAL FAMILY TRUTH
        ↓
AUTHORIZED DERIVATION
        ↓
SEARCH / AI / REPORTING / PRESENTATION
```

Derived systems must not silently become the authority because they are easier to query.

## Principle

Canonical stewardship keeps the family meaning stable while technical representations evolve.
