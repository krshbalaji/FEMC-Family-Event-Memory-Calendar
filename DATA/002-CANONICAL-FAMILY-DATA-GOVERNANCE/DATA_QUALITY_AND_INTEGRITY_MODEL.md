# FEMC Data Quality and Integrity Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Data Governance
**Owner:** Data Office

## Quality Dimensions

- accuracy;
- completeness;
- consistency;
- timeliness;
- validity;
- uniqueness;
- provenance;
- integrity.

## Rules

Quality checks should distinguish:

- invalid data;
- incomplete data;
- conflicting data;
- intentionally unknown data;
- historical data;
- derived data.

Do not silently invent values to make a dataset appear complete.

## Principle

Unknown is sometimes more trustworthy than fabricated certainty.
