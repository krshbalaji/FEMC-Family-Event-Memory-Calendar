# FEMC Data Backup Recovery and Preservation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Data Operations
**Owner:** Data Office

## Recovery Classes

Distinguish between:

- canonical family state;
- derived data;
- media;
- indexes;
- analytical outputs;
- configuration;
- operational evidence.

## Requirements

Recovery procedures should establish:

- trusted source;
- restoration sequence;
- integrity verification;
- reconciliation;
- acceptable data loss window;
- preservation obligations.

## Principle

A backup is trustworthy only when the restored information can be shown to preserve family meaning and integrity.
