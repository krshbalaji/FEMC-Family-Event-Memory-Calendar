# FEMC Data Sharing and Export Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Data Governance
**Owner:** Data Office

## Sharing Classes

- family-internal;
- explicitly shared;
- externally exported;
- operationally transmitted;
- archival/legacy transfer.

## Requirements

Material sharing should define:

- recipient;
- purpose;
- scope;
- format;
- authorization;
- retention expectation;
- revocation or withdrawal behavior where applicable.

## Principle

An export is a new representation of family information and must remain governed as such.
