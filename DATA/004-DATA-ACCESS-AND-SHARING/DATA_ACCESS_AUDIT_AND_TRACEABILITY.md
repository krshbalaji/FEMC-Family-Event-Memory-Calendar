# FEMC Data Access Audit and Traceability

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Data Assurance
**Owner:** Data Office

## Traceability

For material data operations, retain appropriate evidence of:

- actor/service;
- action;
- resource or resource class;
- authorization context;
- time;
- result;
- relevant change or transfer.

## Privacy

Audit evidence must not become an unnecessary duplicate of family content.

## Principle

Material data behavior should be explainable after the fact without creating a second uncontrolled family database.
