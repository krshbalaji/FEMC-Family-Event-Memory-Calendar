# FEMC Incident Operations Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Incident Lifecycle

```text
DETECT
 ↓
TRIAGE
 ↓
CONTAIN
 ↓
RECOVER
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
COMMUNICATE
 ↓
LEARN
```

## 1. Detect

Identify material failures as early as reasonably possible.

## 2. Triage

Prioritize by impact to family trust, integrity, privacy, and availability.

## 3. Contain

Limit damage before pursuing full restoration.

## 4. Recover

Restore trustworthy service and data.

## 5. Verify

Confirm that recovery did not introduce new integrity or access problems.

## 6. Communicate

Material incidents should be communicated honestly and proportionately.

## 7. Learn

Important incidents should produce durable operational learning.

## 8. Principle

An incident is not fully resolved until the family-facing risk is understood and controlled.
