# FEMC Observability and Operations Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Technical Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define operational observability as a means of understanding whether FEMC is healthy, trustworthy, and delivering its intended family experience.

## 1. Observe Without Exposing

Operational visibility must not require unnecessary access to family content.

## 2. User Impact First

Technical health should ultimately be connected to family impact.

## 3. Core Signals

Future operations should consider:

- availability;
- latency;
- errors;
- data integrity;
- security;
- capacity;
- dependency health;
- recovery health;
- AI service health.

## 4. Structured Diagnostics

Operational systems should make failures understandable enough to support diagnosis and recovery.

## 5. Privacy-Aware Telemetry

Telemetry should minimize sensitive family information.

## 6. AI Observability

AI operations should expose appropriate quality, latency, failure, cost, and dependency signals without unnecessarily exposing prompts or family content.

## 7. Principle

Observability exists to protect family trust, not to observe families.
