# FEMC Localization Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Separate Meaning From Presentation

Canonical domain meaning must remain independent from localized wording.

## 2. Language Is a First-Class Experience Concern

Users should eventually be able to interact in their preferred supported language without creating separate domain models.

## 3. Translation Must Preserve Relationship Meaning

Relationship terminology requires particular care because literal translation may change social meaning.

## 4. Time and Dates

Localized presentation must not corrupt the underlying temporal information.

## 5. Names and Scripts

The information model should support names across scripts and naming conventions.

## 6. Cultural Flexibility

The product should allow families to define their own traditions and important occasions rather than assuming a universal list.

## 7. AI Localization

AI-generated content must respect the selected language and cultural context while remaining grounded in canonical family information.

## 8. Accessibility and Localization

Localization should work together with accessibility rather than treating them as separate concerns.

## 9. Global Growth

New markets should not require rewriting the core family domain.

## 10. Principle

Localization changes how FEMC speaks.

It must not change what the family record means.
