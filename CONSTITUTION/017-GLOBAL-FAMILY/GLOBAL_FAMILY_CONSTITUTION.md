# FEMC Global Family Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Ensure FEMC can serve families across countries, cultures, languages, generations, and different family structures without forcing one worldview into the core product.

## 1. Family Is Not One Shape

FEMC must avoid assuming one universal definition of family structure.

The domain model should support different legitimate family contexts.

## 2. Language

FEMC should be capable of multilingual experiences.

Language must not change the canonical meaning of family relationships or memories.

## 3. Names

The platform should eventually accommodate naming conventions that differ across cultures and languages.

## 4. Dates and Calendars

Families may use different calendar systems, date conventions, time zones, and cultural observances.

The domain model must distinguish the underlying event from its presentation.

## 5. Celebrations

Celebrations may vary substantially across families and cultures.

FEMC should support family-defined meaning rather than imposing a universal celebration model.

## 6. Relationships

Relationship terminology may vary by culture and language.

The conceptual relationship model should remain precise while allowing localized expression.

## 7. Accessibility

Global scale includes differences in:

- age;
- literacy;
- language;
- ability;
- connectivity;
- device availability;
- technical familiarity.

## 8. Privacy Expectations

Privacy expectations and legal requirements may differ by jurisdiction.

FEMC must not assume that one region's practices automatically apply everywhere.

## 9. Local Context

Localization should be deeper than translation.

Where appropriate, it may include:

- date presentation;
- calendar conventions;
- language;
- cultural terminology;
- family traditions;
- communication patterns.

## 10. Constitutional Principle

FEMC should be globally adaptable without losing its universal foundation:

**help families remember, connect, celebrate, communicate, and preserve their story.**
