# FEMC Internationalization Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Product Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define internationalization as the ability for FEMC to serve families across languages, regions, cultures, and technical environments without fragmenting the canonical family model.

## 1. Global by Design

FEMC should be capable of serving families across jurisdictions and cultures.

## 2. Canonical Meaning

Localization must change presentation and expression without changing canonical family meaning.

## 3. Language

Family information may exist in multiple languages.

Translation should preserve meaning while retaining the original where appropriate.

## 4. Dates and Time

Regional date, time, calendar, and timezone presentation must remain distinguishable from canonical temporal meaning.

## 5. Names and Relationships

Names, family terms, and relationship language may vary culturally.

The underlying semantic model must remain stable.

## 6. AI Translation

AI translation is a derived transformation and must not silently replace source information.

## 7. Privacy

International operation must not weaken privacy or access boundaries.

## 8. Principle

FEMC should feel local to a family without becoming fragmented into unrelated products.
