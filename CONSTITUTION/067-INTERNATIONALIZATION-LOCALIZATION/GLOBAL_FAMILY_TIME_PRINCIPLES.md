# FEMC Global Family Time Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Canonical Time

An event's underlying temporal meaning must remain distinct from local display.

## 2. Time Zones

A global family may experience the same event across different time zones.

## 3. Day Boundaries

Local calendar dates may differ for the same moment.

## 4. Historical Dates

Historical information may use approximate dates or culturally specific calendars.

## 5. Recurrence

Recurring family occasions may depend on local calendars and traditions.

## 6. Presentation

Users should see time in a form appropriate to their context without altering the underlying event.

## 7. Principle

FEMC must represent family time honestly across borders.
