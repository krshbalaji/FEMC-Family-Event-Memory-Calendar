# FEMC Localization Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Presentation vs Meaning

Localized labels, dates, currencies, and terminology are presentation concerns unless explicitly part of canonical data.

## 2. Family Terminology

Families should be able to use culturally appropriate relationship and celebration terminology.

## 3. Translation

Important family text should preserve source context where practical.

## 4. Mixed-Language Families

A family may use multiple languages simultaneously.

## 5. Search

Search and discovery should support multilingual family information without merging unrelated meanings.

## 6. AI

AI translation and summarization must preserve uncertainty and context.

## 7. Accessibility

Localization and accessibility should work together.

## 8. Principle

Localization adapts FEMC to families; it does not ask families to adapt themselves to one cultural model.
