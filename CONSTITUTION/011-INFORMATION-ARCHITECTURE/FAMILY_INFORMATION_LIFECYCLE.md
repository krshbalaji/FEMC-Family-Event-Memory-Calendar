# FEMC Family Information Lifecycle

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define the conceptual lifecycle of family information.

## Lifecycle

```text
CAPTURE
   ↓
CONTEXTUALIZE
   ↓
CONFIRM / CLASSIFY
   ↓
PRESERVE
   ↓
DISCOVER
   ↓
SHARE / EXPERIENCE
   ↓
EVOLVE
   ↓
ARCHIVE / EXPORT
```

## Capture

Information enters FEMC through:

- human entry;
- media capture;
- import;
- integration;
- future assisted experiences.

## Contextualize

The information is connected to relevant:

- people;
- relationships;
- events;
- time;
- memories;
- media;
- family contexts.

## Confirm / Classify

Information may be:

- confirmed;
- uncertain;
- imported;
- inferred;
- AI-suggested.

The distinction must be preserved.

## Preserve

Canonical information should be protected against accidental loss or corruption.

## Discover

Families may rediscover information through different contextual paths.

## Share / Experience

Authorized people may view, communicate, celebrate, or create new memories around the information.

## Evolve

Information may change as family knowledge changes.

Changes must not silently destroy meaningful history.

## Archive / Export

Families should be able to preserve information beyond the current product implementation.

## Lifecycle Principle

The end of a UI feature must never automatically mean the end of the underlying family information.
