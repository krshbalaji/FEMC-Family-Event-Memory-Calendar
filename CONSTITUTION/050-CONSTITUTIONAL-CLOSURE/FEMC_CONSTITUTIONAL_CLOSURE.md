# FEMC Constitutional Closure

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Milestone  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Establish the conditions under which the Chief Product Architect's initial constitutional foundation may be considered complete.

## 1. Mission

FEMC exists to build a living family memory intelligence platform that helps families remember, connect, celebrate, communicate, and preserve their story.

## 2. Canonical Family Reality

The constitutional architecture recognizes a connected family reality:

**People → Relationships → Family Context → Events/Time → Memories → Media → Communication/Celebration → Legacy**

## 3. Cross-Cutting Trust

The entire ecosystem is governed by:

- privacy;
- security;
- consent;
- access;
- integrity;
- provenance;
- human agency;
- responsible AI;
- portability;
- resilience.

## 4. Technology Independence

Implementation technology remains subordinate to the family domain and constitutional mission.

## 5. AI

AI is an enabling intelligence layer.

It is not the owner of family truth and not a replacement for family relationships.

## 6. Long-Term Continuity

FEMC is designed to survive:

- technology changes;
- provider changes;
- interface changes;
- team changes;
- AI model changes;
- migrations;
- capability retirement.

## 7. Production Readiness Principle

The constitutional foundation does not claim that implementation is complete.

It establishes the principles against which implementation must be evaluated.

## 8. Closure Meaning

Constitutional closure means the initial product-architecture foundation is coherent enough to hand off into subsequent detailed architecture, engineering, governance, and implementation offices.

It does not mean FEMC can never evolve.

## 9. Principle

The constitution should constrain future work without preventing responsible evolution.
