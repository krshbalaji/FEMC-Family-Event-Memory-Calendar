# FEMC Post-Constitutional Handoff

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Handoff  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define what happens after the initial constitutional foundation.

## 1. Constitution Remains Above Implementation

The constitutional library remains the reference point for major product and architecture decisions.

## 2. Detailed Architecture

The next stage may define:

- logical architecture;
- technical architecture;
- data implementation;
- API contracts;
- security architecture;
- deployment architecture;
- operational architecture.

These must remain aligned with constitutional principles.

## 3. Engineering

Production code should begin only under the appropriate engineering office and after the necessary architecture and governance decisions exist.

## 4. Governance

Material deviations from constitutional principles require explicit review.

## 5. AI Council

Future AI Council members should orient themselves through the repository rather than relying on historical conversations.

## 6. Roadmap

Future capabilities may be added without reopening the entire constitution unless they reveal a genuine missing principle.

## 7. Living Constitution

The constitution may evolve through governed amendments.

Evolution must be deliberate, documented, and traceable.

## Final Principle

The foundation is not finished because no more ideas exist.

It is finished when future work can proceed without repeatedly rediscovering what FEMC is supposed to mean.
