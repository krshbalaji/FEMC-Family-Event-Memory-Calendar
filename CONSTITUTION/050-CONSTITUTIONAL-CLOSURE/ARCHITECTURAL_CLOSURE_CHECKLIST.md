# FEMC Architectural Closure Checklist

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Milestone Document  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

Before declaring the initial constitutional architecture complete, verify:

- [ ] Mission is explicit.
- [ ] Product identity is explicit.
- [ ] Family context is canonical.
- [ ] People and relationships are first-class.
- [ ] Events and time are defined.
- [ ] Memories and legacy are defined.
- [ ] Media and albums are contextual.
- [ ] Communication and celebrations are integrated.
- [ ] Search and discovery respect privacy.
- [ ] Consent and access are defined.
- [ ] Security and safety are defined.
- [ ] AI boundaries are defined.
- [ ] Data integrity and provenance are defined.
- [ ] Portability and migration are defined.
- [ ] Resilience and recovery are defined.
- [ ] Scalability principles are defined.
- [ ] Global and accessibility principles are defined.
- [ ] Ecosystem and vendor independence are defined.
- [ ] Documentation and institutional continuity are defined.
- [ ] Architectural evolution is governed.
- [ ] Capability retirement is addressed.
- [ ] Future interfaces are anticipated without premature technology commitment.

## Closure Test

Ask:

> Can a future architecture or engineering team begin detailed implementation without inventing the fundamental product meaning themselves?

If yes, the constitutional foundation has achieved its purpose.

If no, identify the missing constitutional responsibility before proceeding.
