# FEMC Archival Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Preserve Meaning

Archive information with enough context to remain understandable.

## 2. Preserve Provenance

Historical origin should remain identifiable where appropriate.

## 3. Preserve Relationships

Archival records should retain meaningful connections.

## 4. Access

Archived information remains subject to appropriate permissions.

## 5. Integrity

Archives should support confidence that information has not been improperly altered.

## 6. Format Evolution

Archives should be capable of surviving format and technology changes.

## 7. Human Access

Where practical, archival representations should be understandable without specialist software.

## 8. Legacy

Archives may become part of the family's long-term legacy.

## 9. Principle

An archive is not a forgotten storage area.

It is a preserved part of the family record.
