# FEMC Capability Retirement Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Retirement Sequence

```text
ASSESS
  ↓
ANNOUNCE / PLAN
  ↓
PRESERVE
  ↓
MIGRATE
  ↓
VERIFY
  ↓
RETIRE
  ↓
MONITOR
```

## 1. Assess

Understand what the capability owns, references, and exposes.

## 2. Plan

Identify affected families and dependent capabilities.

## 3. Preserve

Protect relevant family information before removal.

## 4. Migrate

Move information into the continuing canonical model where required.

## 5. Verify

Confirm that historical meaning and access expectations remain intact.

## 6. Retire

Remove the obsolete capability only after appropriate safeguards are satisfied.

## 7. Monitor

Watch for unexpected downstream effects.

## 8. Principle

Retirement should reduce obsolete complexity without reducing family history.
