# FEMC Archive and Retirement Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define how FEMC retires capabilities and preserves the family information those capabilities created.

## 1. Capability Retirement Is Not Data Retirement

Removing a feature must not automatically remove the underlying family information.

## 2. Archive Is Meaningful State

Archived information should remain distinguishable from active information.

## 3. Preservation

Information with long-term family value should have an appropriate preservation path.

## 4. Retirement Planning

Before retiring a major capability, evaluate:

- affected information;
- dependencies;
- export;
- migration;
- permissions;
- user impact;
- historical context.

## 5. User Communication

Material retirement affecting families should be communicated clearly.

## 6. AI Retirement

Retiring an AI model or provider must not destroy canonical family information or make it permanently inaccessible.

## 7. Historical Continuity

Retired interfaces may disappear while family history remains.

## 8. Principle

Technology may retire.

Family memory should not disappear merely because a feature did.
