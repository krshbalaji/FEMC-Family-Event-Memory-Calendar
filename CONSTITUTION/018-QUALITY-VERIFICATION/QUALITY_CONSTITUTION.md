# FEMC Quality Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define quality as a product property of FEMC rather than merely a testing activity.

## Quality Principle

FEMC quality means that families can trust the platform to preserve meaning, behave predictably, protect information, and remain useful as it evolves.

## Quality Dimensions

Quality includes:

- correctness;
- data integrity;
- privacy;
- security;
- reliability;
- usability;
- accessibility;
- performance;
- interoperability;
- recoverability;
- maintainability;
- long-term preservation.

## Domain Correctness

A technically functioning feature is not correct if it misrepresents family relationships, events, memories, permissions, or historical context.

## AI Quality

AI quality includes:

- factual grounding;
- appropriate uncertainty;
- permission-aware context;
- predictable behavior;
- meaningful usefulness;
- human control.

## Regression Principle

A new capability must not silently degrade established family capabilities or historical data.

## Quality Before Scale

Scaling an incorrect or untrustworthy product only multiplies the problem.

## Quality Before Novelty

New technology must not be accepted merely because it is technically impressive.

## Quality as a Lifecycle

Quality begins during product definition and continues through:

**Design → Implementation → Verification → Operation → Evolution → Retirement**

## Ten-Year Test

Ask:

> Would a family trust this behavior after relying on FEMC for ten years?

If not, the quality bar has not been met.
