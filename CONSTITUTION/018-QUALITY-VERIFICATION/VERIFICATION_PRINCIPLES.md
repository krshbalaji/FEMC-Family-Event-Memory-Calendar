# FEMC Verification Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Verify Meaning

Verification must test domain meaning, not only technical behavior.

## 2. Verify Relationships

Important relationships between:

- people;
- relationships;
- events;
- memories;
- media;
- permissions

must be verified.

## 3. Verify Historical Preservation

Changes and migrations must be checked for loss or corruption of historical meaning.

## 4. Verify Privacy Boundaries

Verification must include unauthorized-access scenarios, not only successful user journeys.

## 5. Verify AI Boundaries

AI must be tested for:

- hallucinated family facts;
- unauthorized context;
- incorrect relationships;
- misleading confidence;
- unsafe generated actions.

## 6. Verify Failure

Important capabilities should be evaluated under failure and degraded conditions.

## 7. Verify Interoperability

Imports and exports must preserve meaning where the contract requires it.

## 8. Verify Accessibility

Core journeys should remain usable across supported accessibility needs.

## 9. Verification Evidence

Material quality claims should be supported by repeatable evidence where practical.

## 10. No False Confidence

Passing a test does not prove that an entire system is correct.

Quality communication must distinguish verified behavior from assumptions and unknowns.
