# FEMC Legacy Preservation Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Long Horizon

Legacy design should consider decades rather than product release cycles.

## 2. Interpretability

Future generations must be able to understand what preserved information represents.

## 3. Portability

Legacy information should remain portable beyond FEMC where practical.

## 4. Provenance

Future readers should be able to distinguish original information from later interpretation.

## 5. Technology Independence

Legacy must not depend on one AI model, interface, vendor, or storage implementation.

## 6. Privacy Across Generations

Preservation does not eliminate the need for appropriate access controls.

## 7. Principle

A successful legacy system lets future generations encounter family history with context, dignity, and trust.
