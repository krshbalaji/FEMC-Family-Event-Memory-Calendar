# FEMC Family Memory and Legacy Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Product Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define family memory and legacy as the long-term purpose that differentiates FEMC from ordinary scheduling software.

## 1. Memory Is More Than Media

A memory may include:

- people;
- relationships;
- place;
- time;
- story;
- emotion;
- media;
- context;
- celebration;
- meaning.

## 2. Events Can Become Memories

A calendar event may become part of family history when it acquires meaningful context.

## 3. Memory Can Exist Without an Event

Important family memories may have no calendar origin.

## 4. Legacy

Legacy is the continuity of family knowledge and meaning across generations.

## 5. Historical Honesty

Confirmed facts, personal recollections, interpretations, and AI-generated narratives should remain distinguishable.

## 6. Preservation

Long-term preservation must consider both content and the relationships that make content meaningful.

## 7. Principle

FEMC preserves the family story, not merely the family's files.
