# FEMC Memory Context Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Context Is Meaning

A photograph without its people, time, place, or story may lose much of its family meaning.

## 2. Connections

Memories should be capable of connecting to authorized:

- people;
- relationships;
- events;
- places;
- albums;
- other memories.

## 3. Multiple Perspectives

Different family members may remember the same event differently.

The system should allow this without automatically declaring one perspective false.

## 4. Corrections

Important corrections should preserve appropriate historical context.

## 5. AI Storytelling

AI may help transform authorized memories into stories, captions, summaries, or timelines, but generated narrative must remain distinguishable from source history.

## 6. Principle

The richer the context, the more durable the memory.
