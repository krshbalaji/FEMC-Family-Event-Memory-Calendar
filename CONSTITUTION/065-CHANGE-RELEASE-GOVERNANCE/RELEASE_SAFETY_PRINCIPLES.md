# FEMC Release Safety Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Pre-Release

Confirm:

- acceptance criteria;
- security implications;
- privacy implications;
- data migration needs;
- observability;
- rollback or mitigation;
- operational ownership.

## 2. Controlled Deployment

Where appropriate, use staged or reversible release approaches.

## 3. Monitor

Observe real-world behavior after release.

## 4. Verify

Confirm that family journeys and trust boundaries remain correct.

## 5. Roll Back or Mitigate

If material harm appears, act quickly to protect users.

## 6. Learn

Important releases and failures should improve future engineering practice.

## 7. Principle

A safe release is one that can be understood, observed, and corrected.
