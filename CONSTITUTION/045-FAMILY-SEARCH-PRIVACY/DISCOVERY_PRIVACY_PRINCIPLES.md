# FEMC Discovery Privacy Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Discoverability Is Not Visibility

Information may exist in the family record without being discoverable by every participant.

## 2. Contextual Visibility

Discovery should reflect the user's authorized family context.

## 3. Suggestions

Search suggestions must not expose information the user is not authorized to retrieve.

## 4. AI Summaries

Summaries should contain only authorized information.

## 5. Cross-Memory Connections

Connecting two memories may reveal sensitive context and must therefore respect access boundaries.

## 6. Shared Devices

Future experiences must consider the possibility that a device is accessible to multiple people.

## 7. Voice

Voice discovery requires particular care because responses may be audible to others.

## 8. Principle

Privacy must apply not only to what FEMC stores, but also to what FEMC helps people discover.
