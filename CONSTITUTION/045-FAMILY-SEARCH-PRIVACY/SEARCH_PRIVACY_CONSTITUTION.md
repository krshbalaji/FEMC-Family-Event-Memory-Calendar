# FEMC Search Privacy Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define privacy boundaries for searching and discovering family information.

## 1. Search Is Access

A search request must be treated as an attempt to access information, not merely as a technical query.

## 2. Permission Before Retrieval

Search must respect the user's authorization before returning meaningful protected information.

## 3. Query Leakage

Search systems must avoid revealing private information through:

- autocomplete;
- suggestions;
- result counts;
- snippets;
- ranking;
- error messages;
- AI summaries.

## 4. Family Context

The existence of a relationship does not automatically authorize discovery of its details.

## 5. AI Retrieval

AI retrieval must remain within the same permission boundary as ordinary search.

## 6. Sensitive Queries

Future architecture should provide additional safeguards for especially sensitive family information.

## 7. No Inference Leakage

The system should not reveal sensitive facts merely by allowing users to infer them from search behavior or results.

## 8. Audit

Material search-access events should support appropriate accountability without recording unnecessary family content.

## 9. Principle

A powerful search system must remain a privacy-preserving search system.
