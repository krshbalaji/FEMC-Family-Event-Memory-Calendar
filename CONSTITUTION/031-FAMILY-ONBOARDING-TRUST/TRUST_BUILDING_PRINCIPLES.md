# FEMC Trust-Building Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Explain Before Asking

Users should understand important reasons before being asked for sensitive information.

## 2. Show Control

Important privacy and sharing controls should be discoverable and understandable.

## 3. Earn Personalization

FEMC should become more personal through authorized family context, not through unexplained data collection.

## 4. Respect Uncertainty

Families should be able to leave information unknown.

## 5. Easy Correction

Users should be able to correct important information without unnecessary friction.

## 6. No Hidden Expansion

A capability should not quietly broaden the use of family information beyond the user's reasonable expectation.

## 7. Consistency

Trust grows when FEMC behaves consistently across devices, features, AI experiences, and integrations.

## 8. Transparency During Failure

When something goes wrong, explanations should be honest and understandable.

## 9. Long-Term Trust

Trust should be treated as a cumulative product asset.

## 10. Principle

Every interaction should either preserve or strengthen trust; it should never casually spend trust for convenience.
