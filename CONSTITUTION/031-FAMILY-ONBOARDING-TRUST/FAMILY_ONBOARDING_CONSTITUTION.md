# FEMC Family Onboarding Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define onboarding as the beginning of a trusted family relationship with FEMC, not merely an account creation workflow.

## 1. Trust Begins Before Data Entry

Families should understand what FEMC is, what it can do, and how their information will be handled before being asked to provide meaningful family data.

## 2. Progressive Introduction

Do not force families to understand the entire platform on the first interaction.

Introduce complexity as it becomes relevant.

## 3. Family Context

Onboarding should establish enough context to make the family experience meaningful without demanding unnecessary information.

## 4. Identity and Family Are Distinct

Creating an account does not automatically establish the complete family structure.

Family relationships should be built intentionally.

## 5. Consent and Control

Important permissions and sharing decisions should be understandable.

## 6. Data Import

Imported information should retain provenance and should not automatically become confirmed family truth.

## 7. AI Introduction

If AI is introduced during onboarding, its role and boundaries should be understandable.

## 8. Privacy by Default

Initial configuration should favor appropriate privacy-preserving choices.

## 9. No Manipulative Onboarding

FEMC should not use pressure, artificial urgency, or emotional manipulation to encourage data submission.

## 10. Principle

The first FEMC experience should make a family feel that its story is being entrusted to a careful steward—not harvested by an application.
