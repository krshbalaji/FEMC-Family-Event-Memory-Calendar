# FEMC Conceptual Context Map

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Describe how the major FEMC contexts relate without prescribing technical services.

## Primary Context

### Family Memory Context

The central context containing the meaningful family record.

It connects:

- People
- Relationships
- Family membership
- Events
- Memories
- Media
- Time
- Legacy

This context is the primary source of product meaning.

## Supporting Contexts

### Identity Context

Establishes the identity participating in FEMC.

### Trust Context

Controls permissions, visibility, sharing, and authorized access.

### Communication Context

Handles family communication and notification intent.

### Celebration Context

Coordinates activities around meaningful occasions.

### Intelligence Context

Provides search, discovery, organization, recommendations, and AI assistance using authorized information.

### Preservation Context

Supports long-term storage, export, archival, recovery, and generational continuity.

### Integration Context

Connects external systems without allowing external systems to redefine FEMC's canonical domain meaning.

## Context Relationship Principle

Supporting contexts consume and contribute meaningful information through defined conceptual relationships.

They must not silently create competing sources of truth.

## AI Boundary

AI operates as an intelligence context over authorized information.

AI does not become the owner of:

- Family facts
- Relationships
- Memories
- Historical truth
- Identity

AI may propose, summarize, classify, organize, or assist subject to product governance.

## Integration Boundary

External providers are replaceable dependencies.

FEMC's canonical family model must remain independent of any provider-specific representation.
