# FEMC Recovery and Continuity Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Recovery Layers

FEMC should eventually consider recovery at multiple levels:

1. Individual information
2. Family context
3. Media
4. Application capability
5. Platform
6. External dependency

## Recovery Principle

Recovery should restore not only records but their meaningful relationships.

A recovered photograph without its associated memory context may be technically present but functionally incomplete.

## Continuity

Continuity planning should consider:

- service interruption;
- provider outage;
- regional failure;
- compromised credentials;
- corrupted information;
- failed migrations;
- major technology replacement.

## Verification

Recovery must eventually include ways to establish that restored information is trustworthy.

## User Confidence

Where a serious incident affects family information, communication should be understandable and honest.

## Long-Term Archive

Critical family information should have a preservation strategy independent of ordinary application availability.

## Principle

The more irreplaceable the information, the stronger the continuity requirement.
