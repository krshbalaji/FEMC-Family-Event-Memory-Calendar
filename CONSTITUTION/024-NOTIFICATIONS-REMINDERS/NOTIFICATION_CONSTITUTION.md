# FEMC Notification Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define notifications as a supporting communication mechanism rather than the center of the FEMC experience.

## 1. Context Before Notification

Every meaningful notification should have a clear underlying family context.

A notification is not itself the canonical event, memory, relationship, or communication.

## 2. Relevance

Notifications should provide meaningful value to the recipient.

## 3. Permission

Notification delivery must respect the visibility and permission of the underlying information.

## 4. User Control

Users should have meaningful control over notification preferences where appropriate.

## 5. Calm by Default

FEMC should avoid unnecessary notification volume.

More notifications do not equal more family value.

## 6. Urgency Must Be Real

The system should not manufacture urgency to increase engagement.

## 7. AI Notifications

AI-generated notification content must remain grounded in authorized family context.

AI should not invent urgency, memories, relationships, or emotional claims.

## 8. Accessibility

Future notification channels should consider text, voice, visual, and other accessible modalities.

## 9. Failure

Notification failure should not corrupt the underlying family information.

## 10. Principle

The family record must remain valuable even when all notifications are turned off.
