# FEMC Reminder Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Reminder Is a View

A reminder is an assistance mechanism around an underlying event, task, or family context.

## 2. Event First

Where a reminder relates to a family event, the event remains canonical.

## 3. User Intent

Important reminders should reflect an intentional family need rather than system-generated engagement goals.

## 4. Respect Time

Reminder frequency and timing should respect the user's preferences and context.

## 5. No Reminder Spam

A useful reminder is better than many low-value reminders.

## 6. Context

Where appropriate, reminders may include relevant:

- people;
- relationships;
- previous memories;
- event information.

Only authorized context may be used.

## 7. AI Assistance

AI may suggest useful reminders but should distinguish suggestions from user-created commitments.

## 8. Historical Integrity

Changing a reminder must not alter the underlying historical event unless explicitly intended.

## 9. Principle

FEMC should help families remember what matters without becoming another source of noise.
