# FEMC Human Agency Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Principle 1 — Suggest, Do Not Assume

AI recommendations should remain recommendations unless the user explicitly authorizes the relevant action.

## Principle 2 — Explain Consequences

Actions affecting family information, sharing, or history should have understandable consequences.

## Principle 3 — Easy Correction

Users should be able to correct AI mistakes without fighting the system.

## Principle 4 — No Hidden Persuasion

Personalization must not become manipulation.

## Principle 5 — User Can Disagree

The system must allow users to reject AI suggestions.

## Principle 6 — User Can Continue Without AI

Core family functionality should not become unusable merely because AI is unavailable or declined.

## Principle 7 — Preserve Human Relationships

AI should strengthen family communication rather than attempt to become a substitute for it.

## Principle 8 — Respect Silence

Not every memory requires a notification.

Not every event requires a recommendation.

Not every family member needs to be prompted.

## Principle 9 — Calm Intelligence

The best AI behavior may sometimes be to remain silent.

## Principle 10 — Ten-Year Test

Ask:

> If a family becomes deeply dependent on FEMC, will its AI still leave the family more capable and more connected to one another?
