# FEMC Vendor Independence Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. No Unnecessary Lock-In

Do not create dependencies that exist only because they are convenient.

## 2. Critical Dependencies

For critical providers, document:

- purpose;
- data exposure;
- failure impact;
- replacement difficulty;
- migration path.

## 3. AI Provider Independence

AI models and providers must remain replaceable where practical.

## 4. Cloud Independence

Infrastructure decisions should not make family portability impossible.

## 5. Communication Independence

External communication channels should remain integration boundaries rather than becoming the family record.

## 6. Storage Independence

Storage providers should not become the sole interpreters of family meaning.

## 7. Principle

FEMC may depend on technology.

It must not become hostage to technology.
