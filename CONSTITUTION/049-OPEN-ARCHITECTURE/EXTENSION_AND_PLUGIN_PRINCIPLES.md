# FEMC Extension and Plugin Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Extensions Are Bounded

Extensions should operate within explicit permissions and contracts.

## 2. Canonical Protection

An extension must not silently redefine canonical family truth.

## 3. Least Privilege

Extensions should receive only the information and actions necessary for their purpose.

## 4. Failure Isolation

A faulty extension should not compromise the core family record.

## 5. Replaceability

Families should not lose their history if an extension disappears.

## 6. Quality

Extensions should meet appropriate FEMC standards for trust, privacy, accessibility, and reliability.

## 7. AI Extensions

AI extensions follow the same boundaries as other extensions.

## 8. Principle

The ecosystem may extend FEMC's capabilities without fragmenting FEMC's identity.
