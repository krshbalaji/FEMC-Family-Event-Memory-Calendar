# FEMC Open Architecture Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define architectural openness as protection against unnecessary technical and vendor lock-in.

## 1. Open by Principle

FEMC should prefer interoperable, documented, and replaceable architectural boundaries where practical.

## 2. Openness Does Not Mean Uncontrolled Exposure

Open architecture must remain compatible with privacy, security, and access controls.

## 3. Canonical Domain Independence

The family domain must not be shaped solely by external platform limitations.

## 4. Replaceable Providers

Important external dependencies should have boundaries that permit future replacement where justified.

## 5. Documented Contracts

Material interfaces and conceptual contracts should be documented.

## 6. Technology Neutrality

The constitution does not mandate open-source or proprietary technology.

It mandates responsible architectural independence.

## 7. Ecosystem

Future partners and extensions should be able to integrate through governed contracts.

## 8. Principle

FEMC should be open enough to evolve, controlled enough to remain safe, and stable enough to preserve family meaning.
