# FEMC Album Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define albums as intentional collections of family memories and media.

## 1. Album Is a Collection

An album groups meaningful family content.

It should not become a competing source of truth.

## 2. Dynamic and Curated Possibilities

Future albums may be:

- manually curated;
- event-based;
- time-based;
- relationship-based;
- AI-assisted;
- automatically assembled.

## 3. AI-Curated Albums

AI may suggest collections based on authorized information.

Suggestions should remain distinguishable from intentional human curation.

## 4. Historical Context

Albums may themselves become part of family history.

## 5. Permissions

Album visibility does not automatically override the permissions of its contents.

## 6. Empty and Changing Collections

An album may evolve as family information changes.

Its historical meaning should be preserved where appropriate.

## 7. Portability

Album structure and meaningful associations should be considered in future export.

## 8. Principle

An album should help a family tell a story, not merely store a pile of files.
