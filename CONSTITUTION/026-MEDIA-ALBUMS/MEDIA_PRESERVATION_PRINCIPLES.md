# FEMC Media Preservation Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Preserve Context

Media without associated context may lose historical value.

## 2. Preserve Original Where Appropriate

Where practical, preservation should distinguish original media from transformed or derived versions.

## 3. Preserve Metadata

Relevant metadata may include:

- date;
- people;
- event;
- description;
- provenance;
- relationships;
- album membership.

## 4. Derived Versions

Thumbnails, compressed copies, AI-enhanced versions, transcriptions, and descriptions should remain distinguishable from source media.

## 5. Integrity

Future systems should be able to establish whether important media has been altered or corrupted.

## 6. Migration

Media preservation must consider future format and storage changes.

## 7. Privacy

Preservation does not imply unrestricted visibility.

## 8. Long-Term Principle

A future family member should be able to understand both the media and its place in the family story.
