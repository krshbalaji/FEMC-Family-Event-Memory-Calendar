# FEMC Media Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define photos, videos, audio, and future media as supporting components of family memory rather than isolated file storage.

## 1. Media Is Evidence and Expression

Media may preserve evidence of an event, express emotion, or support a family story.

Its meaning comes from context.

## 2. Media Is Not the Entire Memory

A photo may represent a memory without containing the complete meaning of that memory.

## 3. Context

Media should be capable of association with relevant:

- people;
- relationships;
- events;
- memories;
- albums;
- stories.

## 4. Provenance

Where practical, media should retain appropriate origin and metadata.

## 5. Integrity

Media integrity includes both the file and its relationship to family context.

## 6. Privacy

Media may be highly sensitive.

Access must follow applicable permissions.

## 7. AI Media Processing

AI may assist with:

- organization;
- description;
- search;
- transcription;
- classification.

AI output must remain distinguishable from confirmed family information.

## 8. Future Media

The model should be extensible to future media types without redesigning the concept of memory.

## 9. Portability

Media preservation should consider the relationship between media and its contextual metadata.

## 10. Principle

FEMC should preserve not only what a family captured, but why it mattered.
