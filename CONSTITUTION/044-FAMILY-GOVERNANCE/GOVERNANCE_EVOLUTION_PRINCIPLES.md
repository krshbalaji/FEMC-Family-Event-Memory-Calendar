# FEMC Family Governance Evolution Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Start Simple

Governance should be as simple as the family's real needs allow.

## 2. Grow With Complexity

Larger or multi-generational family contexts may require more structured governance.

## 3. Preserve Human Agency

Automation should support governance, not obscure who is responsible.

## 4. Document Material Changes

Important governance changes should have durable records.

## 5. Avoid Permanent Power

Where appropriate, governance authority should be reviewable and changeable.

## 6. Multi-Generational Continuity

Governance should support transitions between generations without breaking the family record.

## 7. Cultural Flexibility

Governance terminology and practices may vary by family and culture.

## 8. Principle

The governance model should evolve with the family while preserving clear responsibility and trust.
