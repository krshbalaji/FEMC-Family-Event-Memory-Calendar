# FEMC Family Stewardship Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Stewardship Is Responsibility

A steward protects family information for present and future participants.

## 2. Least Authority

Stewards should receive only the authority necessary for their responsibilities.

## 3. Accountability

Material stewardship actions should be appropriately accountable.

## 4. Succession

Future architecture should support changes in stewardship over time.

## 5. Continuity

Stewardship should support preservation across generations.

## 6. Privacy

A stewardship role does not justify unrestricted access to every private family item.

## 7. Human Judgment

Important family governance decisions should remain under appropriate human control.

## 8. AI Assistance

AI may help summarize, organize, or surface governance information but should not silently make consequential governance decisions.

## 9. Principle

A steward is a custodian of family continuity, not an owner of family memory.
