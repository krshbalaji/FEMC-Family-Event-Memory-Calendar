# FEMC Interoperability Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define the principles that allow FEMC to exchange information with external systems without surrendering canonical meaning or vendor independence.

## Interoperability Principle

FEMC should be capable of communicating with external systems while remaining conceptually independent of them.

## Canonical Boundary

External systems may provide:

- information;
- media;
- services;
- communication channels;
- AI capabilities;
- identity services.

They must not silently redefine FEMC's canonical family model.

## Import

Imported information should retain appropriate provenance and should not automatically be treated as confirmed family truth.

## Export

Export should preserve meaningful relationships and metadata where practical.

## Integration

Integrations should be:

- purpose-bound;
- permission-aware;
- replaceable;
- observable;
- documented.

## Provider Independence

No integration should make the family record permanently dependent on one provider unless there is a compelling and explicitly governed reason.

## Failure Isolation

An external provider failure should not corrupt the canonical family record.

## Evolution

Interoperability architecture should support future providers and interaction methods without requiring redesign of the family domain.

## User Control

Where an integration affects family information, users should understand the important consequences of enabling it.

## Ten-Year Test

Ask:

> If the external provider disappears in five years, can FEMC preserve the family meaning and continue operating?
