# FEMC Portability Contract Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define what a future portability contract must protect without choosing a technical file format yet.

## Portability Must Preserve

Where applicable:

- stable identifiers;
- people;
- relationships;
- family membership;
- events;
- dates and temporal meaning;
- memories;
- media references;
- provenance;
- descriptions;
- relevant historical context.

## Meaning Over Structure

A technically complete export that loses relationships or historical context is not a successful portability solution.

## Machine and Human Access

Future portability should consider both:

- machine-readable representation;
- understandable human representation.

## Provider Migration

Moving between storage, AI, communication, or infrastructure providers should not require reconstructing the family's history from scratch.

## Future Contract

The eventual technical portability contract must be versioned and designed for evolution.

## Principle

FEMC should make it easier for families to leave, migrate, archive, or preserve their information responsibly—not harder.
