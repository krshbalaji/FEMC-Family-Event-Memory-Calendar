# FEMC Family Graph Evolution Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Stable Semantics

The meaning of core relationships should evolve deliberately.

## 2. Extensible Vocabulary

New relationship concepts should be possible without redesigning the entire model.

## 3. No Relationship Explosion

Do not create dozens of technical relationship types when a smaller semantic model is sufficient.

## 4. Historical Preservation

Changing a relationship should not silently rewrite historical context.

## 5. Multiple Family Contexts

The graph should support people participating in different legitimate family contexts.

## 6. AI-Assisted Evolution

AI may help identify possible connections but should not become the authority that defines family relationships.

## 7. Migration

Graph representation changes must preserve relationship meaning.

## 8. Principle

The family graph should become richer over time without becoming conceptually chaotic.
