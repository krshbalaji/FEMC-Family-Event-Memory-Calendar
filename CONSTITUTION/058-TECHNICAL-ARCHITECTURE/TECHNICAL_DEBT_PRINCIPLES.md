# FEMC Technical Debt Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Debt Is a Trade-off

Technical debt may be acceptable when deliberately chosen and understood.

## 2. Hidden Debt Is Dangerous

Undocumented shortcuts become institutional risk.

## 3. Protect Core Domain

Technical debt must not compromise canonical family meaning or integrity.

## 4. Prioritize

Address debt that threatens:

- security;
- privacy;
- data integrity;
- reliability;
- maintainability;
- portability.

## 5. Record Intent

Where a shortcut is intentional, record why it exists and what would justify replacing it.

## 6. Avoid Permanent Workarounds

Temporary solutions should not silently become permanent architecture.

## 7. Principle

Technical debt is manageable when visible, bounded, and intentional.
