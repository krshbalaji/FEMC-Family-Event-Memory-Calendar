# FEMC Technology Selection Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Selection Sequence

```text
Requirement
 ↓
Constraints
 ↓
Options
 ↓
Evaluation
 ↓
Trade-offs
 ↓
Decision
 ↓
Validation
```

## 1. Requirements First

Technology should solve a defined requirement.

## 2. Total Cost

Consider both initial and long-term operating cost.

## 3. Team Capability

A technically elegant system that cannot be safely maintained is not a good choice.

## 4. Ecosystem Health

Consider maturity, support, interoperability, and long-term viability.

## 5. Security

Security implications must be evaluated before adoption.

## 6. Portability

Avoid unnecessary provider-specific coupling.

## 7. Operational Complexity

Every technology adds operational responsibility.

## 8. AI Technologies

Model capability must be evaluated together with:

- privacy;
- reliability;
- cost;
- latency;
- provider dependence;
- failure behavior.

## 9. Principle

The best technology is the one that satisfies the real requirement with the least unjustified long-term risk.
