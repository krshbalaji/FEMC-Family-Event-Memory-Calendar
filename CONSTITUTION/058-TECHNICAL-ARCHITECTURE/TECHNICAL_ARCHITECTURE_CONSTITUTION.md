# FEMC Technical Architecture Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Technical Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define how technical architecture translates logical architecture into implementable technology without allowing technology to redefine FEMC's domain.

## 1. Technology Serves Architecture

Technology choices must satisfy established product and architectural requirements.

## 2. No Premature Commitment

Do not select technologies before understanding the problem they must solve.

## 3. Explicit Trade-offs

Material technology decisions should record:

- alternatives;
- benefits;
- costs;
- risks;
- operational consequences;
- migration implications.

## 4. Production Grade

Technical architecture must consider:

- security;
- reliability;
- observability;
- scalability;
- maintainability;
- cost;
- portability.

## 5. Provider Independence

Critical external dependencies should remain bounded and replaceable where practical.

## 6. Data Integrity

Technical convenience must not compromise canonical family information.

## 7. AI Infrastructure

AI infrastructure should remain modular enough to evolve as models and providers change.

## 8. Principle

Technical architecture is the implementation strategy for FEMC—not a new product constitution.
