# FEMC Data Validation Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Validate at Boundaries

Information should be validated when entering or materially changing canonical domains.

## 2. Context Matters

Validation should reflect the meaning of the field and its relationship to other information.

## 3. Do Not Over-Validate

Rules should not reject legitimate family history merely because it differs from modern assumptions.

## 4. Historical Data

Approximate, incomplete, and old information should remain representable.

## 5. Conflict Detection

Potential conflicts should be surfaced rather than silently resolved when the system lacks authority to decide.

## 6. Imported Data

Imported information should retain its source and confidence until appropriately confirmed.

## 7. AI Assistance

AI may assist normalization or duplicate detection while keeping proposed changes distinguishable from confirmed changes.

## 8. Principle

Validation protects truth; it should not manufacture it.
