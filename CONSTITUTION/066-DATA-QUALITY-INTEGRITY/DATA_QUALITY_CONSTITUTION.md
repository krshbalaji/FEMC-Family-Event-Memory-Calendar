# FEMC Data Quality and Integrity Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Technical Governance Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define data quality as a permanent requirement for trustworthy family memory.

## 1. Quality Is Meaning

Data quality is not merely valid syntax. It includes correctness, completeness, consistency, provenance, and contextual meaning.

## 2. Canonical Protection

Canonical family information must have stronger integrity guarantees than derived representations.

## 3. Completeness

Missing information should remain identifiable rather than being silently fabricated.

## 4. Consistency

Contradictory information should be detectable and resolvable through appropriate family or governance workflows.

## 5. Historical Integrity

Corrections must not unnecessarily erase meaningful historical states.

## 6. Provenance

Material information should retain sufficient origin and status to establish trust.

## 7. AI

AI may identify possible quality issues but must not silently rewrite canonical family information.

## 8. Principle

FEMC should prefer honest incompleteness over confident falsehood.
