# FEMC Memory-to-Legacy Lifecycle

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Lifecycle

```text
EXPERIENCE
   ↓
MEMORY CAPTURE
   ↓
CONTEXT
   ↓
PRESERVATION
   ↓
DISCOVERY
   ↓
STORY
   ↓
FAMILY HISTORY
   ↓
LEGACY
```

## Experience

Something meaningful happens.

## Memory Capture

The family records or describes what matters.

## Context

People, relationships, time, events, media, and other relevant information are connected.

## Preservation

The information is protected and maintained.

## Discovery

Future family members can find relevant material.

## Story

Human or AI-assisted storytelling can turn connected information into understandable narratives.

## Family History

Repeated memories and events may contribute to a broader understanding of family history.

## Legacy

The preserved and contextualized family record becomes available to future generations according to appropriate governance.

## Principle

Legacy is not created at the end of the lifecycle.

It is built through every trustworthy memory decision from the beginning.
