# FEMC Service Level Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define conceptual service-level thinking before technical targets are established.

## 1. Not All Capabilities Are Equal

Critical family access, memory preservation, AI assistance, analytics, and optional enrichment may have different service expectations.

## 2. Protect Core Functions

Service degradation should prioritize:

1. family information integrity;
2. authorized access;
3. essential memory access;
4. core family journeys;
5. optional intelligence and enrichment.

## 3. Availability vs Integrity

A system must not sacrifice data integrity merely to appear available.

## 4. Performance

Performance expectations should be based on user experience and family value rather than arbitrary technical targets.

## 5. AI Availability

AI may be unavailable without making the core family platform unavailable.

## 6. Communication

Service expectations should eventually be expressed in understandable terms.

## 7. Evolution

Service levels should mature with actual product usage and evidence.

## Principle

The service should remain trustworthy even when every capability cannot be perfect at the same time.
