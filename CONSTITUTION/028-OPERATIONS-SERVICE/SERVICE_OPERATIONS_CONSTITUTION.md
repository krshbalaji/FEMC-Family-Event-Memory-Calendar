# FEMC Service Operations Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define the operating principles required to run FEMC as a trustworthy long-term service.

## 1. Production Is a Stewardship Responsibility

Operating FEMC means protecting family trust continuously, not merely keeping servers running.

## 2. Reliability

Critical family capabilities should have clearly defined reliability expectations.

## 3. Change Management

Production changes should be:

- intentional;
- traceable;
- tested appropriately;
- reversible where practical.

## 4. Dependency Management

External dependencies must have known:

- purpose;
- trust boundary;
- failure implications;
- replacement considerations.

## 5. Incident Response

Future operations must be capable of responding to:

- outages;
- security incidents;
- data integrity incidents;
- privacy incidents;
- provider failures;
- AI failures.

## 6. Communication

Material incidents should be communicated honestly and proportionately.

## 7. Recovery

Operational continuity must connect to the resilience and preservation principles of FEMC.

## 8. Cost Stewardship

Infrastructure and AI costs should be managed without weakening core privacy, integrity, or family value.

## 9. Operational Simplicity

Do not create operational machinery that is more complex than the product's justified needs.

## 10. Ten-Year Principle

Operations should support a service that can remain trustworthy for decades, even as teams and technologies change.
