# FEMC Family Portability Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Establish family portability as a permanent product principle so families can preserve and move their information without surrendering meaning or control.

## 1. Portability Is a Family Right

FEMC should be designed so a family can preserve and move its information responsibly.

## 2. Meaning Over Records

A successful export preserves relationships, context, provenance, and historical meaning—not merely rows and files.

## 3. Complete Family Context

Where applicable, portability should consider:

- people;
- relationships;
- events;
- memories;
- media;
- albums;
- stories;
- provenance;
- permissions-related context.

## 4. Human-Readable Preservation

Families should have understandable representations of their history in addition to machine-oriented representations where practical.

## 5. Machine-Readable Portability

Future technical contracts should support migration and interoperability without depending on one implementation.

## 6. Provider Independence

No provider should become the permanent gatekeeper of family history.

## 7. Long-Term Evolution

Portability contracts must evolve while preserving the ability to understand older family records.

## 8. Principle

FEMC should make family information easier to preserve beyond FEMC itself.
