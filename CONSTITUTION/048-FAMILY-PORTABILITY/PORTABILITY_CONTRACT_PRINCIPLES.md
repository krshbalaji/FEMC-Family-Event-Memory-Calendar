# FEMC Portability Contract Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Stable Identifiers

Portable representations should preserve stable identities for important family concepts.

## 2. Relationships

Relationships are part of portable meaning.

## 3. Temporal Context

Dates, ranges, recurrence, and uncertainty should remain understandable.

## 4. Provenance

Source and status should survive portability where appropriate.

## 5. Media Associations

Media should remain connected to the memories, events, and people that give it meaning.

## 6. Versioning

Contracts should be versioned so future systems can interpret earlier exports.

## 7. Validation

Exports and imports should be verifiable.

## 8. No Hidden Vendor Semantics

Portable contracts should not depend on undocumented provider-specific behavior.

## 9. Principle

Portability is successful when another trustworthy system can reconstruct the family's meaning—not merely its storage format.
