# FEMC Council Handoff Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Handoff Structure

A material handoff should communicate:

1. Context
2. Objective
3. Current decision
4. Constraints
5. Assumptions
6. Dependencies
7. Open questions
8. Expected output
9. Owning office

## 1. No Context Loss

A receiving office should not need to reconstruct the entire conversation.

## 2. Preserve Authority

The sending office must distinguish decisions from recommendations.

## 3. Explicit Unknowns

Unknown information should remain visible.

## 4. Repository First

Durable handoff information belongs in the repository where appropriate.

## 5. AI Handoffs

Future AI Council members should receive enough structured context to continue without depending on hidden conversational memory.

## 6. Principle

A good handoff transfers understanding, not merely a task description.
