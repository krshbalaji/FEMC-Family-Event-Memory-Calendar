# FEMC Office Responsibility Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Governance Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Protect responsibility boundaries between FEMC offices so that architecture, product, engineering, governance, and other functions do not silently absorb one another's authority.

## 1. Ownership Must Be Explicit

Every material responsibility should have a clearly identifiable owning office.

## 2. Chief Product Architect Office

Owns:

- vision;
- product constitution;
- domain modeling;
- product architecture;
- AI strategy;
- future roadmap.

It does not own production implementation.

## 3. Engineering

Engineering owns implementation within approved architecture and governance.

## 4. Governance

Governance owns applicable policy, compliance, risk, and control decisions.

## 5. Documentation

Documentation preserves institutional knowledge and library integrity.

## 6. Cross-Office Decisions

When a decision materially affects another office, the owning office should be identified before proceeding.

## 7. No Silent Authority Transfer

Repeated participation in a task does not automatically transfer ownership.

## 8. Principle

Clear boundaries increase collaboration by reducing ambiguity.
