# FEMC Partner and Platform Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Clear Responsibility

Every partner integration should define who is responsible for:

- data;
- security;
- availability;
- processing;
- support;
- failure handling.

## 2. Minimum Necessary Access

Partners should receive only the information required for their authorized purpose.

## 3. User Understanding

Material partner behavior should be understandable to families.

## 4. Data Return

Where appropriate, families should not lose access to information merely because a partner relationship ends.

## 5. Provider Independence

FEMC should avoid dependencies that make a partner irreplaceable without compelling justification.

## 6. Quality

Partners must not lower FEMC's standards for privacy, integrity, accessibility, or trust.

## 7. Governance

Material ecosystem relationships should be documented and reviewable.

## 8. AI Providers

AI providers are subject to the same integration boundary principles as other partners.

## 9. Principle

A partner extends FEMC; the partner does not redefine FEMC.
