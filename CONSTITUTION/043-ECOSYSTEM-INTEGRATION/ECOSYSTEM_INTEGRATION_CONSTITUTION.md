# FEMC Ecosystem Integration Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define FEMC as an extensible family ecosystem while protecting the canonical family model from fragmentation and vendor dependency.

## 1. Ecosystem Around One Family Reality

External products and services may extend FEMC, but they must connect to the canonical family context.

## 2. Integration Is Purpose-Bound

Every significant integration should have a clear purpose and bounded information scope.

## 3. Canonical Ownership

External systems must not silently become authoritative owners of FEMC family truth.

## 4. Permission

Integrations must respect the family access model.

## 5. Replaceability

Where practical, integrations should be replaceable without rebuilding the family record.

## 6. Failure Isolation

An external integration failure should not corrupt core family information.

## 7. AI Ecosystem

Multiple AI providers or models may eventually participate.

The family record must remain independent of any one model or provider.

## 8. Developer Ecosystem

Future third-party capabilities should operate through governed boundaries.

## 9. No Ecosystem Lock-In

FEMC should not deliberately make migration difficult merely to retain ecosystem participation.

## 10. Principle

The FEMC ecosystem should expand family capability without surrendering family sovereignty.
