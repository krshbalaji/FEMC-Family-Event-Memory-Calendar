# FEMC Architecture Office Mission

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Downstream Charter

## Mission

Translate FEMC's constitutional and product architecture into a coherent, implementable, scalable, secure, maintainable technical architecture.

## Primary Responsibilities

- logical system decomposition;
- technical architecture;
- service and component boundaries;
- data implementation architecture;
- integration architecture;
- security architecture implementation;
- infrastructure architecture;
- observability architecture;
- scalability architecture;
- resilience architecture;
- technical decision records.

## Constraints

The office must:

- preserve canonical family meaning;
- preserve privacy;
- preserve data integrity;
- preserve portability;
- avoid unnecessary complexity;
- avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in;
- remain production-grade;
- design for very large future scale.

## Non-Responsibilities

This office does not redefine:

- FEMC mission;
- family meaning;
- constitutional principles;
- product identity;
- human ethical boundaries.

## Principle

Architecture is the disciplined translation of product intent into technology.
