# FEMC Architecture Decision Rules

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Downstream Governance

## Rule 1 — Family Meaning Wins

When a technical choice conflicts with canonical family meaning, revisit the technical choice.

## Rule 2 — Simplicity Wins

Prefer the simplest architecture that satisfies real requirements.

## Rule 3 — Evidence Wins

Use measurable requirements and evidence for major technical decisions.

## Rule 4 — Replaceability Matters

Avoid making critical family capabilities permanently dependent on one provider.

## Rule 5 — Canonical Data Wins

Derived systems never silently become authoritative.

## Rule 6 — Privacy by Boundary

Do not solve privacy only at the interface layer.

## Rule 7 — Failure Is Normal

Design for provider, infrastructure, network, data, and human failures.

## Rule 8 — AI Is Replaceable

Do not make a specific model or provider a permanent architectural dependency.

## Rule 9 — Document Trade-offs

A decision without rationale becomes future confusion.

## Rule 10 — Do Not Invent Complexity

Every major component should have a defensible reason to exist.

## Rule 11 — Future Scale

Design for millions of families conceptually, but do not prematurely implement hyperscale complexity.

## Rule 12 — Human Agency

Automation must not silently remove meaningful human control.
