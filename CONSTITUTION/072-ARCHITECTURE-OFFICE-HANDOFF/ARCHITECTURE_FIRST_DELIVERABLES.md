**Version:** 1.0.0

2. `DOMAIN_TO_SYSTEM_MAP.md`
3. `LOGICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`
4. `SYSTEM_BOUNDARY_MODEL.md`

6. `SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md`
7. `INTEGRATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`
8. `AI_ARCHITECTURE.md`
9. `RESILIENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md`
10. `SCALABILITY_ARCHITECTURE.md`
11. `OBSERVABILITY_ARCHITECTURE.md`
12. Architecture Decision Records
13. Architecture validation report
14. Engineering handoff package

## Important

These are downstream architecture deliverables.

The Chief Product Architect office should not pre-write technical implementation decisions merely to increase documentation volume.

## Completion Test

The architecture phase is ready for engineering when:

- responsibilities are clear;
- boundaries are explicit;
- canonical data ownership is clear;
- trust boundaries are defined;
- major failure modes are understood;
- technology choices are justified;
- material trade-offs are recorded;
- the resulting design remains faithful to FEMC's constitutional meaning.
