# FEMC Interface Evolution Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Context First

An interface should expose the family context needed for the current task.

## 2. Progressive Disclosure

Do not expose the entire complexity of FEMC when only a small part is needed.

## 3. Consistent Meaning

The same family concept should retain the same meaning across interfaces.

## 4. Cross-Interface Continuity

A user should be able to begin a journey through one interface and continue through another where technically and contextually appropriate.

## 5. Identity

Future interfaces must establish sufficient confidence about who is interacting before exposing protected information.

## 6. Privacy

New interfaces must not weaken privacy merely because they make interaction easier.

## 7. Failure

If an advanced interface fails, the underlying family record must remain safe.

## 8. AI

AI should translate intent into useful actions while preserving human authorization.

## 9. Avoid Novelty for Novelty's Sake

New interaction technology should solve a real family problem.

## 10. Principle

The interface may change.

The family's relationship with its history should remain continuous.
