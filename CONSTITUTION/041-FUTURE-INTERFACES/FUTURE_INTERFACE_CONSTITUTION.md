# FEMC Future Interface Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Ensure FEMC's family model can survive changes in how humans interact with technology.

## 1. Interface Is Replaceable

The canonical family model must not depend on today's interface.

## 2. Possible Future Interfaces

FEMC may eventually support:

- web;
- mobile;
- voice;
- conversational AI;
- wearable devices;
- ambient interfaces;
- future interaction technologies.

No specific future technology is mandated.

## 3. One Family Reality

Different interfaces must connect to the same underlying family context.

## 4. Voice

Voice interactions must respect identity, authorization, privacy, and uncertainty.

## 5. Ambient Experiences

Ambient technology must receive especially careful privacy consideration because interaction may occur without a conventional screen.

## 6. AI Interfaces

Conversational AI must not become a separate family database.

It should operate over authorized canonical family context.

## 7. Continuity

Changing interfaces should not require families to recreate their history.

## 8. Accessibility

Future interfaces should expand participation rather than create new barriers.

## 9. Principle

FEMC should be interface-agnostic at the constitutional level and experience-aware at the product level.
