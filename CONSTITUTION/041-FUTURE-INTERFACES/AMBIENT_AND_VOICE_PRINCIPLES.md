# FEMC Ambient and Voice Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Establish additional safeguards for future voice and ambient family experiences.

## 1. Presence Is Not Authorization

Being physically near a device does not automatically establish permission to access family information.

## 2. Identity Confidence

Sensitive actions require appropriate confidence that the correct person is interacting.

## 3. Context Awareness

Ambient systems must avoid exposing private family information in inappropriate environments.

## 4. Listening Boundaries

Future always-available or voice-enabled experiences require explicit privacy architecture.

## 5. Human Confirmation

Material actions should require appropriate confirmation.

## 6. Accidental Activation

The system should account for accidental activation and unintended listeners.

## 7. AI Transparency

Users should understand when an AI system is generating or interpreting information.

## 8. Family Safety

Voice and ambient experiences must not create easy pathways for unauthorized disclosure.

## 9. Graceful Failure

When identity or context is uncertain, the system should prefer privacy-preserving behavior.

## 10. Principle

Convenience must never make a private family conversation public.
