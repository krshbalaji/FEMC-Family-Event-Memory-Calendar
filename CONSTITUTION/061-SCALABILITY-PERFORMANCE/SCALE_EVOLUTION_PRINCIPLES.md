# FEMC Scale Evolution Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Evidence Before Complexity

Introduce scaling architecture in response to measured needs.

## 2. Capacity Planning

Future operations should understand growth in:

- families;
- people;
- events;
- memories;
- media;
- searches;
- AI workloads.

## 3. Hotspots

Identify workloads that scale differently from the rest of the platform.

## 4. Partitioning

Where scale eventually requires stronger isolation or distribution, preserve canonical family semantics.

## 5. Global Growth

International expansion may introduce regional, latency, compliance, and operational requirements.

## 6. Cost Curve

Architectural scaling should consider long-term unit economics.

## 7. Principle

Scale the platform without scaling away its simplicity for families.
