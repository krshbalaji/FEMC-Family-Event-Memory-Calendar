# FEMC Office Responsibility Matrix

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define clear ownership boundaries for specialized FEMC offices.

| Office | Primary Responsibility | Explicit Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Chief Product Architect | Vision, Constitution, Product Architecture, Domain Model, AI Strategy, Roadmap | No production code |
| Product Office | Product requirements, user outcomes, feature definition | Does not redefine constitutional architecture |
| Domain Office | Domain language, entities, relationships, business rules | Does not prescribe infrastructure |
| Architecture Office | Technical architecture and system boundaries | Does not redefine product mission |
| AI Office | AI capability design and AI implementation strategy | Does not own family truth |
| Privacy & Trust Office | Privacy, permissions, trust principles | Does not own product roadmap |
| Security Office | Security architecture and controls | Does not redefine business domain |
| Data Office | Data architecture, lifecycle, integrity, migration | Does not redefine product meaning |
| UX Office | Experience, interaction, accessibility | Does not redefine domain truth |
| Engineering Office | Production implementation | Does not alter constitutional requirements silently |
| Testing & Quality Office | Verification, quality, resilience | Does not own product decisions |
| Infrastructure / Operations Office | Deployment, reliability, observability, operations | Does not own domain semantics |
| Documentation Office | Documentation quality, structure, discoverability | Does not alter meaning without owner approval |
| Governance Office | Decision process, compliance coordination, change control | Does not replace domain owners |

## Shared Responsibility

Offices may collaborate.

Collaboration does not transfer ownership.

## Boundary Rule

If a document materially changes another office's responsibility, that office must be consulted before the change becomes canonical.
