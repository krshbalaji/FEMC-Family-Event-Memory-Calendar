# FEMC Council Handoff Protocol

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Provide a consistent method for moving work between FEMC offices.

## Handoff Requirements

A handoff should identify:

1. Source office
2. Receiving office
3. Objective
4. Current decision or question
5. Relevant constitutional documents
6. Known constraints
7. Dependencies
8. Open questions
9. Expected deliverable
10. Acceptance criteria where appropriate

## No Silent Scope Transfer

An office must not quietly assume another office's responsibilities because the work appears related.

## Example

The Chief Product Architect may define:

> "FEMC requires privacy-aware family memory sharing."

The Privacy & Trust Office may define the trust principles and policy implications.

The Architecture Office may define technical boundaries.

The Engineering Office may implement the approved architecture.

Each retains its responsibility.

## Handoff Completion

A handoff is complete when the receiving office has enough documented context to proceed without reconstructing the entire project from conversation history.

## Institutional Memory

Important handoffs should be represented in repository documentation or decision records where they affect future work.
