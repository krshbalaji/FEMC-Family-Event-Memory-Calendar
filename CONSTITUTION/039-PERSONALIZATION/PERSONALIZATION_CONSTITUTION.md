# FEMC Personalization Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define personalization as a means of making FEMC more useful to a family without compromising privacy, autonomy, or human relationships.

## 1. Personalization Must Serve the Family

Personalization should improve meaningful family experiences, not maximize engagement.

## 2. Context Before Prediction

Where possible, personalization should rely on authorized family context rather than speculative assumptions.

## 3. User Control

People should have meaningful control over important personalization behavior.

## 4. No Hidden Profiling

FEMC should not create unnecessary personal profiles merely because technology makes it possible.

## 5. Family Context and Individual Context

Information relevant to one person must not automatically become visible or applicable to every family member.

## 6. AI Personalization

AI may adapt language, presentation, or assistance to authorized preferences.

It must not invent personal history.

## 7. Emotional Boundaries

Personalization must not exploit grief, loneliness, fear, family conflict, or other vulnerability to increase engagement.

## 8. Explainability

Material personalization should be understandable where it affects meaningful decisions or recommendations.

## 9. Portability

Personalization should not make a family permanently dependent on one implementation.

## 10. Principle

The best personalization should feel like useful context—not surveillance.
