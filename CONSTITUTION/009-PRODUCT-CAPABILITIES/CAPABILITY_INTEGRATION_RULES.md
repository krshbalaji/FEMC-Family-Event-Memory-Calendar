# FEMC Capability Integration Rules

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Rule 1 — Reuse Canonical Concepts

Capabilities must reuse the canonical domain model.

## Rule 2 — No Parallel Truth

A capability must not create a competing source of truth for people, relationships, events, memories, or other core concepts.

## Rule 3 — Context Must Flow

Where meaningful, capabilities should expose relevant context from connected domains.

## Rule 4 — Context Must Not Become Access

The existence of a relationship between two concepts does not automatically grant unrestricted permission to access both.

## Rule 5 — Derived Information Is Identified

Search indexes, AI summaries, recommendations, classifications, and other derived representations must remain distinguishable from canonical family facts.

## Rule 6 — Privacy Follows the Data

When information moves between capabilities, applicable permissions and privacy boundaries must remain intact.

## Rule 7 — Historical Meaning Is Preserved

A new capability must not reinterpret historical family information without appropriate evidence and governance.

## Rule 8 — User Experience May Differ

Different interfaces may present the same underlying family context differently.

Different presentation is not different truth.

## Rule 9 — External Integrations Are Adapters

External services may enrich a capability but must not redefine FEMC's canonical meaning.

## Rule 10 — Future Compatibility

Capabilities should expose stable conceptual contracts so future experiences can use them without redesigning the underlying family model.
