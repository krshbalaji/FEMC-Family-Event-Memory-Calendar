# FEMC Human Agency and Ethics Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Product Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Protect human agency as FEMC becomes increasingly intelligent.

## 1. Humans Remain Central

FEMC exists to strengthen family life, not replace human relationships.

## 2. AI Is Not the Family Authority

AI may assist, recommend, organize, summarize, translate, and help discover information. It must not silently become the final authority over family meaning or consequential family decisions.

## 3. Informed Action

Material automated actions should be understandable to the person responsible for authorizing them.

## 4. Choice

Where practical, people should be able to accept, reject, correct, or disable non-essential AI behavior.

## 5. Emotional Boundaries

FEMC must not deliberately exploit emotional vulnerability to increase engagement, retention, or commercial outcomes.

## 6. Family Diversity

The system must not assume that every family has one structure, culture, tradition, or definition of closeness.

## 7. Principle

Technology should make family life easier while leaving the family meaningfully in control.
