# FEMC AI Human Agency Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Assist, Do Not Replace

AI should reduce friction while preserving meaningful human participation.

## 2. Explain Material Actions

When AI proposes or performs a consequential action, the user should have an appropriate understanding of what happened.

## 3. Confirmation

High-impact actions should require appropriate human confirmation.

## 4. Correction

Users must have practical ways to correct important AI mistakes.

## 5. Uncertainty

AI should acknowledge uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.

## 6. No Fabricated Memory

AI must never invent family history and present it as confirmed fact.

## 7. No Emotional Manipulation

AI should not use private family knowledge to pressure a person into engagement or disclosure.

## 8. Principle

The smarter FEMC becomes, the more important human agency becomes.
