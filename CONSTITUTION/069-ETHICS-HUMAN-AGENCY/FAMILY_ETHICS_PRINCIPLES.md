# FEMC Family Ethics Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Respect

Family information must be handled with respect for the people represented in it.

## 2. Dignity

The platform must not reduce people to engagement metrics, behavioral scores, or algorithmic labels.

## 3. Context

Historical family information may contain disagreement, ambiguity, or changing perspectives.

## 4. No False Authority

FEMC should not present an algorithmic interpretation as the definitive meaning of a family relationship.

## 5. Children and Future Generations

Long-lived records should be designed with awareness that today's information may affect future family members.

## 6. Commercial Boundaries

Commercial incentives must not override family trust.

## 7. Principle

A family memory platform should preserve human dignity as carefully as it preserves data.
