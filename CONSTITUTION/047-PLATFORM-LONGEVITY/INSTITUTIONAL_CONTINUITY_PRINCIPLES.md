# FEMC Institutional Continuity Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Knowledge Must Survive People

No critical architectural knowledge should exist only in one person's memory.

## 2. Constitutional Record

Major principles belong in the constitutional library.

## 3. Decision Record

Important architectural decisions should preserve rationale and consequences.

## 4. Office Responsibility

Future teams and AI Council members should be able to identify which office owns a decision.

## 5. Succession

The project should support transitions between:

- teams;
- architects;
- maintainers;
- AI systems;
- technology generations.

## 6. Repository Orientation

A new contributor should have a defined path from mission to assigned responsibility.

## 7. Historical Continuity

Superseded decisions should remain understandable rather than disappearing without explanation.

## 8. AI Continuity

Future AI systems should be able to reconstruct their operating context from the repository.

## 9. Principle

FEMC's greatest long-term asset is not its codebase.

It is the continuity of its knowledge, architecture, and family mission.
