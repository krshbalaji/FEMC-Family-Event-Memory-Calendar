# FEMC Maintainability Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Understandable Architecture

Complexity must have a reason.

## 2. Explicit Boundaries

Responsibilities between domains and offices should remain clear.

## 3. Documentation

Material behavior should be documented.

## 4. Testability

Important behavior should be verifiable.

## 5. Replaceability

Components with high change risk should have clear boundaries where practical.

## 6. Operational Simplicity

Avoid creating systems that future teams cannot reasonably operate.

## 7. Dependency Awareness

Critical external dependencies should be documented.

## 8. Migration Readiness

Maintainability includes the ability to change technology safely.

## 9. Principle

A system is maintainable when a competent future team can understand it without needing its original creators.
