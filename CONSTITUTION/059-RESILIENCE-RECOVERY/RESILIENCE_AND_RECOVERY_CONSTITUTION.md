# FEMC Resilience and Recovery Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Technical Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define resilience and recovery as essential protections for a platform entrusted with long-lived family history.

## 1. Resilience Protects Continuity

FEMC must be designed to continue or recover important family capabilities after failures.

## 2. Integrity Before Availability

Data integrity must not be sacrificed merely to keep a system appearing available.

## 3. Failure Is Expected

Architecture should assume that:

- components fail;
- providers fail;
- networks fail;
- deployments fail;
- data can become corrupted;
- humans make mistakes.

## 4. Recovery Objectives

Future technical architecture should define appropriate recovery expectations for different classes of capability.

## 5. Backups

Backups must be treated as a recovery mechanism, not as proof that recovery is possible.

## 6. Restoration

Recovery procedures must be verifiable.

## 7. AI Independence

Loss of an AI provider must not make canonical family information inaccessible.

## 8. Principle

A resilient FEMC can lose technology without losing the family story.
