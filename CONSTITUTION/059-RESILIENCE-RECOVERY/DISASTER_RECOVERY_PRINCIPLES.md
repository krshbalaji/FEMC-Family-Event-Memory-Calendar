# FEMC Disaster Recovery Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Identify Critical Capabilities

Not every capability requires identical recovery priority.

## 2. Protect the Core

Recovery should prioritize:

1. family data integrity;
2. authorized access;
3. core memory access;
4. essential family journeys;
5. optional intelligence and enrichment.

## 3. Dependency Failure

Recovery planning should include failure of major external providers.

## 4. Regional Failure

Where justified by scale and risk, architecture should consider infrastructure or regional failure.

## 5. Human Error

Recovery must account for operational mistakes, not only infrastructure failures.

## 6. Communication

Material incidents should have clear communication and escalation processes.

## 7. Exercises

Recovery procedures should be tested periodically according to operational risk.

## 8. Principle

Disaster recovery is successful when families regain trustworthy access to their history, not merely when servers restart.
