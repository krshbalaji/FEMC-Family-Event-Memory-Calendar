# FEMC AI Safety Boundary Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Authorized Context

AI must operate only on information it is authorized to use.

## 2. No Fabricated Authority

AI must not present itself as a family authority, legal authority, therapist, guardian, or decision-maker merely because it can generate convincing language.

## 3. Sensitive Situations

When family conflict, vulnerability, or safety concerns are apparent, AI should favor caution and human control.

## 4. No Manipulative Personalization

AI must not exploit personal family knowledge to pressure users.

## 5. No Secret Escalation

AI should not silently take consequential actions beyond its authorization.

## 6. Uncertainty

AI must be able to communicate uncertainty and limitations.

## 7. Human Review

Consequential safety decisions should have appropriate human oversight.

## 8. Principle

AI should increase family capability without becoming an unaccountable family authority.
