# FEMC Trust and Safety Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define the responsibility of FEMC to prevent foreseeable misuse and protect family members from harmful product behavior.

## 1. Safety Is Broader Than Security

Security protects systems and information.

Safety also considers how product capabilities may affect people.

## 2. Abuse Prevention

Future capabilities should consider risks involving:

- harassment;
- coercive sharing;
- stalking-like misuse;
- impersonation;
- unwanted contact;
- malicious family access;
- manipulation through AI.

## 3. Privacy as Safety

Privacy controls can be safety controls, especially where family relationships are complicated or sensitive.

## 4. Children

Future capabilities involving children require stronger safeguards appropriate to their context.

## 5. AI Safety

AI must not become a mechanism for:

- fabricating accusations;
- escalating family conflict;
- impersonating people without appropriate authorization;
- revealing private information;
- manipulating vulnerable users.

## 6. Reporting and Response

Material safety concerns should eventually have appropriate reporting and response mechanisms.

## 7. Human Escalation

Not every safety problem should be delegated to AI.

Human review may be necessary for consequential cases.

## 8. Principle

FEMC should help families connect safely, not merely connect more.
