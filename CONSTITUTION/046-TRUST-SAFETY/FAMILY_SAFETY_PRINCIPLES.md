# FEMC Family Safety Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Safety by Context

A capability that is safe in one family situation may be unsafe in another.

## 2. Control

People should have meaningful ways to control:

- access;
- sharing;
- communication;
- notifications;
- AI assistance.

## 3. Boundaries

A family relationship does not automatically eliminate personal boundaries.

## 4. No Forced Exposure

FEMC should not require unnecessary disclosure of private information merely to participate in family life.

## 5. Abuse-Aware Design

Future design should consider how ordinary features could be misused.

## 6. Recovery

Where misuse occurs, the platform should support appropriate corrective and recovery mechanisms.

## 7. AI

AI should prefer de-escalation, uncertainty, and human control in sensitive situations.

## 8. Principle

Family connection is valuable only when people remain safe and respected within it.
