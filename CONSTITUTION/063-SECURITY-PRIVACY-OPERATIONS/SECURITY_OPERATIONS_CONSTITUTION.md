# FEMC Security Operations Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Technical Governance Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define the operational discipline required to keep FEMC secure after architecture and implementation begin.

## 1. Security Is Continuous

Security does not end when a system is deployed.

## 2. Detect

Material security events should be detectable through appropriate operational signals.

## 3. Respond

Security incidents require defined ownership, containment, investigation, recovery, and learning.

## 4. Minimize Exposure

Operational access to family information should remain limited and justified.

## 5. Vulnerability Management

Known vulnerabilities should be assessed according to actual risk to FEMC and its families.

## 6. Dependency Risk

External dependencies must be considered part of the security boundary.

## 7. Secrets

Credentials, keys, and other sensitive operational material require appropriate lifecycle management.

## 8. Privacy Incident

A privacy incident may be a security incident, but privacy impact must also be assessed independently.

## 9. Principle

Security operations should continuously protect the trust established by the product constitution.
