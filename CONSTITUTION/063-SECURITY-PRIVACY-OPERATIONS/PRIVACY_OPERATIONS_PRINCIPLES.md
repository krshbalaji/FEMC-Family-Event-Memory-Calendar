# FEMC Privacy Operations Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Privacy Is Operational

Privacy commitments must be reflected in daily system operation.

## 2. Access Review

Privileged and sensitive access should be reviewable according to risk.

## 3. Data Exposure

Operational workflows should minimize unnecessary exposure of family information.

## 4. Retention

Operational copies, logs, caches, and derived data should have justified retention.

## 5. Incident Response

Privacy-impacting incidents require appropriate assessment and response.

## 6. Third Parties

External processors and providers remain subject to FEMC's privacy boundaries.

## 7. AI

AI processing must remain within authorized privacy boundaries.

## 8. Principle

A privacy promise that cannot survive ordinary operations is not a real privacy promise.
