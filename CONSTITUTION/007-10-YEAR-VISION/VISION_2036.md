# FEMC Vision 2036

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Constitution  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Vision

By 2036, FEMC should be recognized not as a calendar product, but as a trusted digital home for family memory, relationships, celebrations, communication, and intergenerational legacy.

Its enduring value should be that a family can build a meaningful record of its life and continue that record across changing technologies and generations.

## The 2036 Test

A successful FEMC of 2036 should allow a family to answer:

- Who are we?
- How are we connected?
- What have we shared?
- What have we celebrated?
- What memories matter?
- What happened across generations?
- What should we remember?
- What should future generations know?

## Long-Term Product Identity

FEMC should become a living family context in which:

**People create memories.  
Relationships give memories meaning.  
Events organize shared time.  
Media preserves evidence.  
Stories preserve meaning.  
AI helps families discover and understand.  
Legacy carries the story forward.**

## Human Outcome

The platform should help families spend less effort managing family information and more time experiencing family life.

Technology should reduce friction without reducing humanity.

## 2036 Product Characteristics

FEMC should be:

- trusted;
- private;
- interoperable;
- family-owned in spirit;
- multi-generational;
- intelligent;
- accessible;
- emotionally meaningful without being manipulative;
- technically evolvable;
- resilient to vendor and technology changes.

## What FEMC Must Not Become

Even in 2036, FEMC should not become:

- an advertising-first family surveillance system;
- an AI-generated replacement for family history;
- a locked data vault;
- a social network optimized for engagement at any cost;
- a complicated enterprise system that families cannot understand.

## Enduring Measure

The ultimate measure is whether a family would trust FEMC with something precious:

**its story.**
