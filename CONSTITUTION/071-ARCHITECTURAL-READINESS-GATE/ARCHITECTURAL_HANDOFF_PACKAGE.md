# FEMC Architectural Handoff Package

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Handoff Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Handoff Objective

Transfer a coherent product-architecture foundation to downstream offices without transferring hidden assumptions.

## Handoff Contains

1. Mission and product constitution
2. Canonical domain principles
3. Logical architecture principles
4. Data architecture principles
5. Security and privacy boundaries
6. AI strategy and governance
7. Resilience and scalability principles
8. Quality and release gates
9. Internationalization and accessibility principles
10. Human agency and ethical boundaries
11. Memory and legacy principles
12. Office responsibility boundaries

## Receiving Office Responsibilities

The receiving architecture or engineering office must:

- preserve constitutional intent;
- identify implementation-specific decisions;
- record technical trade-offs;
- surface unresolved assumptions;
- avoid silently changing product meaning.

## Principle

A handoff is successful when the receiving team can continue the work without needing the original conversation to reconstruct FEMC's purpose.
