# FEMC Chief Product Architecture Closure

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Milestone  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Statement

The initial Chief Product Architect constitutional and product-architecture foundation is considered sufficiently developed for controlled handoff when the readiness gate is satisfied.

## What Is Closed

The office has established the enduring product-level principles for:

- mission;
- family-centered product identity;
- canonical domain meaning;
- relationships;
- events and time;
- memories and legacy;
- privacy and consent;
- security and safety;
- AI strategy;
- personalization;
- accessibility;
- internationalization;
- portability;
- ecosystem independence;
- resilience;
- scalability;
- quality;
- operations;
- human agency;
- ethical boundaries.

## What Is Not Closed

The following remain implementation responsibilities:

- production code;
- detailed technology selection;
- infrastructure implementation;
- database implementation;
- deployment implementation;
- operational runbooks;
- detailed API implementation;
- UI implementation.

## Amendment Principle

Future discoveries may require constitutional amendment. Such amendments must be explicit, justified, and traceable.

## Final Principle

The Chief Product Architect office does not exist to build every part of FEMC.

It exists to ensure that every part of FEMC has a coherent reason to exist.
