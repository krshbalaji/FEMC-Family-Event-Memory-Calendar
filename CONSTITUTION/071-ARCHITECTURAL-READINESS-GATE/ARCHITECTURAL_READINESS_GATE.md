# FEMC Architectural Readiness Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Chief Product Architect Gate  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Establish the final product-architecture gate before detailed technical implementation proceeds.

## Required Conditions

- [ ] Mission is stable.
- [ ] Product identity is stable.
- [ ] Canonical family domain is defined.
- [ ] Relationship and event semantics are defined.
- [ ] Memory and legacy principles are defined.
- [ ] Consent and access boundaries are defined.
- [ ] Security and privacy architecture is defined.
- [ ] AI boundaries and provenance are defined.
- [ ] Data lifecycle and integrity principles are defined.
- [ ] Portability and vendor independence are defined.
- [ ] Resilience and recovery are defined.
- [ ] Scalability principles are defined.
- [ ] Quality and release principles are defined.
- [ ] Internationalization and accessibility are addressed.
- [ ] Human agency and ethical boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Office responsibilities are clear.
- [ ] Future evolution is governed.

## Gate Decision

If these conditions are satisfied at the appropriate level of abstraction, detailed architecture may proceed.

## Important

This gate does not authorize production coding by the Chief Product Architect office.

It authorizes the transition from product-architecture definition into the appropriate downstream architecture and engineering responsibilities.
