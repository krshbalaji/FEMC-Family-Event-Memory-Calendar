# FEMC Product Measurement Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define how FEMC should measure product success without reducing family value to engagement metrics.

## Primary Measure

The primary question is:

> Is FEMC helping families remember, connect, celebrate, communicate, understand, and preserve their story?

## Outcome Categories

### Family Value

Measure evidence of meaningful outcomes such as:

- successful memory discovery;
- successful family coordination;
- meaningful celebration support;
- preservation activity;
- useful family-history discovery.

### Trust

Consider:

- privacy confidence;
- successful permission behavior;
- security incidents;
- data integrity;
- recovery success;
- export success.

### Product Health

Consider:

- reliability;
- performance;
- accessibility;
- usability;
- support burden;
- operational sustainability.

### Intelligence Quality

Consider:

- usefulness;
- grounding;
- correction rate;
- appropriate abstention;
- user confirmation;
- AI-related trust.

## Anti-Metrics

FEMC should be cautious about treating these as primary success measures:

- screen time;
- notification volume;
- compulsive return frequency;
- infinite scrolling;
- engagement at any cost.

A family using FEMC less because it successfully found what it needed may represent success.

## Measurement Privacy

Measurement systems must not collect more family information than necessary.

Analytics must respect the same trust principles as the product.

## Measurement Evolution

Metrics may change as FEMC matures.

The constitutional mission must remain more stable than the metrics used to evaluate it.
