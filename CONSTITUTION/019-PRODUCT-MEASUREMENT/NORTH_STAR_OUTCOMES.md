# FEMC North-Star Outcomes

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## North Star

**Meaningful Family Connection and Memory Preservation**

FEMC should create measurable evidence that family information becomes easier to preserve, discover, understand, share, and carry forward.

## Outcome 1 — Remember

Families can successfully rediscover meaningful moments.

## Outcome 2 — Connect

Relationships help people understand family context.

## Outcome 3 — Celebrate

FEMC helps families prepare for and remember meaningful occasions.

## Outcome 4 — Preserve

Family history remains protected, organized, and portable.

## Outcome 5 — Understand

People can discover context across time and generations.

## Outcome 6 — Trust

Families feel confident that their information is handled responsibly.

## Outcome 7 — Assist

AI reduces effort without replacing human agency or family truth.

## Outcome 8 — Continue

The family record remains useful as people, devices, technologies, and providers change.

## Measurement Rule

No single metric represents the North Star.

FEMC should use a balanced set of outcome, trust, quality, and operational indicators.
