# FEMC AI Strategy Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define the role, boundaries, principles, and long-term direction of artificial intelligence within FEMC.

## Fundamental Position

AI is a capability of FEMC, not the identity of FEMC.

FEMC exists to strengthen family connection and preserve family memory.

AI exists to make that mission easier, safer, richer, and more useful.

## AI Must Serve the Family

AI may:

- organize information;
- discover connections;
- assist with storytelling;
- help prepare celebrations;
- summarize family history;
- improve search;
- assist communication;
- surface relevant memories;
- reduce repetitive work;
- help preserve context;
- support accessibility and multilingual interaction.

AI must not become the authority over family relationships or historical truth.

## Family Truth

FEMC distinguishes between:

1. User-confirmed information.
2. Imported information.
3. System-derived information.
4. AI-generated suggestions.
5. Unknown or uncertain information.

AI must never silently convert an inference into confirmed family history.

## Contextual Intelligence

AI should receive the smallest appropriate authorized context for a task.

A request concerning one event should not automatically require unrestricted access to the entire family record.

Context must be:

- relevant;
- authorized;
- explainable;
- purpose-bound;
- revocable where appropriate.

## Human Agency

AI should assist rather than manipulate.

The platform must avoid:

- deceptive anthropomorphism;
- pressure to engage;
- fabricated memories;
- artificial emotional dependency;
- hidden persuasion;
- unexplained consequential decisions.

## Privacy

Family data is deeply personal.

AI architecture must therefore support:

- data minimization;
- permission-aware context;
- secure processing;
- provider independence;
- explicit data-use boundaries;
- controlled retention;
- export and deletion rights;
- clear AI activity where appropriate.

## Provider Independence

AI providers are replaceable implementation dependencies.

The FEMC conceptual model must not depend on one model vendor, model family, API, or proprietary representation.

## AI Memory

AI-generated context must not become an uncontrolled second family database.

Persistent family knowledge belongs to the canonical FEMC domain model.

AI may create:

- temporary reasoning context;
- derived indexes;
- embeddings or other representations;
- suggestions;
- summaries;

but these must remain governed by the underlying canonical family record.

## Explainability

Where AI materially affects a user's understanding or action, FEMC should make the basis understandable.

The objective is not to expose internal model reasoning.

The objective is to distinguish:

- fact;
- source;
- inference;
- recommendation;
- uncertainty.

## AI Evolution

FEMC should be capable of adopting better AI technologies without requiring reconstruction of the family domain.

The architecture should allow models to improve while family data remains durable.

## Ten-Year Vision

Over the next decade, AI may become a natural family intelligence layer capable of understanding years of authorized family context.

Even then:

**AI remains the assistant.  
The family remains the owner of its story.**
