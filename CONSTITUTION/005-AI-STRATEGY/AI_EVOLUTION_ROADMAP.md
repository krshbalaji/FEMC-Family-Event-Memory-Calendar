# FEMC AI Evolution Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Horizon 1 — Assisted FEMC

AI primarily helps users perform tasks.

Examples:

- search;
- captions;
- summaries;
- reminders;
- message drafting;
- organization.

## Horizon 2 — Contextual FEMC

AI understands authorized family context.

Examples:

- relationship-aware discovery;
- memory connections;
- event-memory suggestions;
- personalized family timelines;
- contextual celebration assistance.

## Horizon 3 — Family Memory Intelligence

AI becomes capable of understanding long-term family history while remaining subordinate to canonical family data and permissions.

Examples:

- multi-year memory discovery;
- generational storytelling;
- family-history synthesis;
- preservation assistance;
- meaningful pattern discovery.

## Horizon 4 — Adaptive Family Space

FEMC may become an ambient intelligence layer across family life.

Potential capabilities include:

- proactive but controlled assistance;
- cross-generational context;
- natural voice interaction;
- richer multimodal understanding;
- personalized family experiences.

These capabilities must not be treated as commitments to specific technologies.

## Ten-Year Constraint

Future AI capability must never require surrendering:

- family ownership;
- privacy;
- data portability;
- historical integrity;
- human agency;
- vendor independence.

The AI may become more capable.

The constitutional boundaries do not become weaker.
