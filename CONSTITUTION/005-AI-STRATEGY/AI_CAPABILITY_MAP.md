# FEMC AI Capability Map

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define AI capabilities as product capabilities rather than technology features.

## 1. Memory Intelligence

AI may help:

- find memories;
- group related memories;
- identify recurring themes;
- create timelines;
- connect memories to events;
- assist album creation;
- generate family-story drafts.

## 2. Event Intelligence

AI may help:

- identify important occasions;
- prepare reminders;
- suggest relevant past memories;
- prepare celebration plans;
- detect useful temporal relationships.

## 3. Relationship Intelligence

AI may help users understand existing family relationships and history.

AI must not invent relationships.

## 4. Family Storytelling

AI may transform authorized family material into:

- stories;
- summaries;
- timelines;
- captions;
- celebration messages;
- family-history drafts.

Generated storytelling must preserve the distinction between source material and creative interpretation.

## 5. Search Intelligence

Natural-language search may allow requests such as:

- finding memories involving particular people;
- locating events from a period;
- discovering related photographs;
- finding family milestones.

## 6. Communication Assistance

AI may help draft:

- greetings;
- invitations;
- celebration messages;
- memory captions;
- family updates.

The user remains the communicator.

## 7. Preservation Intelligence

AI may assist with:

- identifying missing context;
- detecting possible duplicates;
- suggesting metadata improvements;
- organizing archives;
- preparing preservation summaries.

AI suggestions must not destructively modify canonical history without appropriate authorization.

## 8. Accessibility Intelligence

AI may support:

- multilingual interaction;
- speech interfaces;
- transcription;
- translation;
- simplified explanations;
- accessibility assistance.

## 9. Family Intelligence

Future generations of FEMC may provide deeper contextual assistance across years of authorized family history.

This capability must remain privacy-aware and user-controlled.

## Capability Rule

AI capabilities must be evaluated by the family outcome they improve, not by model novelty.

A more powerful model is not automatically a better FEMC capability.
