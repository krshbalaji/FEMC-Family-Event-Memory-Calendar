## Source-of-Truth Rules

1. Canonical product decisions belong in version-controlled documentation.
2. Conversation history is not the permanent source of truth.
3. Duplicate definitions must be avoided.
4. Material changes require dependency review.
5. Historical decisions remain discoverable.
6. Implementation documents must not silently contradict constitutional documents.

## Document Status

Use:

- `DRAFT` — under development;
- `REVIEW` — awaiting review;
- `APPROVED` — accepted as canonical;
- `SUPERSEDED` — replaced by a newer document;
- `ARCHIVED` — retained for historical reference.

## Change Principle

The library should grow by adding durable knowledge, not by repeatedly rewriting the same ideas.
