# FEMC Responsible Data Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define governance principles for the responsible lifecycle of family information.

## Data Governance Principles

### Purpose

Information should have a justified product purpose.

### Ownership of Decisions

Every material data policy should have an accountable owner.

### Minimization

Do not collect information merely because it may become useful someday.

### Quality

Important information should have appropriate validation and integrity controls.

### Access

Access should be governed by legitimate purpose and authorization.

### Retention

Retention should reflect product meaning, user control, preservation, and applicable obligations.

### Sharing

External sharing should be explicit and appropriately bounded.

### AI Processing

AI processing must remain subject to the same governance principles.

### Change

Material data policy changes should be documented and reviewed.

## Principle

Responsible governance protects both the family and the long-term integrity of FEMC.
