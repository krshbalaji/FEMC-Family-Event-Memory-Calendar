# FEMC Compliance and Responsible Operation Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define the principle that FEMC must operate responsibly across jurisdictions without allowing compliance requirements to fragment the core family model.

## 1. Responsible Operation

FEMC must identify and respect applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and platform obligations.

## 2. Compliance Does Not Replace Product Principles

Legal compliance is a baseline.

FEMC should continue to uphold stronger privacy, integrity, transparency, and human-agency principles where appropriate.

## 3. Jurisdictional Awareness

Different jurisdictions may impose different requirements concerning:

- privacy;
- children;
- data retention;
- communications;
- accessibility;
- consumer protection;
- cross-border data;
- AI.

The architecture should allow these requirements to be addressed without rewriting the canonical family domain.

## 4. Data Governance

Important data practices should have clear ownership and documented purpose.

## 5. User Rights

Where applicable, product design should support meaningful user control over information and participation.

## 6. Records

Material compliance decisions should become durable institutional documentation.

## 7. Change

Compliance requirements may evolve.

The product architecture must support responsible adaptation without sacrificing historical integrity.

## 8. Principle

FEMC should be globally capable while remaining locally responsible.
