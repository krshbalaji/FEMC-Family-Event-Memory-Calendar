# FEMC BM Traceability Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Closure Artifact

## 1. Every Major Decision Has a Home

Material product and architectural principles should be traceable to an appropriate constitutional or architecture document.

## 2. No Orphan Concepts

A new implementation concept should not silently introduce a new fundamental product meaning.

## 3. Downstream Traceability

Detailed architecture should be able to trace major decisions upward to:

**Requirement → Product Intent → Domain Meaning → Architecture Principle → Technical Decision**

## 4. Conflict Detection

If a technical decision conflicts with a constitutional principle, the conflict must be surfaced rather than silently normalized.

## 5. Evolution

When a principle changes, dependent decisions should be identifiable.

## 6. Principle

Traceability prevents architectural drift without freezing implementation.
