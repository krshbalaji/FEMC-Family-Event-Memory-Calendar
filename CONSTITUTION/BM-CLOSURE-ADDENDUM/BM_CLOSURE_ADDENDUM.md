# FEMC BM Closure Addendum

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Final Chief Product Architect Addendum  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

This addendum does not create another product domain or reopen the FEMC constitution.

It closes the remaining architectural-management gap between the completed BM library and downstream execution.

## Scope

The addendum establishes:

1. constitutional completeness criteria;
2. architecture traceability;
3. office handoff discipline;
4. amendment control;
5. downstream entry criteria.

## Boundary

The Chief Product Architect office remains responsible for product meaning and architectural intent.

It does not assume responsibility for production engineering.

## Final Principle

The BM library is successful when future offices can make implementation decisions without inventing FEMC's fundamental meaning themselves.
