# FEMC Constitutional Amendment Protocol

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Closure Governance

## Purpose

Allow FEMC to evolve without casually destabilizing its constitutional foundation.

## Amendment Triggers

An amendment may be considered when:

- a genuine domain gap is discovered;
- two constitutional principles conflict;
- a major new capability creates a previously unknown architectural responsibility;
- external obligations materially change;
- production evidence demonstrates that a principle is insufficient.

## Required Record

A material amendment should document:

1. problem;
2. evidence;
3. affected principles;
4. proposed change;
5. alternatives considered;
6. impact;
7. migration implications;
8. approval/ownership;
9. superseded wording where applicable.

## Rules

- No silent amendments.
- No amendment merely for implementation convenience.
- No amendment merely to accommodate a vendor.
- Preserve historical rationale.
- Prefer the smallest change that resolves the real problem.

## Principle

The constitution is living, but not casually mutable.
