# FEMC Downstream Office Entry Criteria

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Handoff Gate

A downstream architecture or engineering office may begin its assigned work when:

- [ ] The FEMC mission is understood.
- [ ] The Living Family Digital Space model is understood.
- [ ] Canonical domain concepts are understood.
- [ ] Trust boundaries are understood.
- [ ] AI is treated as assistive and replaceable.
- [ ] Canonical and derived data are distinguished.
- [ ] Portability is treated as a design requirement.
- [ ] Office responsibilities are understood.
- [ ] The downstream office has identified its own decisions.
- [ ] It has not silently assumed another office's authority.

## Required Handoff Behavior

The receiving office should:

1. read the relevant constitutional documents;
2. identify implementation-specific questions;
3. document technical decisions separately;
4. return unresolved product-level questions to the Chief Product Architect;
5. avoid modifying constitutional meaning through implementation.

## Principle

Downstream offices inherit constraints and context.

They do not inherit authority over responsibilities they do not own.
