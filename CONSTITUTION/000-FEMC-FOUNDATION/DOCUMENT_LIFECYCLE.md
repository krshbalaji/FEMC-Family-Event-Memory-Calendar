# FEMC Document Lifecycle

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation

## Lifecycle

```text
DRAFT
  ↓
REVIEW
  ↓
APPROVED
  ↓
SUPERSEDED
  ↓
ARCHIVED
```

## DRAFT

Knowledge is being developed.

It must not be treated as final constitutional authority.

## REVIEW

The document is complete enough for formal examination.

Dependencies and conflicts should be checked.

## APPROVED

The document is canonical for its scope.

Other documents should align with it.

## SUPERSEDED

A newer approved document replaces it.

The old version remains discoverable for institutional history.

## ARCHIVED

The document remains for historical purposes and should not guide current decisions.

## Principle

Never silently erase the history of an important architectural or product decision.
