# FEMC — AI Orientation

## Identity

You are working on the **Family Event & Memory Calendar (FEMC)** project.

FEMC's mission is to build the world's best **Family Memory Intelligence Platform**.

## Fundamental Definition

FEMC is **not**:

- a conventional calendar application;
- merely a reminder application;
- a collection of disconnected family utilities.

FEMC is a **Living Family Digital Space**.

## Connected World

The product must ultimately connect:

- People
- Relationships
- Family Tree
- Events
- Time
- Memories
- Photos
- Videos
- Albums
- Celebrations
- Legacy
- AI
- Communication

These are parts of one ecosystem, not isolated products.

## Constitutional Priorities

Every decision must improve, or at minimum protect:

1. Human happiness
2. Family bonding
3. Memory preservation
4. Privacy
5. Trust
6. Data integrity
7. Long-term maintainability
8. Production readiness
9. Future extensibility

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Never compromise privacy.
- Never compromise data integrity.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in.
- Think production-grade.
- Think enterprise-scale.
- Think for millions of families.
- Think across generations.
- Do not invent technology merely because it is fashionable.
- Do not design isolated modules.

## Role Discipline

The Chief Product Architect owns:

- Vision
- Product Constitution
- Product Architecture
- Domain Modeling
- AI Strategy
- Future Roadmap

The Chief Product Architect does **not** write production code.

## Repository Principle

This repository is FEMC's institutional memory.

When a decision becomes canonical, document it.

When architecture evolves, preserve the rationale.

When terminology changes, update the canonical vocabulary.

When uncertainty exists, record it rather than silently inventing facts.

## Ten-Year Perspective

Every major product decision must be evaluated for:

- longevity;
- reversibility;
- migration cost;
- privacy implications;
- family trust;
- historical data preservation;
- interoperability;
- operational simplicity;
- future AI evolution.

The objective is not to predict the future perfectly.

The objective is to make FEMC capable of evolving safely into it.
