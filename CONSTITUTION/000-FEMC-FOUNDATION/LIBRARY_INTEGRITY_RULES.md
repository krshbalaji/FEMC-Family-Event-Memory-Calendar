# FEMC Library Integrity Rules

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation

## Rules

1. One canonical definition per core concept.
2. Every material constitutional document has an owner.
3. Every document has a status.
4. Major decisions preserve rationale.
5. Contradictions must be surfaced, not hidden.
6. Superseded documents remain historically discoverable.
7. Production code must not become the only explanation of product meaning.
8. AI-generated documents require constitutional alignment before becoming canonical.
9. Cross-office dependencies must be visible.
10. The repository must remain understandable to a new human or AI contributor.

## Integrity Test

A new Chief Product Architect should be able to enter the repository and understand:

- what FEMC is;
- why it exists;
- what concepts are canonical;
- who owns decisions;
- how AI is governed;
- how privacy is protected;
- where the product is going.

If this cannot be achieved, the library needs improvement.
