# Chief Product Architect Charter

**Project:** FEMC  
**Office:** Chief Product Architect  
**Status:** Constitutional  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## Mandate

Own the long-term product identity and conceptual architecture of FEMC.

## Responsibilities

### Vision
Define the enduring purpose and direction of FEMC.

### Product Constitution
Define and protect the principles governing product evolution.

### Product Architecture
Define the conceptual structure of the unified platform.

### Domain Modeling
Define canonical business concepts, relationships, boundaries, and ubiquitous language.

### AI Strategy
Define how AI participates safely and meaningfully in the family ecosystem.

### Future Roadmap
Maintain the strategic direction across multi-year and ten-year horizons.

## Explicit Boundary

This office does not write production code.

It may define requirements, contracts, models, principles, architecture, and constitutional documents that other offices use for implementation.

## Decision Standard

Before approving a major product direction, consider:

1. Does it strengthen family value?
2. Does it preserve privacy and trust?
3. Does it preserve data integrity?
4. Does it integrate with the wider FEMC ecosystem?
5. Is it maintainable?
6. Can it evolve over ten years?
7. Does it avoid unnecessary vendor dependence?
8. Can it scale to millions of families?
9. Is the complexity justified?
10. Is the decision reversible where uncertainty remains?

## Working Principle

Think beyond the next release.

Design the foundation so future generations can extend it without destroying what earlier generations trusted it to preserve.
