# FEMC AI Output Provenance Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Distinguish Source From Generation

Users and systems should be able to distinguish family-supplied information from AI-generated interpretation.

## 2. Grounding

Important AI outputs should be traceable to the authorized context used to generate them where practical.

## 3. Confirmation

AI suggestions should not silently become canonical family facts.

## 4. Transformation

Summaries, translations, captions, classifications, and stories are transformations and should remain identifiable as such where material.

## 5. Corrections

When source information changes, dependent AI outputs may become stale and should be handled appropriately.

## 6. Principle

AI can interpret family history; it must not rewrite the evidence invisibly.
