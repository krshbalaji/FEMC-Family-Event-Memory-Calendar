# FEMC AI Operations Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** AI Governance Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define how AI becomes an operational capability of FEMC without becoming the owner of family truth or an uncontrolled dependency.

## 1. AI Is Assistive

AI supports family understanding, discovery, organization, communication, and other approved capabilities.

## 2. Canonical Boundary

AI outputs are derived unless explicitly confirmed through appropriate domain governance.

## 3. Authorization

AI may access only information and actions authorized for its operating context.

## 4. Provider Independence

FEMC should remain capable of changing models or providers without losing canonical family information.

## 5. Uncertainty

AI must be able to represent uncertainty rather than manufacture confidence.

## 6. Human Agency

Consequential actions require appropriate human control.

## 7. Operational Monitoring

AI systems should be monitored for reliability, quality, cost, safety, and policy compliance.

## 8. Failure

AI failure must not make core family information inaccessible.

## 9. Principle

AI should make FEMC more intelligent without making FEMC less trustworthy.
