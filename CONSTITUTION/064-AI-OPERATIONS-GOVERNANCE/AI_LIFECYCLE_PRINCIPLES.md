# FEMC AI Lifecycle Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Lifecycle

```text
SELECT
 ↓
EVALUATE
 ↓
AUTHORIZE
 ↓
INTEGRATE
 ↓
MONITOR
 ↓
REVIEW
 ↓
REPLACE / RETIRE
```

## 1. Select

Choose AI capability based on a real product requirement.

## 2. Evaluate

Assess quality, privacy, security, cost, latency, reliability, and safety.

## 3. Authorize

Define exactly what information and actions the AI may use.

## 4. Integrate

Keep provider-specific behavior behind appropriate boundaries.

## 5. Monitor

Track operational and quality signals.

## 6. Review

Reassess performance and risk as models and usage change.

## 7. Replace or Retire

AI components must be replaceable without damaging canonical family history.

## 8. Principle

An AI model is a replaceable capability, not a permanent foundation of family truth.
