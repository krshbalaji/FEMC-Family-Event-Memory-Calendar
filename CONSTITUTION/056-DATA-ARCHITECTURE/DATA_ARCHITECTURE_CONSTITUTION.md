# FEMC Data Architecture Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Post-Constitutional Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define the principles for turning the canonical family domain into durable, trustworthy data architecture.

## 1. Data Serves the Domain

Data structures exist to preserve family meaning.

## 2. Canonical Data

Authoritative family information must remain distinguishable from derived or temporary representations.

## 3. Integrity

Data integrity takes precedence over convenience.

## 4. Provenance

Important information should retain sufficient provenance to understand its origin and status.

## 5. Temporal Data

The architecture must support historical change without destroying prior meaning.

## 6. Privacy

Data boundaries must support the consent and access constitution.

## 7. Derived Data

Indexes, caches, analytics, embeddings, AI outputs, and recommendations are derived representations unless explicitly governed otherwise.

## 8. Portability

Data architecture must support long-term migration and understandable export.

## 9. Principle

The data architecture is successful when the family story remains trustworthy even as implementations change.
