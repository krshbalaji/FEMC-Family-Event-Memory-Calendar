# FEMC Data Lifecycle Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Lifecycle

```text
CAPTURE
 ↓
VALIDATE
 ↓
CANONICALIZE
 ↓
USE
 ↓
DERIVE
 ↓
PRESERVE
 ↓
MIGRATE / ARCHIVE
 ↓
RETIRE WHERE APPROPRIATE
```

## 1. Capture

Record information with appropriate context and provenance.

## 2. Validate

Identify errors, ambiguity, and conflicts.

## 3. Canonicalize

Only information appropriate for canonical status should enter the authoritative model.

## 4. Use

Apply information within authorized purposes.

## 5. Derive

Create secondary representations where justified.

## 6. Preserve

Protect information with long-term family value.

## 7. Migrate

Move information without losing meaning.

## 8. Archive or Retire

Apply controlled lifecycle decisions while preserving required historical integrity.

## 9. Principle

Every data lifecycle transition must preserve trust, meaning, and appropriate control.
