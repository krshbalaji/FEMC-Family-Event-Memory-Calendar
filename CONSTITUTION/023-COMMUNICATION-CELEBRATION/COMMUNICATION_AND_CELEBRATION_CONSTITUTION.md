# FEMC Communication and Celebration Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define communication and celebration as family capabilities that connect people, occasions, memories, and relationships.

## 1. Communication Serves Relationships

Communication exists to help family members connect.

It should not become an engagement system optimized for message volume.

## 2. Celebration Is Contextual

Celebrations should connect:

- people;
- relationships;
- events;
- history;
- memories;
- communication.

## 3. Event Before Notification

A notification is a delivery mechanism.

The meaningful domain concept is the event or family context behind it.

## 4. Memory Connection

After a celebration, FEMC should make it natural to preserve meaningful memories created during the occasion.

## 5. User Agency

Users should control important communication actions.

AI may assist drafting, but should not silently communicate on behalf of a person where meaningful human authorization is required.

## 6. Privacy

Communication and celebration features must respect the visibility and permissions of the underlying family context.

## 7. Calm Communication

FEMC should avoid unnecessary notifications and artificial urgency.

## 8. Cultural Flexibility

Celebrations may differ across families and cultures.

The platform should support family-defined occasions and traditions.

## 9. AI Assistance

AI may help:

- draft greetings;
- prepare invitations;
- suggest relevant memories;
- organize celebration information.

AI must not fabricate family facts to make a message appear more personal.

## 10. Constitutional Principle

The goal is not more communication.

The goal is more meaningful connection.
