# FEMC Celebration-to-Memory Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Occasion

A meaningful event creates a family opportunity.

## 2. Preparation

FEMC may connect the event with:

- people;
- relationships;
- previous memories;
- communication;
- planning.

## 3. Celebration

The family experiences the occasion.

FEMC should remain supportive rather than becoming the event itself.

## 4. Capture

Participants may choose to preserve:

- photographs;
- videos;
- stories;
- messages;
- reflections;
- other meaningful information.

## 5. Context

New memories should be connected to the appropriate event and people where authorized.

## 6. Discovery

Future family members should be able to discover the occasion through multiple contextual paths.

## 7. Legacy

Repeated celebrations can become part of family tradition and generational history.

## 8. AI

AI may help identify relevant previous memories or prepare summaries, but must not invent tradition or history.

## 9. Privacy

Not every celebration memory is necessarily shared with everyone.

## 10. Principle

A celebration should not end when the event ends.

Where the family chooses, it can become part of the family's continuing memory.
