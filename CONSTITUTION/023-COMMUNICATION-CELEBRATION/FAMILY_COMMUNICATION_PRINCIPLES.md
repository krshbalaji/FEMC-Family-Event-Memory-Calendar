# FEMC Family Communication Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Intent Before Delivery

Understand the intended family communication before choosing how it is delivered.

## 2. Respect Attention

Family members should not be overwhelmed by notifications.

## 3. Contextual Relevance

Communication should contain relevant context where authorized.

## 4. Permission First

The ability to discover information does not automatically mean the ability to communicate it to others.

## 5. Human Voice

AI-assisted communication should remain recognizably under the user's control.

## 6. No Fabricated Personalization

AI should not invent memories, relationships, or emotional details to make messages appear authentic.

## 7. Multilingual Future

Communication should eventually support families communicating across languages without changing canonical family meaning.

## 8. Accessibility

Communication should support appropriate future modalities including text, voice, and accessible interfaces.

## 9. Archive Awareness

Important family communications may eventually become part of family history where intentionally preserved.

## 10. Principle

FEMC communication should help families talk to each other—not talk instead of them.
