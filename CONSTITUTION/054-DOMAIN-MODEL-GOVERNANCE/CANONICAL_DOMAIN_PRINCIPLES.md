# FEMC Canonical Domain Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. One Canonical Meaning

A core family concept should have one authoritative conceptual definition.

## 2. Identity

Person identity must remain distinct from account, role, and authorization.

## 3. Relationships

Relationships are first-class and may contain temporal and provenance context.

## 4. Events

Events represent meaningful family occurrences, not merely calendar rows.

## 5. Memories

Memories preserve family meaning and may contain or reference media.

## 6. Legacy

Legacy represents long-term continuity of family knowledge and history.

## 7. Permissions

Permission controls access to domain information; it does not redefine the information itself.

## 8. Derived Intelligence

AI and analytics may derive information but must not silently promote inference into canonical truth.

## 9. Principle

If a technical structure changes but the family meaning changes with it, the architecture has allowed implementation to own the domain.
