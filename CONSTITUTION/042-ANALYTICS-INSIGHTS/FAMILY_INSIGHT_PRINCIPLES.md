# FEMC Family Insight Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Meaningful Patterns

Prioritize insights that have understandable family value.

## 2. Context

An insight should retain enough context to avoid misleading interpretation.

## 3. Permission

Insights must not reveal information that the recipient could not otherwise access.

## 4. Human Interpretation

Families should be able to interpret and question meaningful insights.

## 5. AI Humility

AI should acknowledge uncertainty when evidence is weak.

## 6. No Pathologizing

FEMC should not casually label family behavior, relationships, or individuals based on limited data.

## 7. No Hidden Scoring

Family members should not be secretly ranked or scored for engagement, affection, participation, or worth.

## 8. Historical Awareness

Patterns should account for changing family circumstances and historical context.

## 9. Principle

A useful insight should illuminate family meaning without pretending to understand more than the evidence supports.
