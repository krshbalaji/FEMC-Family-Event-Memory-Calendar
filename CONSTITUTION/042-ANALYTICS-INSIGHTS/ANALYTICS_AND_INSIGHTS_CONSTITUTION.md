# FEMC Analytics and Insights Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define analytics and insights as tools for understanding family activity and product health without turning family life into a surveillance dataset.

## 1. Analytics Is Not the Family Truth

Analytics are derived views over canonical family information.

They must not replace the underlying family record.

## 2. Purpose Before Collection

Analytics should collect only information justified by a meaningful purpose.

## 3. Family Insights

Future insights may help families understand patterns such as:

- recurring celebrations;
- family milestones;
- memory history;
- participation patterns;
- important anniversaries.

Insights must remain respectful and optional where appropriate.

## 4. No Manipulative Optimization

Analytics must not be used primarily to maximize attention, notifications, or compulsive engagement.

## 5. Privacy

Family analytics may reveal sensitive patterns.

Access must follow appropriate permissions.

## 6. AI Insights

AI-generated insights must distinguish:

- confirmed information;
- derived patterns;
- inference;
- suggestion.

## 7. Uncertainty

Analytics should not create false certainty from incomplete family information.

## 8. Principle

Analytics should help families understand their story—not turn their story into a performance dashboard.
