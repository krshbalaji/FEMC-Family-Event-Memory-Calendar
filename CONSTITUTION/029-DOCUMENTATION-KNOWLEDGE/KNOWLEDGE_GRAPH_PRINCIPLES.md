# FEMC Knowledge Graph Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define how FEMC documentation and product knowledge should remain connected without requiring a specific graph technology.

## 1. Knowledge Is Connected

Important concepts should be traceable across:

- constitution;
- domain model;
- architecture;
- capabilities;
- AI strategy;
- governance;
- roadmap.

## 2. Stable References

Documents should use stable conceptual references where practical.

## 3. Avoid Duplicate Truth

The knowledge graph of documentation must not become another competing source of truth.

## 4. Dependency Visibility

A material architectural decision should be able to identify related documents and affected responsibilities.

## 5. AI Navigation

Future AI Council members should be able to traverse the library by concept, responsibility, and dependency rather than relying only on filenames.

## 6. Human Navigation

The same structure should remain understandable to human contributors.

## 7. Evolution

Knowledge relationships will evolve as FEMC grows.

The architecture should support updating relationships without rewriting history.

## 8. Principle

The goal is not to create the largest knowledge graph.

The goal is to make the right knowledge discoverable when it matters.
