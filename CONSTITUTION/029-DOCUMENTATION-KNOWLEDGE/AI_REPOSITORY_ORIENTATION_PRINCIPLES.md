# FEMC AI Repository Orientation Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define how future AI Council members should enter and understand the FEMC repository.

## Orientation Sequence

A new AI should conceptually traverse:

```text
MISSION
  ↓
MASTER INDEX
  ↓
FEMC ORIENTATION
  ↓
PRODUCT CONSTITUTION
  ↓
ASSIGNED OFFICE
  ↓
RELEVANT DOMAIN / ARCHITECTURE
  ↓
CURRENT TASK
```

## 1. Read Before Acting

An AI should understand the applicable constitutional context before proposing material changes.

## 2. Identify the Office

The AI must know its assigned responsibility before making decisions.

## 3. Follow Canonical References

When multiple documents discuss the same concept, the AI should identify the canonical source.

## 4. Do Not Invent Missing Authority

If a required decision belongs to another office, the AI should identify the dependency rather than assume authority.

## 5. Repository Over Conversation

Where durable repository documentation exists, it should take precedence over an unrecorded conversational assumption.

## 6. Output Discipline

AI outputs should identify:

- scope;
- assumptions;
- dependencies;
- affected offices;
- proposed documents or changes.

## 7. No Silent Constitutional Change

An AI must never convert a proposal into constitutional truth merely by writing it.

## 8. Principle

Every future AI should be able to enter FEMC without needing the original conversations to understand its responsibilities.
