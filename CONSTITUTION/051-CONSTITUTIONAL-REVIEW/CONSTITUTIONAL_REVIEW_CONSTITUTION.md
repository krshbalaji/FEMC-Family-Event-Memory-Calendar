# FEMC Constitutional Review Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Post-Closure Governance Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define how the completed initial constitution is reviewed without reopening stable principles unnecessarily.

## 1. Review Is Not Restart

Constitutional review exists to identify contradictions, omissions, duplication, or obsolete assumptions.

It is not permission to redesign FEMC casually.

## 2. Review Triggers

A constitutional review may be initiated by:

- a material new capability;
- a major architectural discovery;
- a significant legal or privacy requirement;
- a fundamental technology change;
- a discovered contradiction;
- a serious production lesson.

## 3. Stable Core

The following should be treated as highly stable:

- family-centered mission;
- privacy;
- data integrity;
- human agency;
- canonical family context;
- portability;
- long-term preservation.

## 4. Evidence

Proposed constitutional changes should identify the evidence or problem that requires change.

## 5. No Silent Amendments

Material constitutional changes must be explicitly documented.

## 6. Historical Continuity

Superseded principles should remain discoverable with their rationale and replacement.

## 7. Principle

The constitution evolves deliberately—not continuously for its own sake.
