# FEMC Constitutional Consistency Check

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Review Artifact  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Review Questions

### Mission
Does every major principle support the family memory intelligence mission?

### Domain
Are People, Relationships, Family Context, Events, Time, Memories, Media, Communication, and Legacy coherent?

### Trust
Are privacy, security, consent, access, safety, and integrity consistently applied?

### AI
Is AI consistently treated as assistance rather than canonical family authority?

### Portability
Can family meaning survive provider and technology changes?

### Architecture
Are technology decisions subordinate to stable domain meaning?

### Governance
Are responsibilities and boundaries explicit?

### Experience
Do simplicity and family value remain more important than engagement?

### Documentation
Can a future human or AI reconstruct the reasoning?

## Failure Condition

If two constitutional documents create materially conflicting authority, the conflict must be resolved before dependent implementation proceeds.

## Principle

Consistency is more valuable than document volume.
