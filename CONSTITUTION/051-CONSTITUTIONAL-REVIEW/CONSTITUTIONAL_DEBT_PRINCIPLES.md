# FEMC Constitutional Debt Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. What Is Constitutional Debt?

Constitutional debt exists when important product meaning is:

- ambiguous;
- duplicated;
- contradictory;
- undocumented;
- dependent on temporary assumptions.

## 2. Do Not Hide Debt

Known uncertainty should be recorded rather than converted into false certainty.

## 3. Prioritize Material Debt

Resolve debt that could affect:

- family trust;
- data integrity;
- architecture;
- security;
- privacy;
- long-term portability.

## 4. Avoid Perfectionism

Not every unresolved detail requires constitutional treatment.

## 5. Principle

The goal is not a perfect constitution.

The goal is a constitution that is clear enough to safely guide real work.
