# FEMC Time Model Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Preserve Original Meaning

The underlying temporal meaning should remain stable even when displayed differently.

## 2. Precision Is Optional

A time value may have different levels of precision.

The model should preserve what is actually known.

## 3. Relative Time

Some family history is naturally expressed relative to another event.

Future architecture should be capable of representing such relationships.

## 4. Recurrence

A recurring family occasion is a pattern, not merely a large collection of duplicated dates.

## 5. Historical Context

Historical calendar systems, cultural observances, and localized presentation may eventually need support.

## 6. Time Zones

A global family may contain members experiencing the same event from different time zones.

## 7. Ordering

When exact dates are unknown, the system should still support meaningful approximate ordering where appropriate.

## 8. AI

AI-generated temporal interpretation must retain uncertainty when the source information is uncertain.

## 9. Portability

Temporal information must remain understandable during export and migration.

## 10. Principle

FEMC should represent time honestly rather than forcing family history into convenient technical fields.
