# FEMC Portability Evolution Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Portability Is Continuous

Portability is not a feature added at the end of the product lifecycle.

## 2. Stable Concepts

Portable representations should center on stable family concepts rather than implementation details.

## 3. Versioned Contracts

Future export and import contracts should be versioned.

## 4. Backward Understanding

New versions should consider how older family information remains understandable.

## 5. Media Context

Media portability must preserve relevant family associations.

## 6. Human Readability

Where practical, portability should include understandable representations for people.

## 7. Provider Replacement

A provider replacement should not require reconstructing the family story from scratch.

## 8. Principle

A family should be able to move its information without losing its identity or history.
