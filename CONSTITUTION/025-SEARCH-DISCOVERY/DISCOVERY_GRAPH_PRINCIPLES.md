# FEMC Discovery Graph Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Core Idea

Family information gains meaning through relationships.

A discovery path may look like:

```text
Person
  ↓
Relationship
  ↓
Event
  ↓
Memory
  ↓
Media
  ↓
Story
  ↓
Legacy
```

## Principle 1 — Multiple Paths

The same memory should be discoverable through different meaningful contexts where authorized.

## Principle 2 — Relationship-Aware

Discovery should use relationship context without assuming that every relationship grants access.

## Principle 3 — Time-Aware

Discovery should understand dates, periods, recurrence, and historical context.

## Principle 4 — Provenance-Aware

Results should retain sufficient context to understand where important information came from.

## Principle 5 — No Fabricated Connections

AI or search systems must not create family relationships merely because two pieces of information appear related.

## Principle 6 — Explainable Context

Important intelligent discovery should provide enough context for users to understand why information was surfaced.

## Principle 7 — Stable Canonical Model

The discovery graph may evolve independently from the canonical domain model.

## Principle 8 — Replaceable Intelligence

Search and discovery mechanisms may change without changing the underlying family record.
