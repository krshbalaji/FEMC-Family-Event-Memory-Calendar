# FEMC Delegation Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Explicit Scope

Delegation should identify what authority is being delegated.

## 2. Time Boundaries

Where appropriate, delegation may have a defined duration.

## 3. Action Boundaries

Viewing, editing, sharing, deleting, exporting, and administering are distinct permissions.

## 4. No Hidden Expansion

Delegating one capability must not automatically grant unrelated capabilities.

## 5. Revocation

Where applicable, delegated access should be revocable.

## 6. Historical Integrity

Revoking delegation should not erase actions that legitimately occurred while the delegation was active.

## 7. Auditability

Material delegation changes should support appropriate accountability.

## 8. AI Delegation

Granting an AI assistant permission to help with one task must not automatically grant unrestricted access to family information.

## 9. Principle

Delegation should make family stewardship easier without making authority ambiguous.
