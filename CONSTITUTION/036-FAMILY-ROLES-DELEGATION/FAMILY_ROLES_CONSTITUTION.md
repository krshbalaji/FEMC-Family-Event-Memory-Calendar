# FEMC Family Roles Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define roles as contextual responsibilities within a family ecosystem, without confusing roles with identity, ownership, or permanent authority.

## 1. Role Is Contextual

A role describes what a person may do within a particular family context.

It does not completely define the person.

## 2. Role Is Not Identity

A login, account, person, and role are distinct concepts.

## 3. Role Is Not Ownership

Having an administrative or stewardship role does not mean owning the family's memories.

## 4. Roles May Change

Family responsibilities evolve.

The system must support role changes without destroying historical context.

## 5. Delegation

People may delegate specific responsibilities where appropriate.

Delegation should be:

- explicit;
- bounded;
- understandable;
- revocable where applicable.

## 6. Guardianship and Dependents

Future capabilities must be capable of supporting situations where one person manages certain activities on behalf of another.

## 7. AI

AI may assist a role but must not silently acquire the authority represented by the role.

## 8. Principle

Roles define responsibilities.

They do not redefine the family.
