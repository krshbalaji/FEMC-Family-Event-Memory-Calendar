# FEMC Accessibility Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Constitutional Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Establish accessibility as a core property of FEMC so family members across generations and abilities can participate in the family memory ecosystem.

## 1. Accessibility Is Core

Accessibility is not an optional enhancement or a separate product mode.

## 2. Generational Design

FEMC should account for differences in:

- age;
- vision;
- hearing;
- mobility;
- cognition;
- literacy;
- technical familiarity.

## 3. Multiple Interaction Modes

Future experiences may include:

- visual interaction;
- keyboard interaction;
- voice;
- text;
- assistive technologies;
- other accessible modalities.

## 4. Information Must Remain Meaningful

Changing presentation for accessibility must not remove important family context.

## 5. AI Accessibility

AI may assist with:

- reading;
- transcription;
- translation;
- summarization;
- voice interaction.

AI output must remain grounded and controllable.

## 6. Simplicity

Accessibility often improves the experience for everyone.

The product should favor understandable interaction over unnecessary interface complexity.

## 7. Media Accessibility

Where practical, important media should support appropriate accessibility representations such as descriptions, captions, or transcripts.

## 8. No Separate Family

Accessibility should not force family members into an isolated product experience.

## 9. Principle

Every generation should have a meaningful way to participate in the family's digital memory space.
