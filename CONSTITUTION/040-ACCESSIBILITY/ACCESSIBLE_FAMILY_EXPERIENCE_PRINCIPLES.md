# FEMC Accessible Family Experience Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Understandable

Core actions should be understandable without requiring technical expertise.

## 2. Perceivable

Important information should be available through appropriate supported modalities.

## 3. Operable

Core journeys should remain usable through supported input methods.

## 4. Forgiving

The experience should help users recover from mistakes.

## 5. Consistent

Accessibility behavior should remain coherent across family journeys.

## 6. Multilingual

Accessibility and language support should work together.

## 7. Voice

Voice may become an important interface for family members who find visual interfaces difficult.

## 8. AI Assistance

AI may help adapt information to user needs, but adaptation must not change canonical meaning.

## 9. Family Participation

Accessibility should enable participation in:

- memories;
- celebrations;
- communication;
- family history;
- discovery.

## 10. Principle

Accessibility means access to family meaning, not merely access to screens.
