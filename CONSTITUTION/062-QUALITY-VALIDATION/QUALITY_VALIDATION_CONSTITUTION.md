# FEMC Quality and Validation Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Technical Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define quality as evidence that FEMC behaves according to its product and constitutional commitments.

## 1. Quality Is Broader Than Testing

Quality includes:

- correctness;
- privacy;
- security;
- integrity;
- usability;
- accessibility;
- reliability;
- portability;
- maintainability;
- AI trustworthiness.

## 2. Family Journey Focus

Validation should prioritize meaningful family journeys rather than isolated technical units alone.

## 3. Canonical Integrity

Tests and validation must protect canonical family information from corruption or unintended mutation.

## 4. Permission Validation

Access boundaries must be verified, not assumed.

## 5. AI Validation

AI behavior should be evaluated for:

- grounding;
- uncertainty;
- authorization;
- harmful behavior;
- fabricated family facts;
- human control.

## 6. Production Readiness

A capability is not production-ready merely because it passes functional tests.

## 7. Principle

Quality is demonstrated evidence that FEMC can be trusted to do what it promises.
