# FEMC Family Crisis Continuity Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Crisis-Aware Design

FEMC should consider how family access may be affected during major disruptions.

## 2. Preserve Core Access

When optional capabilities fail, prioritize trusted access to important family information.

## 3. Stewardship

Where appropriate, families may require trusted delegated stewardship during periods when a primary participant cannot manage information.

## 4. Privacy

Crisis conditions must not become a blanket justification for unrestricted access.

## 5. Recovery

Recovery must preserve family context and historical integrity.

## 6. Communication

Material disruptions should be communicated honestly.

## 7. Principle

Resilience should protect both family continuity and family boundaries.
