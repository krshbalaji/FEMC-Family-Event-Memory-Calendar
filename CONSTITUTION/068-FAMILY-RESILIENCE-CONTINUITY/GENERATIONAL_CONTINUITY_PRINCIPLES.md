# FEMC Generational Continuity Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Generational Handover

Family stewardship may pass between generations.

## 2. Context Preservation

Future generations need more than files; they need understandable context.

## 3. Historical Honesty

Stories, memories, confirmed facts, and uncertainty should remain distinguishable.

## 4. Access Evolution

Permissions and roles may evolve as family circumstances change.

## 5. Technology Renewal

Future generations should not require obsolete software to understand family history.

## 6. Human Agency

AI may help future generations discover history but should not become the authority that defines it.

## 7. Principle

Every generation should inherit a family story they can understand, not merely a storage archive they can open.
