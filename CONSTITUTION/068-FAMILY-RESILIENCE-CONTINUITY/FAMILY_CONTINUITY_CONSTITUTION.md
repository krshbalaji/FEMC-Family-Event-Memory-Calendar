# FEMC Family Continuity Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Product Architecture Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Purpose

Define continuity as the ability of families to maintain access to meaningful relationships, memories, and history through changes in people, technology, circumstances, and generations.

## 1. Continuity Is a Product Outcome

FEMC should help preserve family connection and memory over time.

## 2. Human Continuity

Family participation may change as generations grow, move, or assume new responsibilities.

## 3. Technology Continuity

Technology changes must not destroy family meaning.

## 4. Stewardship Continuity

Future family stewards should be able to continue preservation without depending on the original team.

## 5. Information Continuity

Important information should remain understandable across migrations, archives, and implementation changes.

## 6. AI Continuity

AI model changes must not break access to canonical family history.

## 7. Crisis

Continuity planning should consider major operational, infrastructure, security, and provider failures.

## 8. Principle

FEMC should be designed so family history can outlive the circumstances under which it was created.
