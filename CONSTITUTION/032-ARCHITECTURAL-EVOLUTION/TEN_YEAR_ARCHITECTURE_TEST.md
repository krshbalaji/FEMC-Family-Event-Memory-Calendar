# FEMC Ten-Year Architecture Test

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

Before approving a major architectural decision, ask:

## Family

Will this still make sense for a family that has used FEMC for ten years?

## Memory

Can the decision preserve decades of family history?

## Privacy

Does it maintain trustworthy family boundaries as scale increases?

## Data

Can the underlying information remain portable and understandable?

## AI

Can AI providers and models change without breaking the family record?

## Infrastructure

Can underlying infrastructure evolve without changing canonical meaning?

## Operations

Can future teams operate and recover the system?

## Cost

Can the architecture remain economically sustainable at scale?

## Complexity

Is the complexity justified by a real requirement?

## Migration

If this decision proves wrong, can FEMC recover or evolve without catastrophic loss?

## Final Question

> Are we building a ten-year foundation, or merely solving today's technical problem?

The architecture should favor the former.
