# FEMC Architectural Decision Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Decision Sequence

```text
Need
 ↓
Context
 ↓
Constraints
 ↓
Options
 ↓
Trade-offs
 ↓
Decision
 ↓
Consequences
 ↓
Review
```

## 1. Start With the Problem

Do not start with a technology and search for a problem.

## 2. State Constraints

Consider:

- privacy;
- data integrity;
- scale;
- cost;
- maintainability;
- portability;
- reliability;
- AI boundaries.

## 3. Compare Alternatives

Important decisions should identify meaningful alternatives.

## 4. Record Trade-offs

There is rarely a perfect architecture.

The rationale for accepting a trade-off is part of the institutional memory.

## 5. Separate Constitutional From Technical Decisions

A technology choice should not silently become a product principle.

## 6. Review

Major decisions should be revisited when assumptions materially change.

## 7. Historical Record

Superseded architectural decisions should remain discoverable.

## 8. Principle

Good architecture is not the architecture with the most technology.

It is the architecture that best protects FEMC's long-term mission under real constraints.
