# FEMC Experience Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Strategic Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## 1. Warm, Not Manipulative

FEMC may feel welcoming and human without pretending to be human.

## 2. Simple, Not Shallow

The interface should be simple while the underlying family context can remain rich.

## 3. Connected, Not Crowded

Integration should reduce fragmentation, not fill every screen with information.

## 4. Personal, Not Intrusive

Personalization must respect privacy and user control.

## 5. Intelligent, Not Mysterious

AI should provide useful assistance while preserving understandable distinctions between facts and generated content.

## 6. Generational, Not Age-Specific

Design for families spanning generations rather than optimizing only for one demographic.

## 7. Accessible, Not Specialized

Accessibility should be part of the core experience.

## 8. Calm, Not Addictive

FEMC should not manufacture urgency or endless engagement.

## 9. Memory-Rich, Not Media-Heavy

A large number of photos does not automatically equal a rich memory.

The experience should preserve meaning and context.

## 10. Future-Proof, Not Trend-Driven

Experience principles should survive changes in devices, interfaces, and AI capabilities.

## Final Test

A family should feel that FEMC helps them remember and connect—not that the family has been asked to manage another complicated system.
