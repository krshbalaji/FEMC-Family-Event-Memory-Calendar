# FEMC Consent Lifecycle Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Owner:** Chief Product Architect

## Lifecycle

```text
INFORM
  ↓
REQUEST
  ↓
UNDERSTAND
  ↓
DECIDE
  ↓
RECORD
  ↓
USE
  ↓
REVIEW / CHANGE
  ↓
WITHDRAW WHERE APPLICABLE
```

## 1. Inform

People should receive meaningful information before important decisions.

## 2. Request

The request should identify the relevant purpose.

## 3. Understand

The experience should make important consequences understandable.

## 4. Decide

The person should have meaningful choice where applicable.

## 5. Record

Material consent or authorization states should be represented appropriately.

## 6. Use

Information should be used consistently with the authorized purpose.

## 7. Review

Permissions and consent may need to evolve.

## 8. Withdraw

Where withdrawal is applicable, the system should handle it predictably.

## 9. Historical Record

Withdrawal should not necessarily erase the historical fact that a previous authorization existed.

## 10. Principle

Consent is a continuing relationship of trust, not a one-time checkbox.
