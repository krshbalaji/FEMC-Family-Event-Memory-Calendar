# FEMC AI Event Assistance Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Events Intelligence
**Owner:** Events Office

## AI May Assist With

- extracting event candidates from authorized content;
- categorizing events;
- suggesting dates or relationships;
- summarizing event history;
- identifying potential conflicts;
- drafting reminders.

## AI Must Not Silently

- invent an event;
- convert an uncertain date into fact;
- alter canonical event state;
- expose restricted participant information;
- infer permission from event participation.

## Principle

AI may interpret event information; authoritative event state remains governed by the Events domain.
