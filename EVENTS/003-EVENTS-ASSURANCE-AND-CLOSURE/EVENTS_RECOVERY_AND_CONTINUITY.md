# FEMC Events Recovery and Continuity

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Events Assurance
**Owner:** Events Office

## Recovery

After data recovery or migration, verify:

- event identity;
- time semantics;
- recurrence;
- participants;
- status;
- relationships;
- provenance.

## Continuity

Calendar presentation may change while event meaning remains preserved.

## Principle

FEMC should recover the family's understanding of time, not merely restore calendar rows.
