# FEMC Event Scheduling and Conflict Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Events Architecture
**Owner:** Events Office

## Scheduling

Scheduling may consider:

- participants;
- availability;
- location;
- duration;
- recurrence;
- family preferences;
- existing commitments.

## Conflicts

A conflict is information, not automatically an error.

The system should distinguish:

- hard conflict;
- soft conflict;
- informational overlap;
- intentional overlap;
- unresolved conflict.

## Principle

FEMC should surface scheduling tension clearly rather than silently changing family plans.
