# FEMC Recurrence and Event Relationships

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Events Architecture
**Owner:** Events Office

## Recurrence

A recurring event should retain:

- recurrence rule;
- effective period;
- exceptions;
- instance state;
- source event identity.

## Relationships

Events may relate to:

- people;
- families;
- memories;
- places;
- other events;
- traditions;
- communications.

## Principle

Recurrence describes a pattern; each occurrence may still acquire its own family meaning and history.
