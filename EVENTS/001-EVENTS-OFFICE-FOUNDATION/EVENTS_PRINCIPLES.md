# FEMC Family Event Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Events Foundation
**Owner:** Events Office

## Principles

1. An event has meaning beyond its calendar rendering.
2. Time is part of event meaning.
3. Participants are explicit.
4. Recurrence is governed, not assumed.
5. Historical events remain distinguishable from planned events.
6. Event status must be explicit.
7. Time-zone context matters.
8. Uncertain dates remain uncertain.
9. Events can connect memories, people, relationships, and communication.
10. AI may assist event understanding but must not silently invent events.
11. Event visibility follows authorization and family context.
12. Event history should survive interface changes.

## Principle

FEMC should understand family time, not merely display dates.
