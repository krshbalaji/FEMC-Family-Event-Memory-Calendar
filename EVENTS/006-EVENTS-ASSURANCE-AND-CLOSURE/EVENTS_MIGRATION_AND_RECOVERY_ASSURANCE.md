# FEMC Events Migration and Recovery Assurance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Events Assurance
**Owner:** Events Office

## Verify After Migration or Recovery

- event identity;
- timestamps;
- time zones;
- recurrence rules;
- exceptions;
- participant relationships;
- status;
- provenance;
- reminder state;
- linked memories and communications.

## Principle

Recovery is complete only when both event meaning and its dependent family context remain coherent.
