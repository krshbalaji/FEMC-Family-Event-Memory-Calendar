# FEMC Media Album and Collection Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Media Architecture
**Owner:** Media Office

## Collections

Media may be organized into:

- albums;
- events;
- trips;
- people;
- family milestones;
- themes;
- stories;
- custom collections.

## Rules

Collections are organizational projections and must not duplicate or become the owner of the underlying media asset.

A media asset may belong to multiple collections.

## Principle

Collections organize family media without fragmenting its canonical identity.
