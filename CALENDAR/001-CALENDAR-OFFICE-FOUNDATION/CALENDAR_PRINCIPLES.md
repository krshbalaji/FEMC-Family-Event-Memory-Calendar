# FEMC Calendar Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Foundation
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Principles

1. Events remain canonical.
2. Calendar views are projections.
3. Time-zone meaning must be preserved.
4. Family context improves calendar usefulness.
5. Individual preferences remain meaningful.
6. Shared calendars do not imply universal access.
7. Historical events remain distinguishable from planned events.
8. External calendar providers remain replaceable.
9. Synchronization must not silently corrupt event meaning.
10. Reminders depend on valid event state.
11. Calendar should reduce coordination effort.
12. Calendar should remain useful without becoming intrusive.

## Principle

FEMC should make family time visible and manageable without turning every moment into an obligation.
