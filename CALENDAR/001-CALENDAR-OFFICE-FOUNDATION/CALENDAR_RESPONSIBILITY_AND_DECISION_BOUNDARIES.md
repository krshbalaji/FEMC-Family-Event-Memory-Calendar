# FEMC Calendar Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Foundation
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Calendar Decisions

The Calendar Office governs:

- calendar representation;
- views;
- agenda organization;
- calendar preferences;
- synchronization behavior;
- calendar-specific projections;
- calendar quality.

## Coordination

Calendar coordinates with:

- Events for canonical event meaning;
- Family for shared context;
- Identity for participants;
- Places for geographic context;
- Communication for reminders;
- Privacy for visibility;
- Data for persistence.

## Principle

Calendar owns how family time is experienced, not the underlying truth of family events.
