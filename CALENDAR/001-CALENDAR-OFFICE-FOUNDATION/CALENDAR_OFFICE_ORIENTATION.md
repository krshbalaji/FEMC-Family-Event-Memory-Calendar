# FEMC Calendar Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Foundation
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Mission

Turn canonical family events into a coherent, trustworthy experience of family time across calendars, views, reminders, schedules, and future interfaces.

## Calendar Owns

- calendar projections;
- calendar views;
- scheduling presentation;
- agenda experience;
- calendar-specific organization;
- calendar synchronization semantics;
- calendar quality and assurance.

## Calendar Does Not Own

Calendar does not redefine:

- event meaning;
- family identity;
- memory;
- places;
- media;
- communication;
- privacy;
- security;
- canonical data.

## Principle

The calendar is an experience of family time; the Event domain remains the authority for what actually happened or is planned.
