# FEMC Calendar Smart Planning Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Intelligence
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Smart Assistance

Authorized intelligence may suggest:

- suitable times;
- preparation windows;
- travel buffers;
- related family events;
- potential conflicts;
- recurring planning patterns.

## Rules

Suggestions remain suggestions until accepted through the appropriate authority.

## Principle

Smart planning helps families decide; it does not silently decide for them.
