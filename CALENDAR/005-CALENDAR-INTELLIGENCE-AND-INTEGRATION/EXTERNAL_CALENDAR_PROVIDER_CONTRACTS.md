# FEMC External Calendar Provider Contracts

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Integration
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Providers

External calendars may support:

- import;
- export;
- synchronization;
- availability;
- reminders;
- invitations.

## Contract Boundary

Provider identifiers, event formats, and synchronization mechanisms remain external representations.

FEMC canonical Event meaning must remain independent of provider implementation.

## Principle

External calendars extend FEMC's reach; they do not become FEMC's source of family truth.
