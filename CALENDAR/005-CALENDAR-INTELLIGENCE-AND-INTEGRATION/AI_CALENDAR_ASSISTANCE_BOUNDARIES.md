# FEMC AI Calendar Assistance Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Intelligence
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## AI May Assist With

- natural-language scheduling;
- agenda summaries;
- conflict explanations;
- reminder drafting;
- preparation suggestions;
- family-calendar summaries.

## AI Must Not Silently

- create authoritative events from speculation;
- change event ownership;
- override participant preferences;
- expose restricted calendar information;
- treat inferred availability as confirmed availability.

## Principle

AI can make calendar interaction conversational without becoming the hidden scheduler of family life.
