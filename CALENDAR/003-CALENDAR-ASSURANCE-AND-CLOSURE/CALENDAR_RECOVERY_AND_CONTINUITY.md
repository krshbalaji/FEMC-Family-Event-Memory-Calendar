# FEMC Calendar Recovery and Continuity

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Assurance
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Recovery

After migration, synchronization failure, or system recovery, verify:

- event identity;
- dates and times;
- time zones;
- recurrence;
- participant visibility;
- calendar membership;
- reminders;
- provider synchronization state.

## Principle

A restored calendar is correct only when it faithfully represents the authorized Event state.
