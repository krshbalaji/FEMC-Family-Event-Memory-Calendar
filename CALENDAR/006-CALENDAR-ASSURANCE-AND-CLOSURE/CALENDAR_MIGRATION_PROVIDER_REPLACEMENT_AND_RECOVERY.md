# FEMC Calendar Migration Provider Replacement and Recovery

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Assurance
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Verify

After migration, provider replacement, or recovery, verify:

- event identity;
- dates and times;
- time zones;
- recurrence;
- exceptions;
- participants;
- visibility;
- reminders;
- synchronization state;
- external-provider independence.

## Principle

A calendar provider may change; the family's canonical event history must remain coherent.
