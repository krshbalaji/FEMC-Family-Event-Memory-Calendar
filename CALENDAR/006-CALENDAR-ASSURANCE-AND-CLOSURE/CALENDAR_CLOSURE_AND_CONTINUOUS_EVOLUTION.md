# FEMC Calendar Closure and Continuous Evolution

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Coverage

Calendar foundations now address:

- calendar authority;
- family calendars;
- views;
- projections;
- preferences;
- shared views;
- synchronization;
- reconciliation;
- scheduling;
- conflicts;
- availability;
- reminders;
- smart planning;
- AI boundaries;
- external providers;
- scale;
- negative testing;
- migration;
- recovery.

## Closure Rule

New calendar capabilities can be evaluated against established rules for canonical Event authority, family coordination, temporal correctness, authorization, provider independence, resilience, and user control.
