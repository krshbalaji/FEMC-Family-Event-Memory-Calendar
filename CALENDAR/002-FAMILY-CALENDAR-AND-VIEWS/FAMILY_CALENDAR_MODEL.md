# FEMC Family Calendar Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Architecture
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Calendar Contexts

FEMC may support:

- personal calendar;
- family calendar;
- household calendar;
- shared event calendar;
- historical calendar;
- milestone calendar.

## Rules

A calendar is a view over authorized events.

One event may appear in multiple calendar contexts without becoming multiple canonical events.

## Principle

Multiple calendars may present the same family time from different perspectives.
