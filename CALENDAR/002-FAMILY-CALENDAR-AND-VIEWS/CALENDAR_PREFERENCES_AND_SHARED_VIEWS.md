# FEMC Calendar Preferences and Shared Views

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Governance
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Preferences

Users/families may control, where applicable:

- default view;
- week start;
- time format;
- time zone presentation;
- visible calendar categories;
- family overlays;
- reminder presentation;
- language.

## Shared Views

A shared calendar view must respect effective event visibility.

## Principle

Personalizing a calendar changes the experience, not the underlying family event.
