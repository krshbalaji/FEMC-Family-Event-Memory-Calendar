# FEMC Calendar Conflict and Availability Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Architecture
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Conflict Types

- hard conflict;
- soft conflict;
- informational overlap;
- intentional overlap;
- unresolved conflict.

## Availability

Availability is contextual and may depend on:

- person;
- calendar;
- family context;
- time zone;
- event priority;
- explicit preferences.

## Principle

A conflict is information to coordinate, not permission for the system to rewrite an event.
