# FEMC Calendar Scheduling and Coordination Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Architecture
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Purpose

Help families coordinate time around canonical events without silently changing plans or converting convenience into authority.

## Coordination

Scheduling may consider:

- participants;
- availability;
- duration;
- location;
- preparation time;
- family preferences;
- existing commitments.

## Principle

Calendar coordination surfaces choices; it does not make family decisions without appropriate authority.
