# FEMC Calendar Reminder and Preparation Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Calendar Architecture
**Owner:** Calendar Office

## Reminder Context

Reminders may account for:

- event importance;
- preparation;
- travel;
- participant role;
- family preference;
- prior acknowledgment.

## Rules

Reminder behavior must follow current event state and recipient authorization.

Cancelled or obsolete events must not continue generating actionable reminders.

## Principle

A reminder should reduce forgotten commitments, not create unnecessary pressure.
