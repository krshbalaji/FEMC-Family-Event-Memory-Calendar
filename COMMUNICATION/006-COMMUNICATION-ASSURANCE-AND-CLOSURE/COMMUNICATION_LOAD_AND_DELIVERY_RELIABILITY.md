# FEMC Communication Load and Delivery Reliability

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Assurance
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Reliability Areas

Test and observe:

- queue behavior;
- provider throttling;
- bursts;
- retries;
- duplicate suppression;
- delayed delivery;
- dead-letter handling;
- channel degradation.

## Principle

Communication reliability means preserving the user's expectation about whether a meaningful message was sent, delivered, delayed, or failed.
