# FEMC Communication Closure and Continuous Evolution

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Coverage

Communication foundations now address:

- communication authority;
- principles;
- notification semantics;
- preferences;
- family message context;
- delivery;
- privacy/content assurance;
- testing;
- channels;
- provider portability;
- multichannel consistency;
- automation;
- AI assistance;
- human control;
- reliability;
- negative testing.

## Closure Rule

The Communication foundation is sufficiently defined when new communication capabilities can be evaluated against established rules for purpose, context, control, privacy, channel independence, reliability, and family value.
