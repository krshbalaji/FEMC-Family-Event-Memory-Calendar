# FEMC Communication Privacy and Content Assurance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Assurance
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Verify

Communication assurance should consider:

- recipient authorization;
- message content;
- family context;
- channel exposure;
- attachments/media;
- AI-generated content;
- logging;
- retention;
- external provider handling.

## Rules

Do not include more family information in a message than the communication purpose requires.

## Principle

A secure channel does not make an overexposed message private.
