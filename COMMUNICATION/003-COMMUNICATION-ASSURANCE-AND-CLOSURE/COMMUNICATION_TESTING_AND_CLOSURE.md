# FEMC Communication Testing and Closure

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Test Areas

- recipient correctness;
- authorization;
- notification preference behavior;
- duplicate prevention;
- delayed delivery;
- provider outage;
- retry behavior;
- content correctness;
- AI-assisted messaging;
- privacy boundaries;
- unsubscribe/quiet controls;
- recovery and reconciliation.

## Closure Rule

The Communication foundation is sufficiently defined when new communication capabilities can be evaluated against established principles for purpose, context, control, privacy, delivery, failure, and family value.

## Principle

Communication remains healthy when it becomes more useful without becoming more intrusive.
