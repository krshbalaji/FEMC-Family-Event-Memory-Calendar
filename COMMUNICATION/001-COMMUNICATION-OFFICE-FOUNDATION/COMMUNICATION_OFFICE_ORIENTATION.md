# FEMC Communication Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Foundation
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Mission

Enable trustworthy, useful, respectful communication between FEMC and families without turning communication into noise, manipulation, or uncontrolled data exposure.

## Communication Owns

- communication architecture at the product/capability level;
- notification semantics;
- message lifecycle;
- communication preferences;
- delivery-channel governance;
- family communication experience;
- communication quality and assurance.

## Communication Does Not Own

The Communication Office does not redefine:

- constitutional principles;
- family-domain meaning;
- product strategy;
- architecture;
- privacy;
- security;
- engineering implementation;
- external provider ownership.

## Principle

FEMC communicates because something meaningful should be communicated—not merely because a channel is available.
