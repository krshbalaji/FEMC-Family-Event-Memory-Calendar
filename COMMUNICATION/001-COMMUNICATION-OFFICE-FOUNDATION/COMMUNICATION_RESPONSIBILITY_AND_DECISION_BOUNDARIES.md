# FEMC Communication Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Foundation
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Communication Decisions

The Communication Office governs:

- when a communication capability is appropriate;
- message purpose;
- recipient context;
- channel selection;
- delivery lifecycle;
- user preferences;
- communication reliability;
- communication evidence.

## Coordination

Communication coordinates with:

- Product for family value;
- Privacy for visibility and consent;
- Security for channel protection;
- Data for recipient and event information;
- AI for authorized message assistance;
- Operations for delivery incidents.

## Principle

Communication owns the act of communicating; it does not own the family information that makes communication meaningful.
