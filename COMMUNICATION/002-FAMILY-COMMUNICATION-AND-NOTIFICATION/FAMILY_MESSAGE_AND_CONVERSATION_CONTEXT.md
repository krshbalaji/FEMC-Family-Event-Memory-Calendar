# FEMC Family Message and Conversation Context

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Architecture
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Context

A family message may relate to:

- person;
- relationship;
- event;
- memory;
- celebration;
- reminder;
- family activity;
- operational matter.

## Rules

Messages should retain enough context for the recipient to understand why they received them.

AI-generated messages must remain distinguishable from direct family-authored communication where that distinction matters.

## Principle

Context makes communication meaningful; automation must not erase context.
