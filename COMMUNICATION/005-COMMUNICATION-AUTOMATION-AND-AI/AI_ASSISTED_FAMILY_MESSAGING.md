# FEMC AI-Assisted Family Messaging

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Intelligence
**Owner:** Communication Office

## AI May Assist With

- drafting;
- translation;
- tone adaptation;
- summarization;
- accessibility transformations;
- event-related reminders;
- authorized personalized wording.

## AI Must Not Silently

- invent family facts;
- impersonate a family member;
- expose private context;
- alter material commitments;
- conceal that generated content is generated when that distinction matters.

## Principle

AI can help a family communicate more naturally without becoming the hidden author of family relationships.
