# FEMC Communication Automation Safety and Human Control

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Assurance
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Human Control

Human review or confirmation should be available where automation could create:

- material commitments;
- sensitive disclosures;
- emotionally consequential communication;
- broad family distribution;
- irreversible external actions.

## Safety Controls

Consider:

- preview;
- approval;
- recipient confirmation;
- rate limits;
- duplicate prevention;
- cancellation;
- audit evidence;
- fallback behavior.

## Principle

The more consequential the message, the stronger the case for human control.
