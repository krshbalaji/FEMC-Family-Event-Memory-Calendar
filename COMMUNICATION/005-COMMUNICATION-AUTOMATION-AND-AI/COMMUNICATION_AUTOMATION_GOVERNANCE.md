# FEMC Communication Automation Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Communication Governance
**Owner:** Communication Office

## Automation May

- schedule authorized reminders;
- select appropriate channels;
- suppress duplicate messages;
- summarize approved context;
- coordinate recurring family communications;
- assist with message preparation.

## Automation Must Respect

- recipient authorization;
- preferences;
- quiet periods;
- privacy;
- message purpose;
- family context;
- failure/recovery rules.

## Principle

Automation may reduce communication effort; it must not remove meaningful family control.
