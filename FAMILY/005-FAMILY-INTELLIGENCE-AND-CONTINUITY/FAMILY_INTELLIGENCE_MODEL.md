# FEMC Family Intelligence Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Intelligence
**Owner:** Family Office

## Intelligence May Connect

- people and relationships;
- events;
- memories;
- places;
- media;
- traditions;
- communication;
- family patterns.

## Rules

Intelligence should expose useful connections while preserving provenance and uncertainty.

Family intelligence is advisory unless explicitly governed as authoritative.

## Principle

FEMC may help a family notice patterns in its shared life without claiming to know what the family feels or should decide.
