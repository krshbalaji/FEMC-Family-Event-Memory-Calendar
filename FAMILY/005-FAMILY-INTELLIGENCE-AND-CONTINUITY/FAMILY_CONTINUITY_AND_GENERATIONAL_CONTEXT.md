# FEMC Family Continuity and Generational Context

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Continuity
**Owner:** Family Office

## Continuity

Preserve enough context for future family members to understand:

- people;
- relationships;
- places;
- events;
- memories;
- traditions;
- family decisions where appropriate.

## Rules

Current interfaces must not become prerequisites for understanding historical family context.

## Principle

Family continuity means the meaning of family life survives changes in members, technology, providers, and interfaces.
