# FEMC Family AI Assistance Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Intelligence
**Owner:** Family Office

## AI May Assist With

- family summaries;
- milestone discovery;
- coordination suggestions;
- reminders;
- relationship-aware navigation;
- tradition discovery;
- authorized family planning support.

## AI Must Not Silently

- decide family relationships;
- invent family history;
- infer consent;
- expose private family context;
- replace family members' decisions;
- turn suggestions into family policy.

## Principle

AI may assist the family; it must not become the family authority.
