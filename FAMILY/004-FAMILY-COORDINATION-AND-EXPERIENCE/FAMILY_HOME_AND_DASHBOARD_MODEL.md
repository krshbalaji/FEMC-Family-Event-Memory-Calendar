# FEMC Family Home and Dashboard Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Experience Architecture
**Owner:** Family Office

## Purpose

Provide a coherent family-level view without replacing the canonical domains beneath it.

## Family Home May Surface

- upcoming events;
- recent memories;
- family milestones;
- relevant places;
- shared media;
- important communication;
- family activity.

## Rule

The family home is a projection of authorized family context, not a new source of truth.

## Principle

The family should experience one coherent FEMC even though many specialized domains power it.
