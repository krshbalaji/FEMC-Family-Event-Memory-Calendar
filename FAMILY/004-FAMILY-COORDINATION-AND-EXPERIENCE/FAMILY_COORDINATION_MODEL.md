# FEMC Family Coordination Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Architecture
**Owner:** Family Office

## Purpose

Coordinate shared family activity while preserving individual agency and domain ownership.

## Coordination Areas

- shared calendars;
- family events;
- celebrations;
- memories;
- media collections;
- journeys;
- reminders;
- family tasks;
- shared traditions.

## Rule

Coordination creates shared context; it does not automatically create shared permission.

## Principle

FEMC should make family coordination easier without making every family decision centralized.
