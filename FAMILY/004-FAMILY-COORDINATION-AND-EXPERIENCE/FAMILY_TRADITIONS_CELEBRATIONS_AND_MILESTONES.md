# FEMC Family Traditions Celebrations and Milestones

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Experience Architecture
**Owner:** Family Office

## Family Meaning

FEMC may represent:

- birthdays;
- anniversaries;
- festivals;
- recurring traditions;
- family rituals;
- achievements;
- reunions;
- important milestones.

## Rules

Traditions may be recurring without being mandatory.

Historical traditions should remain distinguishable from current practice.

## Principle

FEMC should help families carry meaningful traditions forward without turning tradition into obligation.
