# FEMC Family Conflict and Reconciliation Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Assurance
**Owner:** Family Office

## Conflicts

Potential conflicts include:

- membership disagreement;
- relationship disagreement;
- household boundaries;
- conflicting family labels;
- duplicate family spaces;
- historical versus current context.

## Rules

Material disagreement should remain visible and governed rather than silently resolved by technical precedence.

## Principle

Family truth may contain perspectives; FEMC should not manufacture consensus where none exists.
