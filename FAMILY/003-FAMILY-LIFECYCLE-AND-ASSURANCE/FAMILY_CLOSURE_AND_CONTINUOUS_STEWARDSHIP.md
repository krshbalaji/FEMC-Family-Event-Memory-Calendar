# FEMC Family Closure and Continuous Stewardship

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Family Office

## Coverage

Family foundations now address:

- family authority;
- family-space semantics;
- membership;
- roles;
- shared context;
- preferences;
- lifecycle;
- conflict;
- reconciliation;
- privacy;
- continuity.

## Closure Rule

The Family foundation is sufficiently defined when new family capabilities can be evaluated against established rules for membership, shared context, individual agency, lifecycle, privacy, historical continuity, and reconciliation.

## Principle

The family is the living context FEMC serves; the software should adapt around that context rather than forcing the family to adapt to the software.
