# FEMC Family Lifecycle Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Architecture
**Owner:** Family Office

## Lifecycle

```text
CREATE / RECOGNIZE
 ↓
FORM
 ↓
GROW / CHANGE
 ↓
REORGANIZE
 ↓
PRESERVE
 ↓
TRANSITION
```

## Changes

Family context may change through:

- new members;
- marriages or partnerships;
- births;
- separation;
- household changes;
- migration;
- loss;
- changing family structures.

## Principle

Family lifecycle changes should update current context without unnecessarily erasing historical context.
