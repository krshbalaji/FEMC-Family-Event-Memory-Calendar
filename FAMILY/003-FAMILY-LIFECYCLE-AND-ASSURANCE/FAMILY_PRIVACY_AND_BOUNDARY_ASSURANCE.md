# FEMC Family Privacy and Boundary Assurance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Assurance
**Owner:** Family Office

## Verify

Family features must respect:

- individual privacy;
- family-space boundaries;
- household boundaries;
- membership state;
- historical context;
- communication preferences;
- sensitive relationships.

## Principle

Being part of a family context does not mean every family member should automatically see everything.
