# FEMC Family Scale and Performance Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Assurance
**Owner:** Family Office

## Scale Dimensions

FEMC may support:

- small households;
- extended families;
- multiple households;
- historical family records;
- multiple family spaces;
- complex relationship graphs;
- large media and memory collections.

## Priorities

Protect:

1. family-context integrity;
2. privacy boundaries;
3. responsive family experience;
4. historical continuity;
5. cross-domain coherence.

## Principle

Family complexity must not be solved by flattening the family model.
