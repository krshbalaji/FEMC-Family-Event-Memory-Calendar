# FEMC Family Expanded Negative Testing

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Assurance
**Owner:** Family Office

## Deliberate Tests

Test:

- unauthorized family membership;
- accidental cross-family visibility;
- stale membership;
- incorrect relationship propagation;
- family-space duplication;
- historical context overwritten by current state;
- individual preference overridden incorrectly;
- AI-generated false family connection;
- departed member retaining unintended access;
- family dashboard exposing restricted information.

## Principle

Family trust requires testing the boundaries between shared life and individual privacy.
