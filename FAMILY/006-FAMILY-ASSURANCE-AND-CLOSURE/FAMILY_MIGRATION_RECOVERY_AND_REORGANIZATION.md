# FEMC Family Migration Recovery and Reorganization

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Assurance
**Owner:** Family Office

## Verify

After migration, recovery, or family-space reorganization, verify:

- family identity;
- membership;
- relationships;
- historical context;
- permissions;
- shared content;
- individual preferences;
- provenance.

## Principle

Reorganizing the family space must not rewrite the family's history or accidentally broaden access.
