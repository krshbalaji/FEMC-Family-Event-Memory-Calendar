# FEMC Family Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Foundation
**Owner:** Family Office

## Mission

Represent the family as a living social context in which people, relationships, events, memories, places, media, communication, and traditions can meaningfully connect.

## Family Owns

- family-space semantics;
- family membership context;
- household/group context;
- family lifecycle;
- family-level preferences;
- family continuity;
- family-context assurance.

## Family Does Not Own

The Family Office does not redefine:

- constitutional principles;
- individual identity;
- canonical data;
- memory meaning;
- privacy authority;
- security;
- product strategy;
- technical implementation.

## Principle

FEMC is not merely a collection of individual accounts. Its value emerges from the family context connecting them.
