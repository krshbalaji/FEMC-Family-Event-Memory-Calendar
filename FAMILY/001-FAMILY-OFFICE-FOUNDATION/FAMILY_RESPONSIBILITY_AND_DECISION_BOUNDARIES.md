# FEMC Family Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Foundation
**Owner:** Family Office

## Family Decisions

The Family Office governs:

- family-space representation;
- family membership context;
- household/group semantics;
- family lifecycle;
- family-level settings;
- continuity of family context;
- family-level discovery boundaries.

## Coordination

Family coordinates with:

- Identity for person representation;
- Privacy for family visibility;
- Security for protection;
- Data for canonical representation;
- Events for shared family time;
- Memory for shared history;
- Communication for family coordination;
- Product for family experience.

## Principle

Family defines the shared context; individual identity remains individually meaningful within it.
