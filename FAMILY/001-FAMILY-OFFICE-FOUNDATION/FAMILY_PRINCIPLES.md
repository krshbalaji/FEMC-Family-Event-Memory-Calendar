# FEMC Family Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Foundation
**Owner:** Family Office

## Principles

1. Family is a living context, not merely an account group.
2. Membership and identity are distinct.
3. Family relationships may be complex and evolving.
4. Family context should preserve history.
5. Family-level settings do not erase individual preferences.
6. Shared access must be explicit and bounded.
7. Family continuity should survive technical changes.
8. A family may contain multiple households or contexts.
9. Family members retain individual agency.
10. AI may assist family coordination without becoming family authority.
11. Family context must not become a surveillance mechanism.
12. Family meaning should remain understandable across generations.

## Principle

FEMC should strengthen family connection without reducing people to membership records.
