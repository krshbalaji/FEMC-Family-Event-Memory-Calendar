# FEMC Family Preferences and Shared Context

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Governance
**Owner:** Family Office

## Shared Preferences

A family may establish preferences for:

- calendars;
- celebrations;
- communication;
- language;
- discovery;
- presentation;
- shared traditions.

## Individual Override

Where appropriate, individual preferences remain authoritative for the individual's own experience.

## Principle

Family personalization should coordinate people, not erase their differences.
