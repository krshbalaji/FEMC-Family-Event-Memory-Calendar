# FEMC Family Membership and Roles Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Architecture
**Owner:** Family Office

## Membership

Membership may include:

- active member;
- invited member;
- pending member;
- historical member;
- departed member;
- represented non-account member.

## Roles

Roles may describe family participation or administration, but must remain distinct from personal identity and technical authorization.

## Principle

A family role describes context; it does not define the whole person.
