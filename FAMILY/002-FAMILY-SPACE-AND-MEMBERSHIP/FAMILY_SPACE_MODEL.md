# FEMC Family Space Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Family Architecture
**Owner:** Family Office

## Family Space

A family space may contain:

- family identity;
- members;
- households;
- relationships;
- shared events;
- shared memories;
- shared media;
- places;
- traditions;
- communication contexts;
- family preferences.

## Rules

A family space is a contextual boundary, not an automatic permission grant.

One person may participate in multiple legitimate family contexts.

## Principle

Family space organizes shared context without flattening individual identity.
