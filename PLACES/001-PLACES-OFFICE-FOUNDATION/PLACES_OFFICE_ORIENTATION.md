# FEMC Places Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Foundation
**Owner:** Places Office

## Mission

Give FEMC a trustworthy understanding of place so family events, memories, people, stories, and journeys can retain meaningful geographic context without turning location into unnecessary surveillance.

## Places Owns

- place semantics;
- geographic context;
- place identity;
- place relationships;
- location provenance;
- place lifecycle;
- geographic quality and assurance.

## Places Does Not Own

The Places Office does not redefine:

- family identity;
- events;
- memories;
- privacy;
- security;
- communication;
- product strategy;
- engineering implementation.

## Principle

A place is family context, not merely a coordinate.
