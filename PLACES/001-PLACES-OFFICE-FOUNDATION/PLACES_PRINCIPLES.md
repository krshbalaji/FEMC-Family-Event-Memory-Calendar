# FEMC Family Places Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Foundation
**Owner:** Places Office

## Principles

1. Place is contextual meaning, not merely coordinates.
2. Location precision should be proportional to purpose.
3. Historical place context matters.
4. Provenance matters.
5. Approximate places remain approximate.
6. Private places require stronger protection.
7. Current location and historical location are distinct.
8. A place may have multiple names over time.
9. Family place meaning can exist without live tracking.
10. AI may assist place understanding but must not invent geographic facts.
11. Geographic representations should remain portable.
12. Family location context should not become surveillance by default.

## Principle

FEMC should help families remember where life happened without needing to know where everyone is all the time.
