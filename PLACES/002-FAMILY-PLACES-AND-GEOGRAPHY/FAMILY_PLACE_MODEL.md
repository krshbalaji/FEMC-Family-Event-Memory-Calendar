# FEMC Family Place Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Architecture
**Owner:** Places Office

## Place Components

A material place may contain:

- place identity;
- name;
- geographic representation;
- address or locality;
- place type;
- time context;
- relationships;
- provenance;
- visibility;
- confidence.

## Place Types

Examples include:

- home;
- school;
- workplace;
- family landmark;
- travel destination;
- event venue;
- ancestral place;
- meaningful personal place.

## Principle

FEMC models places according to family meaning, not only commercial map categories.
