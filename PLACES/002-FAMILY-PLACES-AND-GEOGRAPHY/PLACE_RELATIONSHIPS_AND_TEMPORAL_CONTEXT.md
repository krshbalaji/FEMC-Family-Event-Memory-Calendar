# FEMC Place Relationships and Temporal Context

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Architecture
**Owner:** Places Office

## Relationships

Places may connect to:

- people;
- families;
- events;
- memories;
- stories;
- journeys;
- communications.

## Temporal Context

A place relationship may be:

- current;
- historical;
- planned;
- recurring;
- approximate;
- ended.

## Principle

The fact that a family once lived, studied, worked, or gathered somewhere does not mean that location remains current.
