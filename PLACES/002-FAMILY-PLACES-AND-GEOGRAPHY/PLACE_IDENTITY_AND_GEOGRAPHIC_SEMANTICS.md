# FEMC Place Identity and Geographic Semantics

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Architecture
**Owner:** Places Office

## Geographic Concepts

FEMC should distinguish:

- exact coordinate;
- address;
- locality;
- region;
- country;
- landmark;
- area;
- approximate location.

## Rules

A place identity may survive changes in address, naming, administrative boundaries, or mapping providers.

Do not treat a mapping-provider identifier as the canonical family identity of a place.

## Principle

Maps change; family place meaning can remain.
