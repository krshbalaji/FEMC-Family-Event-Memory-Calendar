# FEMC Place Migration Provider Replacement and Recovery

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Assurance
**Owner:** Places Office

## Verify

After migration, provider replacement, or recovery, verify:

- canonical place identity;
- geographic representation;
- precision;
- historical names;
- temporal context;
- relationships;
- provenance;
- visibility;
- map/search behavior.

## Principle

Provider replacement should change the technical map service, not erase the family's geographic history.
