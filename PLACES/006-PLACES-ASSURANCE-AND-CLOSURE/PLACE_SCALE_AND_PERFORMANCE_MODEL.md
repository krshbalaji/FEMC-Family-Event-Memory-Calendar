# FEMC Place Scale and Performance Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Assurance
**Owner:** Places Office

## Scale Dimensions

Places may scale across:

- families;
- historical locations;
- journeys;
- map projections;
- geographic search;
- provider requests;
- location-linked memories and events.

## Priorities

Protect:

1. canonical place integrity;
2. privacy and precision controls;
3. responsive place discovery;
4. provider resilience;
5. secondary map experiences.

## Principle

Geographic scale must not weaken location privacy or canonical family meaning.
