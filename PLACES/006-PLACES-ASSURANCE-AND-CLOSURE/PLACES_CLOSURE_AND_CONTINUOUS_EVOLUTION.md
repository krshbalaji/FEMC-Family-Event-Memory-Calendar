# FEMC Places Closure and Continuous Evolution

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Places Office

## Coverage

Places foundations now address:

- place authority;
- geographic meaning;
- identity;
- temporal context;
- privacy and precision;
- maps;
- journeys;
- discovery;
- landmarks;
- Event/Memory linkage;
- AI assistance;
- external providers;
- scale;
- negative testing;
- migration;
- recovery.

## Closure Rule

The Places foundation is sufficiently defined when new geographic capabilities can be evaluated against established rules for family meaning, precision, privacy, provenance, provider independence, discovery, and recovery.
