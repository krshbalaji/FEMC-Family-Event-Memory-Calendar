# FEMC AI Geographic Assistance Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Intelligence
**Owner:** Places Office

## AI May Assist With

- geocoding suggestions;
- place-name normalization;
- geographic summaries;
- journey reconstruction suggestions;
- related-place discovery;
- map explanations;
- authorized location-aware search.

## AI Must Not Silently

- invent a family location;
- expose restricted exact coordinates;
- convert an approximate place into false precision;
- infer current whereabouts without authorized purpose;
- replace canonical place identity with provider data.

## Principle

AI may improve geographic understanding without becoming an uncontrolled location authority.
