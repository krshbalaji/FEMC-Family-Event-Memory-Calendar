# FEMC External Map and Geography Provider Contracts

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Integration
**Owner:** Places Office

## Provider Uses

External providers may support:

- geocoding;
- reverse geocoding;
- maps;
- routing;
- geographic search;
- place metadata.

## Contract Boundary

Provider identifiers and classifications remain external references.

FEMC canonical place identity must remain independent enough to survive provider replacement.

## Principle

A map provider can describe a place technically; the family determines what that place means to them.
