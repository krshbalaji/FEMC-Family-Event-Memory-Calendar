# FEMC Places Negative Testing

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Assurance
**Owner:** Places Office

## Deliberate Tests

Test:

- unauthorized exact-location exposure;
- stale location context;
- mapping-provider mismatch;
- incorrect geocoding;
- coordinate precision leakage;
- historical place incorrectly shown as current;
- deleted place resurfacing;
- AI-generated false location;
- timezone/location confusion.

## Principle

Place trust is demonstrated by preventing geographic meaning from becoming unintended geographic exposure.
