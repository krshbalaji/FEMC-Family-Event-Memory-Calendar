# FEMC Places Migration and Recovery Assurance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Assurance
**Owner:** Places Office

## Verify

After migration or recovery, verify:

- place identity;
- geographic representation;
- precision;
- names;
- historical context;
- relationships;
- provenance;
- visibility;
- mapping-provider independence.

## Principle

Recovery must preserve the family's relationship to a place, not merely restore coordinates.
