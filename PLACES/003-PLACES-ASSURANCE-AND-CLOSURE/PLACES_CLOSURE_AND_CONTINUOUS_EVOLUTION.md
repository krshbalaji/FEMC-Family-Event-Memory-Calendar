## Coverage

Places foundations now address:

- place authority;
- principles;
- family place model;
- geographic semantics;
- place identity;

- privacy and precision;
- negative testing;

## Closure Rule

The Places foundation is sufficiently defined when new place capabilities can be evaluated against established rules for geographic meaning, precision, privacy, provenance, historical context, and provider independence.
