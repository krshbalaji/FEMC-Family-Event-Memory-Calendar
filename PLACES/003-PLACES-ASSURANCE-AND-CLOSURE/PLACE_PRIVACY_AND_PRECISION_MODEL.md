# FEMC Place Privacy and Precision Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Assurance
**Owner:** Places Office

## Precision

Possible representations include:

```text
EXACT
 ↓
ADDRESS
 ↓
LOCALITY
 ↓
REGION
 ↓
COUNTRY
```

Use the least precise representation that still satisfies the intended purpose when higher precision is unnecessary.

## Sensitive Places

Additional care may be required for:

- homes;
- children's locations;
- private family locations;
- medical or sensitive locations;
- temporarily sensitive destinations.

## Principle

More geographic precision is not automatically better.
