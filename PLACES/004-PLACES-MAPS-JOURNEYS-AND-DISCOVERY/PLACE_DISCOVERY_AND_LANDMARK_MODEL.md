# FEMC Place Discovery and Landmark Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Intelligence
**Owner:** Places Office

## Discovery

Authorized discovery may surface:

- frequently remembered places;
- family landmarks;
- places linked to important events;
- travel destinations;
- ancestral places;
- places associated with recurring traditions.

## Rules

Discovery should respect visibility, precision, relevance, and family context.

## Principle

Place discovery should reveal meaningful family geography, not maximize location exposure.
