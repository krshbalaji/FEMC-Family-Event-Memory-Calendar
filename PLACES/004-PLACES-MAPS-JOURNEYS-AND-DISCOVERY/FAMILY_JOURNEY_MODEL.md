# FEMC Family Journey Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Places Architecture
**Owner:** Places Office

## Journey

A journey may connect:

- origin;
- destination;
- stops;
- participants;
- events;
- memories;
- dates;
- transportation context;
- stories.

## Rules

A journey may be reconstructed from authorized events and memories, but inferred paths remain distinguishable from explicitly recorded journeys.

## Principle

A family journey is a story through places and time, not merely a sequence of coordinates.
