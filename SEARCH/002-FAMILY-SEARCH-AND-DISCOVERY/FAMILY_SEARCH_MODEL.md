# FEMC Family Search Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Search Architecture
**Owner:** Search Office

## Search Domains

Authorized search may span:

- people;
- relationships;
- events;
- memories;
- places;
- media;
- stories;
- family records.

## Query Modes

- exact;
- semantic;
- temporal;
- relational;
- geographic;
- media-aware;
- natural language.

## Principle

A family search can ask “what happened at grandmother's old home?” even when no single record contains those exact words.
