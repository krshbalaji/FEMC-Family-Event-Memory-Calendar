# FEMC Search Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Search Foundation
**Owner:** Search Office

## Mission

Help families find meaningful information across people, events, memories, places, media, and family records without making search the owner of the underlying truth.

## Search Owns

- discovery semantics;
- indexing strategy;
- query interpretation;
- relevance;
- result presentation;
- search lifecycle;
- search quality and assurance.

## Search Does Not Own

Search does not redefine canonical identity, data, memory, events, places, media, privacy, security, or authorization.

## Principle

Search is a window into family knowledge, not the source of family truth.
