# FEMC Search Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Search Foundation
**Owner:** Search Office

## Search Decisions

The Search Office governs:

- indexing;
- query interpretation;
- relevance;
- ranking;
- discovery behavior;
- result explanation;
- index lifecycle;
- search quality.

## Coordination

Search coordinates with Identity, Data, Memory, Events, Places, Media, Privacy, Security, AI, and Product.

## Principle

Search may connect information across domains without becoming authoritative over those domains.
