# FEMC Search Temporal Relational and Contextual Querying

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Search Architecture
**Owner:** Search Office

## Dimensions

Search may combine:

- who;
- relationship;
- when;
- where;
- event;
- memory;
- media;
- story;
- family theme.

## Example

A query such as “photos from our trip with uncle two summers ago” may require relationship, time, journey, and media context.

## Principle

Family search often depends on relationships and context rather than isolated keywords.
