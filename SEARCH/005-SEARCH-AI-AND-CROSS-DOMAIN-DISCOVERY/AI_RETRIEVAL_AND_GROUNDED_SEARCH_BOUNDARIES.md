# FEMC AI Retrieval and Grounded Search Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Search Intelligence
**Owner:** Search Office

## AI May Assist With

- query interpretation;
- semantic retrieval;
- result grouping;
- summaries;
- relationship discovery;
- natural-language answers grounded in authorized records.

## AI Must Not

- invent search results;
- cite inaccessible records;
- hide uncertainty;
- convert inference into canonical family fact;
- bypass authorization through reasoning.

## Principle

AI may explain what authorized family knowledge contains; it must not manufacture what search could not find.
