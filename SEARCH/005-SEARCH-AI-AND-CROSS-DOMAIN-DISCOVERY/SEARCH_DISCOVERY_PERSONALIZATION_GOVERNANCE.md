# FEMC Search Discovery Personalization Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Search Governance
**Owner:** Search Office

## Personalization

Search may adapt to:

- user's language;
- family vocabulary;
- recent authorized context;
- preferred presentation;
- known relationship terminology.

## Boundaries

Personalization must not:

- reveal hidden information;
- permanently narrow discovery without user control;
- optimize for addictive engagement;
- convert assumptions into facts.

## Principle

Personalized search should understand the family better, not quietly decide what the family is allowed to know.
