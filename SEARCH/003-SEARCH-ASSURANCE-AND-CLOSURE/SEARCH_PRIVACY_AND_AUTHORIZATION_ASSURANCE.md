# FEMC Search Privacy and Authorization Assurance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Search Assurance
**Owner:** Search Office

## Verify

Search must enforce effective visibility across:

- canonical records;
- indexes;
- caches;
- snippets;
- suggestions;
- autocomplete;
- AI retrieval;
- exports.

## Principle

A hidden record is not hidden if its existence can still be discovered through search.
