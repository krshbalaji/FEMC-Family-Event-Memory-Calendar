# FEMC Search Resilience and Recovery

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Search Assurance
**Owner:** Search Office

## Recovery

After index loss or migration:

- canonical sources remain available;
- indexes can be rebuilt;
- authorization is re-applied;
- stale results are not presented as current;
- provenance remains intact.

## Principle

Loss of an index should reduce convenience, not corrupt family truth.
