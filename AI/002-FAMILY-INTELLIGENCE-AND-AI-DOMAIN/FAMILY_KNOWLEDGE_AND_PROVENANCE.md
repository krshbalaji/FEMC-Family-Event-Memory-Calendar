# FEMC Family Knowledge and AI Provenance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Domain
**Owner:** AI Office

## Knowledge Classes

- canonical family fact;
- user-provided statement;
- historical record;
- media-derived observation;
- AI inference;
- AI-generated narrative;
- external information.

## Provenance

Material AI responses should be capable of distinguishing source-backed information from inference or generation where the experience requires it.

## Correction

Users or authorized family processes must have a path to correct incorrect family information.

## Principle

AI should make family knowledge easier to understand without making its origin harder to understand.
