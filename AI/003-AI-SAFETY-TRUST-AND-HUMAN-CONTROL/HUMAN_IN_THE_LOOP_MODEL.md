# FEMC Human-in-the-Loop Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Safety
**Owner:** AI Office

## Control Levels

### Assist
AI suggests; human acts.

### Confirm
AI prepares an action; human explicitly confirms.

### Supervised Automation
AI acts within tightly bounded rules and remains observable.

### Prohibited Autonomous Authority
AI must not independently decide matters that alter constitutional commitments, family authorization, or canonical family truth.

## Principle

Human control should increase with consequence.
