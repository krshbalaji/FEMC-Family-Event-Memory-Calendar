# FEMC AI Evaluation and Trust Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Safety
**Owner:** AI Office

## Evaluation Areas

- factual grounding;
- family-context correctness;
- authorization adherence;
- privacy;
- consistency;
- harmful behavior;
- uncertainty handling;
- prompt-injection resistance;
- tool-use safety;
- user correction behavior.

## Gate Outcomes

**READY** — evidence supports controlled use.

**CONDITIONAL** — bounded limitations and mitigations are explicit.

**BLOCKED** — material trust or safety risk remains.

## Principle

A capable model is not automatically a trustworthy FEMC capability.
