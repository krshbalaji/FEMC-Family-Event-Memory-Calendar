# FEMC AI Safety Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Safety
**Owner:** AI Office

## Safety Areas

- privacy;
- authorization;
- hallucination;
- prompt injection;
- harmful inference;
- emotional manipulation;
- unsafe automation;
- data leakage;
- misleading family history.

## Risk Levels

Higher-risk AI behavior requires stronger validation, narrower permissions, and greater human control.

## Principle

AI safety is not only about malicious users; it is also about ordinary model error affecting meaningful family information.
