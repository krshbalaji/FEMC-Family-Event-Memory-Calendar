# FEMC AI Closure Assessment

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Foundation Closure
**Owner:** AI Office

## Coverage

AI foundations now address:

- AI responsibility;
- family intelligence;
- memory and context;
- provenance;
- safety;
- human control;
- retrieval authorization;
- grounding;
- agents;
- tools;
- automation;
- evaluation;
- red teaming;
- provider portability;
- model selection;
- cost;
- family experience;
- communication;
- accessibility.

## Closure Rule

The AI foundation is sufficiently defined when new AI capabilities can be evaluated against established boundaries rather than requiring a new AI doctrine for each feature.

## Principle

AI documentation should establish the rules of trustworthy intelligence; real evaluation should determine whether a capability deserves to exist.
