# FEMC AI Production Trust Operating Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Handoff
**Owner:** AI Office

## Continuous Conditions

- [ ] AI remains within authorized context.
- [ ] Canonical family truth remains protected.
- [ ] Material outputs remain appropriately grounded.
- [ ] Tool authority remains bounded.
- [ ] Human control matches consequence.
- [ ] Evaluation remains current.
- [ ] Known failure modes are monitored.
- [ ] Provider changes are controlled.
- [ ] AI cost remains sustainable.
- [ ] Family experience remains respectful and non-manipulative.
- [ ] Material residual risks have owners.

## Gate Meaning

This is not a permanent approval.

A material change in model, data, capability, authority, or behavior can reopen the gate.

## Principle

FEMC AI remains trustworthy by continuously proving that its behavior still deserves trust.
