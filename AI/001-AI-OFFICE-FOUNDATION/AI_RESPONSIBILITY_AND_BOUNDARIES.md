# FEMC AI Responsibility and Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Foundation
**Owner:** AI Office

## Core Distinctions

```text
FAMILY TRUTH
    ≠
AI INTERPRETATION
    ≠
AI RECOMMENDATION
    ≠
AI ACTION
```

## Rules

AI may interpret, summarize, recommend, classify, assist, and generate where authorized.

AI must not silently convert interpretation into canonical family truth.

AI actions must remain bounded by authorization and product policy.

## Escalation

Material changes to family meaning, trust, privacy, or architecture move to the appropriate authority.

## Principle

AI can help FEMC understand family life; it cannot redefine family life.
