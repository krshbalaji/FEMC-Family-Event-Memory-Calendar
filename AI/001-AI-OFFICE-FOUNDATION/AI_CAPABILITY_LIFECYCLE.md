# FEMC AI Capability Lifecycle

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Foundation
**Owner:** AI Office

## Lifecycle

```text
IDEA
 ↓
FAMILY VALUE
 ↓
RISK ASSESSMENT
 ↓
PROTOTYPE
 ↓
EVALUATE
 ↓
CONTROL
 ↓
RELEASE
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
IMPROVE / RETIRE
```

## Required Questions

- What family problem does this solve?
- What information does it require?
- What could it get wrong?
- What authority does it have?
- How is output validated?
- How can users correct it?
- Can it be replaced?
- Can it be retired safely?

## Principle

An AI capability is successful only when its family value exceeds its trust and operational cost.
