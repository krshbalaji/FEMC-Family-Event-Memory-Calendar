# FEMC AI Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Foundation
**Owner:** AI Office

## Mission

Design and govern FEMC's artificial intelligence capabilities so AI strengthens family memory, connection, discovery, communication, and legacy without becoming the authority over family truth.

## AI Owns

- AI capability strategy;
- AI domain patterns;
- AI experience principles;
- model/provider abstraction strategy;
- AI evaluation;
- AI safety coordination;
- AI capability roadmap;
- AI-specific evidence.

## AI Does Not Own

AI does not redefine:

- family-domain truth;
- constitutional principles;
- authorization;
- privacy requirements;
- product authority;
- architectural boundaries.

## Principle

AI is a capability inside FEMC's family intelligence platform, not a separate intelligence product attached to it.
