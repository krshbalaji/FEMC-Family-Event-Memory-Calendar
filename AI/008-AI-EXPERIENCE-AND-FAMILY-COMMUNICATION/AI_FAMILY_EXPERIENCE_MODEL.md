# FEMC AI Family Experience Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Product Architecture
**Owner:** AI Office

## Experience Principle

AI should make family information easier to discover, understand, celebrate, communicate, and preserve.

## Experience Modes

- conversational discovery;
- memory resurfacing;
- event assistance;
- celebration assistance;
- family storytelling;
- organization assistance;
- communication drafting;
- legacy exploration.

## Rules

AI should remain understandable and respectful.

The system must not manufacture urgency, guilt, dependency, or artificial emotional pressure.

## Principle

AI should feel like helpful family intelligence—not an engagement trap.
