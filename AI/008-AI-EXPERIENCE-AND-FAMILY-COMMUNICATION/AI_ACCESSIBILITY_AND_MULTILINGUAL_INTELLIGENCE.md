# FEMC AI Accessibility and Multilingual Intelligence

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Product Architecture
**Owner:** AI Office

## Objective

Make family intelligence accessible across language, literacy, interaction, and accessibility needs.

## Requirements

AI experiences should support, where product scope permits:

- multilingual interaction;
- translation with uncertainty awareness;
- speech input/output;
- simplified explanations;
- accessible presentation;
- culturally respectful phrasing.

## Rule

Translation or personalization must not silently alter family facts, relationships, dates, permissions, or meaning.

## Principle

Language adaptation changes the expression of family knowledge, not the family knowledge itself.
