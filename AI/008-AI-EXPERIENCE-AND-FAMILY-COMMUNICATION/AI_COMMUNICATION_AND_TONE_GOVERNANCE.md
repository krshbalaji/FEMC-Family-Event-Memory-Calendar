# FEMC AI Communication and Tone Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Product Architecture
**Owner:** AI Office

## Communication Requirements

AI communication should:

- respect family relationships;
- distinguish certainty from uncertainty;
- avoid pretending to be a real family member;
- avoid manipulative emotional language;
- preserve user choice;
- adapt presentation without changing underlying truth.

## Important Boundary

Personalization may change how information is expressed; it must not change authorization or factual integrity.

## Principle

Warmth is valuable when it remains honest.
