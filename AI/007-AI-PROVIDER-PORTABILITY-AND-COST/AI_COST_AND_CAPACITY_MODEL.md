# FEMC AI Cost and Capacity Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Operations
**Owner:** AI Office

## Cost Drivers

- inference volume;
- context size;
- media processing;
- embeddings/retrieval;
- model tier;
- storage;
- evaluation;
- human review.

## Requirements

Track cost by meaningful capability or workload where practical.

Cost optimization must not weaken:

- privacy;
- family-data integrity;
- grounding;
- safety;
- portability.

## Principle

AI economics must scale with family value, not with uncontrolled model consumption.
