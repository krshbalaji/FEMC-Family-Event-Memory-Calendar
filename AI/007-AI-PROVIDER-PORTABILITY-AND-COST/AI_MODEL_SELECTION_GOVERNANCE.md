# FEMC AI Model Selection Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Governance
**Owner:** AI Office

## Selection Factors

Evaluate models using:

- task quality;
- grounding;
- safety;
- privacy;
- latency;
- reliability;
- cost;
- context requirements;
- tool behavior;
- portability.

## Rule

The largest or newest model is not automatically the correct model.

Use the smallest capability that reliably satisfies the authorized task.

## Principle

Model selection is a product-and-trust decision, not a prestige decision.
