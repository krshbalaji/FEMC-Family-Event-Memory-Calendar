# FEMC AI Automation Levels

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Architecture
**Owner:** AI Office

## Levels

### Level 0 — Inform
AI provides information.

### Level 1 — Assist
AI prepares work for the user.

### Level 2 — Confirm
AI proposes an action and requires explicit confirmation.

### Level 3 — Bounded Automation
AI performs narrowly defined low-risk actions under policy.

### Level 4 — Restricted Autonomous Workflow
Allowed only where explicitly governed, reversible, observable, and low-consequence.

### Prohibited
Unbounded autonomy over family authorization, canonical truth, or constitutional decisions.

## Principle

Automation level follows consequence, not model capability.
