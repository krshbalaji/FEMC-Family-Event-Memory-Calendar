# FEMC AI Production Trust Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Assurance
**Owner:** AI Office

## Conditions

- [ ] Authorized context boundaries are verified.
- [ ] Canonical truth remains separate from AI output.
- [ ] Material AI claims have appropriate grounding.
- [ ] Tool permissions are bounded.
- [ ] High-impact actions have human/control safeguards.
- [ ] Prompt-injection defenses are tested.
- [ ] Privacy leakage tests pass.
- [ ] Failure behavior is understood.
- [ ] Monitoring and evaluation are operational.
- [ ] Provider replacement remains feasible.
- [ ] Material residual risks have owners.

## Outcome

**READY** — evidence supports controlled production use.

**CONDITIONAL** — bounded risks and mitigations are explicitly accepted.

**BLOCKED** — material AI trust or safety risk remains.

## Principle

Production AI is earned through evidence, not enabled because the model is impressive.
