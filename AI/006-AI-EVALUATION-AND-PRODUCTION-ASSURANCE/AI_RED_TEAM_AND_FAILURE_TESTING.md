# FEMC AI Red Team and Failure Testing

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Assurance
**Owner:** AI Office

## Test Categories

- prompt injection;
- context leakage;
- authorization bypass;
- hallucinated family history;
- misleading summaries;
- unsafe tool use;
- emotional manipulation;
- malicious media/content;
- provider failure;
- degraded retrieval.

## Required Outcome

Tests should establish not only that the model succeeds normally, but that predictable failures remain bounded.

## Principle

A trustworthy AI system is designed around its failure modes, not only its successful demonstrations.
