# FEMC Grounded AI Output Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** AI Architecture
**Owner:** AI Office

## Output Classes

### Source-grounded
Directly supported by authorized family records.

### Derived
Reasoned from available family context.

### Generative
Created for presentation, storytelling, or assistance.

### Uncertain
Insufficient evidence exists.

## Rules

Material family claims should distinguish fact from inference when the experience requires it.

When evidence conflicts, AI should not silently choose a convenient version.

## Principle

The more important the family claim, the stronger the grounding requirement.
