# FEMC Continuous Improvement Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Improvement Sources

Learn from:

- incidents;
- near misses;
- recovery exercises;
- releases;
- capacity trends;
- security events;
- privacy reviews;
- user-impact evidence;
- operational friction.

## Improvement Flow

```text
EVIDENCE
 ↓
ROOT CAUSE / PATTERN
 ↓
IMPROVEMENT
 ↓
IMPLEMENT
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
STANDARDIZE
```

## Principle

A mature living platform becomes safer and more reliable because every meaningful operational lesson feeds the system back into improvement.
