# FEMC Operational Governance Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Governance Areas

- reliability;
- security;
- privacy;
- capacity;
- change;
- recovery;
- dependencies;
- operational risk;
- production evidence.

## Governance Cycle

```text
MEASURE
 ↓
REVIEW
 ↓
DECIDE
 ↓
ACT
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
RECORD
```

## Escalation

Operational evidence that indicates an architecture, product, or constitutional problem must be routed to the appropriate authority.

## Principle

Operations governs system behavior without becoming the hidden owner of product meaning.
