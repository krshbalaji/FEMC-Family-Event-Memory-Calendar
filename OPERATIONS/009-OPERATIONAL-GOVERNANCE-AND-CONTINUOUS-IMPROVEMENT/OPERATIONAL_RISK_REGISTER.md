# FEMC Operational Risk Register

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Risk Classes

- availability;
- data integrity;
- privacy;
- security;
- dependency;
- capacity;
- recovery;
- operational complexity;
- human error.

## Each Material Risk Should Have

- description;
- impact;
- likelihood assessment;
- current controls;
- owner;
- mitigation;
- evidence;
- review date.

## Principle

Operational risks should become visible while they are still cheaper to control.
