# FEMC Operations Readiness Review

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Review Areas

- service health;
- incident response;
- recovery;
- backup;
- security;
- privacy;
- capacity;
- dependency continuity;
- release operations;
- support;
- observability;
- operational ownership.

## Evidence

Every material readiness claim should have supporting evidence, test results, operational procedures, or an explicit accepted gap.

## Outcome

**READY** — operational controls and evidence are sufficient.

**CONDITIONAL** — bounded gaps exist with owners and mitigations.

**NOT READY** — material operational risk remains uncontrolled.

## Principle

Operational readiness is a measurable state, not a ceremonial checklist.
