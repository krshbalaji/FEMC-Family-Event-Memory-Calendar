# FEMC Operational Scale Readiness Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Scale Dimensions

FEMC must consider growth in:

- families;
- geographic distribution;
- media volume;
- concurrent activity;
- search;
- AI;
- notifications;
- integrations;
- support demand;
- operational staff workload.

## Readiness Evidence

Scale readiness should be based on measured capacity, controlled load exercises, observed bottlenecks, and recovery behavior.

## Principle

Millions of families require operational scale, not merely infrastructure scale.
