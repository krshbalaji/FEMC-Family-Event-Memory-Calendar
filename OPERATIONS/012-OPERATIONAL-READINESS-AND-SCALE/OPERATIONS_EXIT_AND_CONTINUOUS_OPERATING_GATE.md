# FEMC Operations Continuous Operating Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Gate

Operations continuously verifies that:

- core family journeys remain trustworthy;
- canonical data remains protected;
- recovery remains executable;
- dependencies remain within acceptable risk;
- security and privacy controls remain effective;
- capacity remains adequate;
- support remains safe;
- operational evidence remains current.

## Escalation Trigger

A material change in product meaning, architecture, security boundary, or constitutional principle must leave the normal operational loop and return to the appropriate authority.

## Principle

Operations never declares FEMC “finished.” It keeps the living platform within its trust and readiness boundaries.
