# FEMC Production Change Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Change Classes

### Routine
Pre-approved low-risk operational changes.

### Planned
Material changes with documented impact and rollback/mitigation.

### Emergency
Changes required to protect users, security, availability, or integrity.

## Requirements

Material changes should identify:

- purpose;
- impact;
- owner;
- timing;
- validation;
- rollback or mitigation;
- monitoring;
- completion evidence.

## Principle

Production change is a controlled operation, not an informal administrative action.
