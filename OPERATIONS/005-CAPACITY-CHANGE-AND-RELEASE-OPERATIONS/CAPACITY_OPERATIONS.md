# FEMC Capacity Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Capacity Domains

Monitor and plan capacity for:

- compute;
- storage;
- media processing;
- database workloads;
- search;
- AI;
- queues;
- network;
- external provider limits.

## Operating Cycle

```text
MEASURE
 ↓
FORECAST
 ↓
PLAN
 ↓
SCALE
 ↓
VERIFY
```

## Rules

Capacity actions must not weaken privacy, integrity, or recovery.

## Principle

Capacity planning should anticipate real growth while avoiding complexity created only for imaginary demand.
