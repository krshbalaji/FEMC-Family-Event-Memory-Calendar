# FEMC Support Escalation and Case Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Case Classes

- general guidance;
- account/access issue;
- family-data issue;
- privacy concern;
- security concern;
- suspected integrity issue;
- platform outage;
- product or architecture concern.

## Escalation

```text
SUPPORT
  ↓
SPECIALIST
  ↓
SECURITY / OPERATIONS / ENGINEERING
  ↓
ARCHITECTURE / PRODUCT / GOVERNANCE
```

Escalate based on responsibility, not hierarchy.

## Principle

A support case should reach the smallest authority capable of resolving it correctly, while material risks move upward immediately.
