# FEMC Family Support Operating Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Purpose

Provide human support without turning support operations into unrestricted access to family information.

## Support Principles

- verify identity and authority;
- minimize information exposure;
- follow least-privilege procedures;
- record material support actions;
- distinguish explanation from modification;
- escalate security or privacy concerns.

## Support Must Not

- bypass family authorization;
- expose unrelated family records;
- alter canonical information without approved authority;
- use private family content for curiosity or convenience.

## Principle

Support exists to help families safely, not to become an alternate administrator of their lives.
