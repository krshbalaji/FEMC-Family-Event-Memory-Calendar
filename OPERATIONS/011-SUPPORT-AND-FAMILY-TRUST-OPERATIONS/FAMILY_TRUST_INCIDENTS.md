# FEMC Family Trust Incident Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Examples

Family trust incidents may include:

- unexpected sharing;
- incorrect access;
- lost family context;
- misleading system state;
- incorrect AI representation of family history;
- failed deletion or export;
- accidental exposure.

## Response

1. Protect affected people and information.
2. Stop further impact.
3. Preserve evidence.
4. Establish scope.
5. Correct canonical state where authorized.
6. Communicate clearly.
7. Capture systemic improvement.

## Principle

Trust incidents are product-impact incidents, not merely technical tickets.
