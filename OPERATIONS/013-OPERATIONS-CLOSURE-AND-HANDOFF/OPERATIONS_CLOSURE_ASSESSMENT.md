# FEMC Operations Closure Assessment

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations Office Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Purpose

Determine whether the Operations Office has established sufficient operating doctrine for transition into continuous production operations.

## Coverage

Operations documentation now covers:

- office responsibility;
- service health;
- observability;
- alerting;
- incident response;
- security and privacy operations;
- change and release operations;
- capacity;
- continuity;
- disaster recovery;
- backup and restoration;
- data lifecycle;
- portability;
- dependency and vendor risk;
- family support;
- operational scale;
- continuous improvement.

## Closure Rule

Closure does not mean operations stop.

It means the foundational operating model is sufficiently defined that future operational work can be driven by real production evidence rather than more abstract documentation.

## Principle

Once the operating doctrine is sufficient, production evidence should become the primary source of further operational improvement.
