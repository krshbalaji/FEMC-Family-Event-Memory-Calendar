# FEMC Operations to Continuous Improvement

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations Handoff
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Evidence Sources

Continuous improvement should draw from:

- incidents;
- near misses;
- recovery exercises;
- security events;
- privacy events;
- capacity trends;
- dependency failures;
- release outcomes;
- support patterns;
- family-impact evidence.

## Improvement Routing

```text
PRODUCTION EVIDENCE
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
┌───────────────┬───────────────┬───────────────┐
▼               ▼               ▼
OPERATIONS   ENGINEERING    ARCHITECTURE
                                  │
                                  ▼
                               PRODUCT
                                  │
                                  ▼
                             CONSTITUTION
```

Not every lesson belongs everywhere.

## Principle

The living platform should evolve through evidence while preserving its constitutional identity.
