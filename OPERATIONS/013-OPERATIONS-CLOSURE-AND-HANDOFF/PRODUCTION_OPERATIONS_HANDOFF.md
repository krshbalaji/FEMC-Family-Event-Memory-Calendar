# FEMC Production Operations Handoff

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations Handoff
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Handoff From

Engineering and Architecture provide the implemented and validated system.

## Handoff To

The ongoing Production Operations function.

## Required Inputs

- approved architecture;
- engineering release evidence;
- deployment procedures;
- observability;
- incident procedures;
- recovery procedures;
- security and privacy controls;
- dependency inventory;
- operational risk register;
- support procedures.

## Production Loop

```text
RELEASE
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
OPERATE
 ↓
INCIDENT / CHANGE / RECOVERY
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
LEARN
 ↓
IMPROVE
```

## Escalation

Operational evidence that reveals a product, architecture, or constitutional issue must return to the appropriate authority.

## Principle

Production operations inherits responsibility for running the system, not authority to redefine what the system means.
