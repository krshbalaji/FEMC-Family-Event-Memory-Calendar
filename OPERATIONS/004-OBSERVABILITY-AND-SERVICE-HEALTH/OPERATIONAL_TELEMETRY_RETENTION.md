# FEMC Operational Telemetry Retention

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Objective

Retain enough operational evidence to diagnose incidents, measure trends, and meet accountability needs while minimizing unnecessary family information.

## Rules

- Prefer technical metadata over family content.
- Apply access controls to operational telemetry.
- Define retention according to purpose.
- Review long-lived telemetry for unnecessary personal information.
- Protect security evidence from unauthorized alteration.
- Distinguish operational telemetry from canonical family records.

## Principle

Operational history should be useful without becoming a shadow archive of family life.
