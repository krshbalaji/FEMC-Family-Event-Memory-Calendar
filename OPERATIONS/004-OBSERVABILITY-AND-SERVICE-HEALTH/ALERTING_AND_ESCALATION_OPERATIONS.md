# FEMC Alerting and Escalation Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Alert Classes

- critical integrity/security;
- critical availability;
- major degradation;
- dependency failure;
- capacity warning;
- operational maintenance.

## Rules

1. Alerts must have an actionable owner.
2. Alert severity reflects user or system impact.
3. Avoid alert storms and duplicate escalation.
4. Security-sensitive alerts follow protected handling.
5. Alerts without meaningful action should be reviewed or removed.

## Escalation

Escalate when impact exceeds the operating team's authority or indicates an architectural/product issue.

## Principle

An alert is useful only when it leads to an appropriate decision.
