# FEMC Post-Incident Learning

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations Foundation

## Review Questions

- What happened?
- What was the user/family impact?
- How was it detected?
- Why did existing controls fail?
- Was canonical data affected?
- Was privacy affected?
- What prevented faster recovery?
- What should change?
- Which assumptions were wrong?

## Actions

Actions should have:

- owner;
- priority;
- evidence;
- target;
- verification method.

## Principle

The purpose of a post-incident review is to improve the system, not to manufacture blame.
