# FEMC Incident Command and Escalation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations Foundation

## Command Principles

During a material incident:

- establish a clear owner;
- establish impact;
- protect evidence;
- communicate appropriate status;
- avoid uncontrolled changes;
- coordinate technical and security response;
- escalate product or architecture implications.

## Escalation

Security incidents → Security authority.

Architecture-impacting incidents → Architecture Office.

Product-meaning impact → Product authority.

Constitutional conflict → Constitutional governance.

## Principle

An incident commander coordinates response; they do not silently redefine FEMC.
