# FEMC Incident Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations Foundation

## Incident Priorities

Incidents should be assessed according to impact on:

1. family privacy and safety;
2. canonical data integrity;
3. authorized access;
4. core family journeys;
5. secondary capabilities;
6. optional intelligence and integrations.

## Incident Lifecycle

```text
DETECT
 ↓
CLASSIFY
 ↓
CONTAIN
 ↓
RECOVER
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
CLOSE
 ↓
LEARN
```

## Principle

Incident response must protect people and family history before convenience features.
