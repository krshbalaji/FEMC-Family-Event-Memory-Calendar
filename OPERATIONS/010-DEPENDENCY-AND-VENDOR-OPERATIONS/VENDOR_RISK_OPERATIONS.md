# FEMC Vendor Risk Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Risk Areas

Evaluate material vendors for:

- service continuity;
- security;
- privacy;
- data portability;
- contractual exposure;
- pricing and capacity;
- provider concentration;
- retirement risk.

## Operational Review

Material vendor changes should trigger impact assessment before they become production surprises.

## Exit Readiness

Where practical, maintain enough knowledge to replace a critical vendor without losing canonical family meaning.

## Principle

Vendor convenience must never become irreversible family dependence.
