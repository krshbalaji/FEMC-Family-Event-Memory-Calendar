# FEMC Dependency Operations Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Purpose

Operate external and internal dependencies without allowing dependency failure to become family-data failure.

## Dependency Classes

- infrastructure;
- storage;
- communication;
- identity;
- AI;
- search;
- payment or commercial services where applicable;
- integration partners.

## Operational Responsibilities

For material dependencies, maintain visibility into:

- health;
- availability;
- contract status;
- capacity limits;
- security posture;
- known incidents;
- replacement or continuity options.

## Principle

A dependency is part of FEMC's operating environment, not the owner of FEMC's family domain.
