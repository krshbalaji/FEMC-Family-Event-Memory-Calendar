# FEMC Operations Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations Foundation
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Mission

Operate FEMC as a trustworthy living family platform after engineering delivery.

Operations owns the health, continuity, observability, incident response, recovery, capacity, and operational readiness of the running system.

## Operations Inherits

- Constitution;
- Architecture;
- Engineering implementation;
- security boundaries;
- data integrity requirements;
- recovery requirements;
- observability requirements.

## Operations Owns

- operational health;
- incident response;
- service continuity;
- capacity management;
- recovery execution;
- operational change coordination;
- production evidence.

## Boundary

Operations must not redefine family-domain meaning.

Material product, architectural, or constitutional changes must be escalated to the responsible office.

## Principle

Operations protects the family's ability to trust FEMC tomorrow, not merely its ability to run today.
