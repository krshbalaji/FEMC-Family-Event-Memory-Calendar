# FEMC Security and Privacy Incident Playbook

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Immediate Priorities

1. Protect affected people and family information.
2. Contain unauthorized access or exposure.
3. Preserve evidence.
4. Determine scope.
5. Recover trusted operation.
6. Communicate through the appropriate authority.
7. Record corrective actions.

## Do Not

- destroy evidence;
- make uncontrolled changes;
- expose additional family information while investigating;
- assume an incident is isolated without evidence.

## Escalation

Material incidents involving privacy, security, canonical integrity, or constitutional commitments must reach the appropriate security, architecture, product, or governance authority.

## Principle

During a security incident, speed matters—but disciplined containment matters more.
