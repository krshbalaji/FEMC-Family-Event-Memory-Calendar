# FEMC Privacy Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Privacy Responsibilities

Operations should monitor and support:

- access control;
- privileged access;
- data exposure risks;
- retention behavior;
- exports;
- sharing;
- deletion workflows;
- operational handling of family information.

## Rules

Operational staff should receive the minimum family information required to perform their duties.

Privacy concerns that indicate product or architecture gaps must be escalated rather than patched indefinitely through operational workarounds.

## Principle

Operational convenience never outranks family privacy.
