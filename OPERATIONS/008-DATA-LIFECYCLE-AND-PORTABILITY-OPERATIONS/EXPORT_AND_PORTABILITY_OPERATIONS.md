# FEMC Export and Portability Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Operational Flow

```text
REQUEST
 ↓
AUTHORIZE
 ↓
PREPARE
 ↓
VALIDATE
 ↓
EXPORT
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
DELIVER
 ↓
RECORD
```

## Requirements

Exports containing family information require appropriate protection throughout preparation, storage, transfer, and delivery.

Verification should establish that the export is complete and internally consistent to the defined scope.

## Principle

Portability is part of family trust; exports must be treated as sensitive operational products.
