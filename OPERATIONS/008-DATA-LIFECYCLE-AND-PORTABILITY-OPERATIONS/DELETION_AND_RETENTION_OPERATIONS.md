# FEMC Deletion and Retention Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Rules

- Retention behavior must follow approved policy.
- Deletion must be authorized.
- Operational copies must be considered.
- Backups require separate recovery-aware handling.
- Deletion failures must be visible and recoverable.
- Operators should not manually delete family information outside approved procedures.

## Verification

Material deletion workflows should verify intended scope and resulting state.

## Principle

Deletion is a governed family-data operation, not an informal cleanup task.
