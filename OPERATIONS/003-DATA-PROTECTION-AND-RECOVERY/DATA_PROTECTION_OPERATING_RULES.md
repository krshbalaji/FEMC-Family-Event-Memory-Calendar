# FEMC Data Protection Operating Rules

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations Foundation

## Rules

1. Protect canonical family information from unauthorized operational access.
2. Protect backups with appropriate security controls.
3. Keep recovery artifacts governed.
4. Minimize operational exposure to family content.
5. Verify restoration integrity.
6. Treat exports and recovery copies as sensitive.
7. Record material data-protection incidents.
8. Do not use production family data casually for operational testing.

## Principle

Operational access exists to keep FEMC trustworthy, not to provide curiosity-driven access to family information.
