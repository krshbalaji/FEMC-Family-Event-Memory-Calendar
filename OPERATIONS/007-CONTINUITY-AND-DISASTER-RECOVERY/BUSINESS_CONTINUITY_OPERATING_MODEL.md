# FEMC Business Continuity Operating Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Purpose

Keep essential family capabilities available or recoverable during major disruption.

## Continuity Priorities

1. Protect family privacy and safety.
2. Protect canonical family information.
3. Restore trusted identity and access.
4. Restore essential family journeys.
5. Restore media and secondary capabilities.
6. Restore derived intelligence and optional integrations.

## Principle

Continuity means preserving trusted family access and history, not merely keeping servers running.
