# FEMC Recovery Objectives and Testing

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Operations
**Owner:** Operations Office

## Objectives

For each critical capability define, as appropriate:

- recovery priority;
- acceptable data-loss window;
- acceptable service interruption;
- dependencies;
- verification method;
- responsible owner.

## Testing

Recovery exercises should include both expected and degraded conditions.

Test results must record:

- scenario;
- outcome;
- elapsed recovery time;
- integrity findings;
- unresolved gaps;
- corrective actions.

## Principle

Recovery objectives are meaningful only when measured through realistic exercises.
