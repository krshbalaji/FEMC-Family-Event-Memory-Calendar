# FEMC Communication and Celebration Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Purpose

Connect family communication and celebrations to the canonical family context rather than treating them as isolated messaging features.

## Connected Model

```text
PERSON
 ↓
FAMILY CONTEXT
 ↓
EVENT / OCCASION
 ↓
CELEBRATION
 ↓
COMMUNICATION
 ↓
MEMORY
 ↓
LEGACY
```

## Principles

1. Communication occurs within an authorized family context.
2. Celebrations may create or enrich memories.
3. Messages and media may become part of family history when intentionally preserved.
4. External communication providers remain replaceable.
5. Notifications must not become intrusive engagement mechanisms.

## Principle

Communication is valuable when it strengthens family connection and creates meaningful continuity.
