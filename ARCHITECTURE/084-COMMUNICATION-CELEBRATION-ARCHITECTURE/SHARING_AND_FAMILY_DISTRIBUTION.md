# FEMC Sharing and Family Distribution

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Purpose

Define sharing as controlled distribution of family information rather than unrestricted forwarding.

## Sharing Flow

```text
SOURCE
 ↓
REQUEST
 ↓
IDENTITY
 ↓
RESOURCE
 ↓
CONSENT / POLICY
 ↓
RECIPIENT
 ↓
CHANNEL
 ↓
AUDIT / REVOCATION WHERE APPLICABLE
```

## Principles

- Sharing must respect the source family's authorization.
- Recipient access should be explicit.
- External channels are separate trust domains.
- Exported copies may no longer be fully controllable by FEMC.
- Users should understand material sharing consequences.

## Principle

Sharing family history should be deliberate, understandable, and respectful.
