# FEMC Notification Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Notification Classes

Conceptually distinguish:

- required security notifications;
- important family notifications;
- event reminders;
- celebration prompts;
- optional recommendations.

## Principles

1. Notifications are derived from authorized context.
2. Users should have meaningful control over non-essential notifications.
3. Critical information must not depend on one notification channel.
4. Notification failures must not corrupt canonical family data.
5. Frequency must be governed to avoid notification fatigue.

## AI

AI may personalize notification content where authorized, but should not manipulate users into engagement.

## Principle

The best notification is useful at the right moment—not the most frequent notification.
