# FEMC Architecture Quality Attributes

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture Assurance
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Primary Attributes

- correctness;
- privacy;
- security;
- availability;
- resilience;
- portability;
- scalability;
- maintainability;
- observability;
- accessibility;
- cost sustainability;
- evolvability.

## Trade-offs

No architecture maximizes every attribute simultaneously.

Material trade-offs must be explicit and governed.

## Principle

Architecture quality is fitness for FEMC's long-term family mission, not generic technical excellence.
