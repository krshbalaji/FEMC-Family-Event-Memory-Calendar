# FEMC Architecture Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture Foundation
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Mission

Preserve FEMC's long-term coherence by defining the system boundaries, domain relationships, technical principles, and architectural decisions that allow the family platform to evolve safely.

## Architecture Owns

- system architecture;
- domain boundaries;
- integration boundaries;
- architectural principles;
- quality attributes;
- technology-independent structural decisions;
- architecture decision records;
- architectural evolution.

## Architecture Does Not Own

Architecture does not redefine:

- constitutional principles;
- family product meaning;
- implementation details belonging to Engineering;
- day-to-day production operation;
- security/privacy authority.

## Principle

Architecture exists to make future change possible without making the family platform incoherent.
