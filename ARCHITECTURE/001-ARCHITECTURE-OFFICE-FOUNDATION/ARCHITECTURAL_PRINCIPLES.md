# FEMC Architectural Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture Foundation
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Principles

1. Family domain before technology.
2. Canonical truth has explicit ownership.
3. Boundaries are explicit.
4. Authorization follows the data.
5. Derived information does not silently replace source truth.
6. External providers remain replaceable where practical.
7. Components fail independently where possible.
8. Observability is designed, not attached later.
9. Portability is considered before lock-in.
10. Complexity must justify itself.
11. Architecture should be understandable by future teams.
12. Evolution must preserve existing family value.

## Principle

The best architecture is not the most sophisticated architecture. It is the architecture that remains trustworthy and changeable over time.
