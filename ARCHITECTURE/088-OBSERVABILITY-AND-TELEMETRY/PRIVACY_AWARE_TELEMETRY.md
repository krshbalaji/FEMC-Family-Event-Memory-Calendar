# FEMC Privacy-Aware Telemetry Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Objective

Make operational diagnosis possible while minimizing unnecessary exposure of family information.

## Rules

1. Do not log sensitive family content merely because it is available.
2. Prefer identifiers, categories, counts, timings, and technical metadata where sufficient.
3. Protect telemetry with access controls.
4. Define retention according to operational need.
5. Separate security evidence from general operational analytics where appropriate.
6. Ensure AI telemetry does not become behavioral surveillance.

## Principle

The minimum information needed for diagnosis is usually better than the maximum information available.
