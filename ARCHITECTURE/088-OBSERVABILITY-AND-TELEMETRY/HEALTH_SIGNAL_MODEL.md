# FEMC Health Signal Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Signal Classes

### Availability
Can the capability be reached?

### Performance
Is it responding within expected limits?

### Correctness
Is it producing trustworthy results?

### Integrity
Is canonical information remaining consistent?

### Security
Are suspicious or unauthorized conditions occurring?

### Capacity
Is the system approaching resource limits?

### Dependency
Are external services behaving as expected?

### AI Quality
Are AI capabilities operating within acceptable quality and safety boundaries?

## Principle

A green server is not enough. FEMC health must ultimately include trustworthy family functionality.
