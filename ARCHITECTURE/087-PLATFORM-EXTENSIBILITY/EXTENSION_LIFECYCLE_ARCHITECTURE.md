# FEMC Extension Lifecycle Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Lifecycle

```text
PROPOSE
 ↓
REVIEW
 ↓
AUTHORIZE
 ↓
INTEGRATE
 ↓
MONITOR
 ↓
UPDATE
 ↓
SUSPEND / REVOKE
 ↓
RETIRE
```

## Requirements

Extension lifecycle management should consider:

- security;
- privacy;
- reliability;
- compatibility;
- family impact;
- provider dependence;
- data portability.

## Retirement

Retiring an extension must not make canonical family information inaccessible.

## Principle

An extension is a participant in the ecosystem, never the owner of the ecosystem.
