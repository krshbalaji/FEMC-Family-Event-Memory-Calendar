# FEMC Capability Lifecycle Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Lifecycle

```text
DISCOVER
  ↓
DESIGN
  ↓
BUILD
  ↓
VALIDATE
  ↓
OPERATE
  ↓
EVOLVE
  ↓
RETIRE
```

## Capability Requirements

Every material capability should have:

- clear purpose;
- domain owner;
- trust boundary;
- data ownership;
- dependencies;
- failure behavior;
- quality criteria;
- operational owner;
- retirement or replacement consideration.

## Retirement

Retiring a capability must not destroy canonical family information.

## Principle

Capabilities may come and go.

The family record must remain.
