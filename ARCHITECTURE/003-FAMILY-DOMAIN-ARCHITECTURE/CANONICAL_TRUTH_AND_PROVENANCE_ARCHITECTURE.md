# FEMC Canonical Truth and Provenance Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Domain Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Information Classes

```text
CANONICAL
   ↓
DERIVED
   ↓
AI INTERPRETATION
   ↓
PRESENTATION
```

## Rules

Canonical information has explicit ownership.

Derived and AI-generated information must retain a conceptual relationship to its source.

Presentation formatting must never silently become domain truth.

Conflicting information requires governed resolution rather than silent overwrite.

## Principle

FEMC must always be able to distinguish what the family recorded from what the platform inferred.
