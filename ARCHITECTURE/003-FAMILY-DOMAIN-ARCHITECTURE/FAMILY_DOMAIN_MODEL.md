# FEMC Family Domain Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Domain Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Core Entities

```text
FAMILY
 ├── PERSON
 ├── RELATIONSHIP
 ├── EVENT
 ├── MEMORY
 ├── MEDIA
 ├── STORY
 ├── PLACE
 ├── TRADITION
 └── LEGACY
```

Supporting concepts include:

- authorization context;
- family membership;
- privacy context;
- provenance;
- time;
- lifecycle.

## Rule

Domain meaning belongs to the family domain, not to presentation screens, AI prompts, or storage schemas.

## Principle

The domain model is the long-lived semantic center of FEMC.
