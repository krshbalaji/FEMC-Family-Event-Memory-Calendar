# FEMC Family Relationship and Time Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Domain Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Relationship Model

Family relationships are contextual domain information and may evolve over time.

Do not assume:

- one permanent relationship state;
- one universal visibility rule;
- one family structure;
- one interpretation of historical context.

## Time

Important family information may have:

- event time;
- recorded time;
- effective time;
- historical context;
- future expectation.

## Principle

FEMC must model family life as something that changes through time while preserving historical meaning.
