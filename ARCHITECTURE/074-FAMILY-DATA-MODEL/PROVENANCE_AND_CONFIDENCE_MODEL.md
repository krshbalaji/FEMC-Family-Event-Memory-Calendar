# FEMC Provenance and Confidence Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Provenance

Important information may originate from:

- family member input;
- import;
- migration;
- external integration;
- system derivation;
- AI transformation.

## Confidence

Confidence is not the same as truth.

A record may be:

- confirmed;
- reported;
- approximate;
- disputed;
- inferred;
- unknown.

## AI

AI-generated information remains derived unless explicitly confirmed through an appropriate human or domain process.

## Principle

FEMC should preserve what is known, who supplied it, and how certain it is without pretending uncertainty does not exist.
