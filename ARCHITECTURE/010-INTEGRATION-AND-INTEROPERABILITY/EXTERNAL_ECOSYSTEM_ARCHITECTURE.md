# FEMC External Ecosystem Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Ecosystem Participants

Potential participants include:

- communication services;
- media services;
- AI providers;
- identity providers;
- family-facing applications;
- future partner systems;
- export/import tools.

## Boundary

External participants receive only the capabilities and data necessary for their governed purpose.

## Exit

Every material external dependency should have a defined disablement, replacement, or graceful-degradation strategy.

## Principle

FEMC should be extensible without becoming dependent on an uncontrolled ecosystem.
