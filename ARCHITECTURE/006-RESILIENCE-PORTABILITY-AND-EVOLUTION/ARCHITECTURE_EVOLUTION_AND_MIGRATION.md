# FEMC Architecture Evolution and Migration

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Evolution Flow

```text
OBSERVE
 ↓
IDENTIFY LIMIT
 ↓
ASSESS IMPACT
 ↓
DESIGN TRANSITION
 ↓
MIGRATE
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
RETIRE OLD PATH
```

## Migration Rules

- Preserve canonical family meaning.
- Prefer incremental migration where risk warrants.
- Maintain rollback or recovery options for material changes.
- Verify derived data after migration.
- Record architectural rationale.

## Principle

Ten-year architecture is less about predicting the final system and more about making the path between systems safe.
