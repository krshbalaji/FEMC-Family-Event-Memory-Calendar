# FEMC Architecture Risk Register

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Risk Categories

### Domain Risk
Technical structures distort family meaning.

### Data Risk
Canonical information becomes inconsistent or unrecoverable.

### Privacy Risk
Family information crosses an unintended boundary.

### Security Risk
Unauthorized users or services gain access.

### AI Risk
AI fabricates, leaks, overreaches, or becomes an uncontrolled dependency.

### Vendor Risk
A provider becomes difficult to replace.

### Scale Risk
Growth creates unacceptable performance or cost.

### Operational Risk
Failures cannot be detected or recovered.

### Complexity Risk
Architecture becomes harder to maintain than the problem requires.

## Principle

Risk should be recorded while it is still cheap to change the architecture.
