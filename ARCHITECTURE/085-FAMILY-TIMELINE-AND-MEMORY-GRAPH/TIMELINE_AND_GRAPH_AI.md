# FEMC Timeline and Memory Graph AI Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## AI Uses

AI may assist with:

- timeline summaries;
- memory clustering;
- duplicate discovery;
- relationship suggestions;
- story generation;
- contextual search.

## Controls

AI-generated relationships or summaries must remain distinguishable from canonical records.

## Grounding

Material AI outputs should be traceable to authorized source context where practical.

## Human Confirmation

High-impact corrections or relationship changes should require appropriate human confirmation.

## Principle

AI may reveal connections already supported by family information; it must not create fictional family history.
