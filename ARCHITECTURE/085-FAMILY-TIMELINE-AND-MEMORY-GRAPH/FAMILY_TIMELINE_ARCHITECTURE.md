# FEMC Family Timeline Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Purpose

Define the family timeline as a connected interpretation of canonical family information rather than a simple chronological list.

## Timeline Sources

```text
People
Relationships
Events
Time
Memories
Media
Celebrations
Legacy
```

## Model

```text
TIME
 │
 ├── EVENT
 │     ├── PEOPLE
 │     ├── MEDIA
 │     └── MEMORY
 │
 ├── RELATIONSHIP CHANGE
 │
 ├── CELEBRATION
 │
 └── LEGACY MILESTONE
```

## Principles

1. Timeline views are derived from canonical domain information.
2. A family member may see different timelines according to authorization.
3. Approximate and uncertain dates remain distinguishable.
4. Historical corrections must not silently rewrite meaningful history.
5. AI may summarize a timeline but cannot invent events.

## Principle

The timeline is a view of family history, not the family history itself.
