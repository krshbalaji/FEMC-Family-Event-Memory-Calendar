# FEMC Scalability Architecture Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Scale Dimensions

FEMC may scale across:

- families;
- family members;
- memories;
- media;
- search indexes;
- AI workloads;
- events;
- integrations;
- geographic distribution.

## Rules

Scale the appropriate boundary rather than scaling the entire platform indiscriminately.

Canonical family operations should remain protected from noisy secondary workloads.

## Principle

Scalability means growing capacity without proportionally increasing fragility or complexity.
