# FEMC Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Recovery Priorities

Recovery planning must distinguish:

- canonical family state;
- essential access;
- media;
- search/derived systems;
- AI;
- integrations;
- analytics.

## Recovery Questions

- What is the trusted recovery source?
- What can be rebuilt?
- What must be restored?
- What may be temporarily unavailable?
- How is integrity verified?
- How is the family informed when appropriate?

## Principle

Recovery is successful only when FEMC restores trustworthy family continuity, not merely running infrastructure.
