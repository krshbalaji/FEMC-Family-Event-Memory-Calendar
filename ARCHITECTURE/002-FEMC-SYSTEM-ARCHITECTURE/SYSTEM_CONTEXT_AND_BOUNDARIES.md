## Boundary Rule

The FEMC family domain remains the canonical center.

External providers, AI models, search engines, media infrastructure, and integrations must not become the hidden owner of family meaning.

## Principle

FEMC is a family platform with supporting technology—not a technology platform searching for a family purpose.
