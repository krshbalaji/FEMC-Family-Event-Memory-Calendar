# FEMC AI Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Architecture
**Owner:** Architecture Office

## Purpose

Define AI as a governed intelligence layer around the canonical family domain.

## Model

```text
CANONICAL FAMILY DOMAIN
          │
          ▼
   AUTHORIZED CONTEXT
          │
          ▼
   AI ORCHESTRATION
          │
    ┌─────┼─────┐
    ▼     ▼     ▼
  MODEL  TOOLS  MEMORY/RETRIEVAL
    │     │     │
    └─────┼─────┘
          ▼
   VALIDATION / POLICY
          │
          ▼
   DERIVED AI OUTPUT
          │
          ▼
 HUMAN CONFIRMATION / USE
```

## Rules

- AI receives only authorized context.
- AI tools have explicit permissions.
- AI output remains derived.
- Material actions may require confirmation.
- Model providers remain replaceable.
- AI failure cannot block canonical family access.

## Principle

AI surrounds the family domain with intelligence; it does not replace the family domain.
