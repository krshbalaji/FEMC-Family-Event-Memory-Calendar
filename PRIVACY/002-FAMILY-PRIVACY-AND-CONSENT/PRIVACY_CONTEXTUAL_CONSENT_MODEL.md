# FEMC Contextual Consent Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Architecture
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Consent Context

Consent may depend on:

- purpose;
- person;
- family context;
- information type;
- recipient;
- duration;
- processing activity.

## Rules

Consent for one purpose must not silently become permission for unrelated purposes.

Where consent is inappropriate as the legal or governance basis, the applicable governed basis must be explicit.

## Principle

Permission to use information is contextual; it should not become an invisible lifetime license.
