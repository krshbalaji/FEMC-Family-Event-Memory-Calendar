# FEMC Privacy Minimization Retention and Deletion

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Governance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Minimize

Retain only information needed for a legitimate purpose, continuity requirement, or explicitly governed preservation purpose.

## Retention

Retention should distinguish:

- operational data;
- temporary processing data;
- derived data;
- historical family records;
- legal or governance retention.

## Deletion

Deletion must consider:

- canonical records;
- indexes;
- caches;
- media derivatives;
- AI-derived representations;
- exports;
- backups;
- linked records.

## Principle

Deletion is not complete if the same information remains discoverable through a derivative path that should also have been governed.
