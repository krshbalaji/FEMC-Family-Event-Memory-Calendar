# FEMC Family Visibility and Privacy Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Architecture
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Visibility Levels

Information may require distinctions such as:

- private;
- person-specific;
- household;
- selected family;
- family-wide;
- externally shareable.

## Rules

Family-wide context must not automatically expose person-specific information.

Visibility must propagate correctly across search, media, memory, calendar, communication, AI retrieval, and exports.

## Principle

Being connected to a family does not mean being transparent to the entire family.
