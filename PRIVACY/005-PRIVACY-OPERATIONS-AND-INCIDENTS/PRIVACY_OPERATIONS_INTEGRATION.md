# FEMC Privacy Operations Integration

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Operations
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Integration

```text
PRIVACY SIGNAL
 ↓
ASSESS
 ↓
CONTAIN / CORRECT
 ↓
OPERATIONS
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
LEARN
```

## Responsibilities

Privacy works with Operations and Security on:

- access concerns;
- data exposure;
- deletion failures;
- retention failures;
- sharing incidents;
- export issues;
- privacy-related support cases.

## Principle

Privacy incidents are handled operationally while preserving privacy's independent authority to assess the underlying trust issue.
