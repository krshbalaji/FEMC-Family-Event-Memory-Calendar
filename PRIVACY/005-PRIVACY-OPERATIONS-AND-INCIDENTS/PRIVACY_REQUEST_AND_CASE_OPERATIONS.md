# FEMC Privacy Request and Case Operations

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Operations
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Case Types

- access/control question;
- data correction;
- sharing concern;
- export request;
- deletion request;
- retention concern;
- privacy complaint;
- suspected exposure.

## Requirements

Cases should have:

- verified requester authority;
- defined scope;
- responsible owner;
- controlled handling;
- completion evidence;
- escalation where needed.

## Principle

Privacy cases should be resolved consistently without giving support personnel unnecessary access to family information.
