# FEMC Privacy Incident Response

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Operations
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Response

1. Identify the affected information.
2. Protect people and family data.
3. Stop further exposure where possible.
4. Determine scope.
5. Preserve appropriate evidence.
6. Correct or restore trusted state.
7. Assess notification/escalation requirements.
8. Record systemic improvements.

## Examples

- unauthorized family-memory visibility;
- incorrect sharing;
- failed deletion;
- excessive AI context;
- unintended export exposure;
- stale search/index visibility.

## Principle

A privacy incident is about the gap between intended family control and actual information behavior.
