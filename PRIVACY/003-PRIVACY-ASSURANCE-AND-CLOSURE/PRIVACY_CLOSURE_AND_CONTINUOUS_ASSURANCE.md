# FEMC Privacy Closure and Continuous Assurance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Coverage

Privacy foundations now address:

- privacy authority;
- principles;
- contextual consent;
- family visibility;
- minimization;
- retention;
- deletion;
- processing transparency;
- negative testing;
- provider migration;
- recovery.

## Closure Rule

New FEMC capabilities can be evaluated against established rules for purpose, visibility, consent context, minimization, retention, deletion, derived data, AI processing, provider independence, and family dignity.

## Continuous Loop

```text
COLLECT WITH PURPOSE
 ↓
USE WITH CONTEXT
 ↓
SHARE WITH BOUNDARIES
 ↓
RETAIN DELIBERATELY
 ↓
DELETE / PRESERVE
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
IMPROVE
 ↺
```

## Principle

Privacy must remain a living property of FEMC, not a document completed once.
