# FEMC Privacy Data Flow and Processing Register

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Assurance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Track

Material processing should be understandable in terms of:

- source;
- purpose;
- processing;
- recipients;
- storage;
- retention;
- derived outputs;
- deletion behavior.

## Principle

FEMC should be able to explain where sensitive family information comes from, why it is used, and where it goes.
