# FEMC Privacy Provider Migration and Recovery

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Assurance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Verify

After migration or provider replacement, verify:

- privacy classification;
- visibility;
- consent context;
- retention;
- deletion;
- derived-data handling;
- exports;
- backups;
- third-party copies.

## Principle

Changing infrastructure must not silently weaken privacy boundaries.
