# FEMC Privacy Negative Testing

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Assurance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Deliberate Tests

Test:

- cross-family disclosure;
- hidden-record leakage;
- unauthorized search discovery;
- excessive media precision;
- AI inference leakage;
- stale permissions;
- deleted-data resurfacing;
- export overexposure;
- provider-copy persistence;
- family membership exposing private information.

## Principle

Privacy assurance is demonstrated by preventing unintended knowledge, not merely preventing direct record access.
