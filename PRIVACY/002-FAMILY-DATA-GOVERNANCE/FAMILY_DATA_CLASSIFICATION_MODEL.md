# FEMC Family Data Classification Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Governance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Conceptual Classes

- identity and account information;
- family relationships;
- events;
- memories;
- media;
- communications;
- derived/inferred information;
- AI-generated information;
- operational information;
- exports and archival representations.

## Rule

Classification should reflect sensitivity, context, expected use, and potential harm—not merely the database table containing the information.

## Principle

The meaning and sensitivity of family information depend on context as much as on data type.
