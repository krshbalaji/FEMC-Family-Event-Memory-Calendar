# FEMC Sharing, Retention and Deletion Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Governance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Sharing

Sharing must have:

- clear scope;
- authorized recipient;
- understandable consequence;
- appropriate revocation behavior.

## Retention

Retention should be purposeful and distinguish canonical history from temporary or derived representations.

## Deletion

Deletion must account for:

- canonical records;
- media;
- indexes;
- caches;
- AI-derived artifacts;
- operational copies;
- backups and recovery requirements.

## Principle

A privacy promise is incomplete if alternate representations silently keep what the family intended to remove.
