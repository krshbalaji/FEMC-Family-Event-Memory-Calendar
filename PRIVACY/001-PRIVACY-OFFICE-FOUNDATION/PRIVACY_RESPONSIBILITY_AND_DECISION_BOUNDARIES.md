# FEMC Privacy Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Foundation
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Privacy Decisions

The Privacy Office governs:

- purpose limitation;
- data minimization;
- visibility;
- consent context;
- retention;
- disclosure boundaries;
- privacy risk;
- privacy-by-design requirements.

## Coordination

Privacy coordinates with Identity, Family, Data, Memory, Media, Places, Search, Communication, Security, AI, and Product.

## Principle

Privacy establishes boundaries around information use; it does not become the owner of the information's domain meaning.
