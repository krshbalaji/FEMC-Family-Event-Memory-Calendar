# FEMC Privacy Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Foundation
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Principles

1. Collect only what has a legitimate purpose.
2. Use information only within its authorized context.
3. Visibility should be deliberate.
4. Consent is contextual, not a universal permission.
5. Family membership does not erase individual privacy.
6. Sensitive information deserves stronger boundaries.
7. Historical preservation does not automatically justify current disclosure.
8. Derived data can still be personal data.
9. AI inference must respect the same privacy boundaries as source data.
10. Deletion and retention must be governed deliberately.
11. Privacy should survive provider and interface changes.
12. Privacy decisions should remain understandable to families.

## Principle

The safest family platform is not the one that knows the least; it is the one that knows and uses information with the right boundaries.
