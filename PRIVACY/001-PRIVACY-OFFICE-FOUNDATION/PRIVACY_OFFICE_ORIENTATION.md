# FEMC Privacy Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Foundation
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Mission

Protect the dignity, autonomy, confidentiality, and contextual boundaries of people and families while allowing FEMC to preserve meaningful family life.

## Privacy Owns

- privacy semantics;
- data-purpose boundaries;
- consent context;
- visibility principles;
- minimization;
- retention expectations;
- privacy rights and assurance.

## Privacy Does Not Own

Privacy does not redefine:

- family meaning;
- identity;
- event truth;
- memory meaning;
- security implementation;
- product strategy;
- technical implementation.

## Principle

Privacy is not merely hiding data. It is controlling how personal and family information may be collected, understood, used, shared, retained, and remembered.
