# FEMC Privacy Operating Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Foundation
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Lifecycle

```text
UNDERSTAND PURPOSE
 ↓
MINIMIZE
 ↓
CONTROL
 ↓
USE
 ↓
MONITOR
 ↓
RETAIN / ARCHIVE
 ↓
DELETE / PRESERVE
 ↓
LEARN
```

## Rules

Privacy must be considered throughout the family-data lifecycle, including derived information, AI context, media, search, exports, backups, and operational copies.

## Principle

Privacy follows family information wherever FEMC stores, derives, processes, or communicates it.
