# FEMC Privacy Responsibility and Authority

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Foundation
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Responsibility Areas

- data minimization;
- purpose limitation;
- family-context privacy;
- access visibility;
- sharing;
- retention;
- deletion;
- export;
- privacy risk;
- privacy incident response;
- privacy assurance.

## Authority

Privacy review is required when a material capability changes how family information is collected, inferred, used, shared, retained, or exposed.

## Principle

Privacy authority protects family expectations without becoming an excuse for unnecessary product friction.
