# FEMC Consent and User Control Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Governance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## User Control

Where consent or explicit choice is appropriate, the experience should make clear:

- what is being enabled;
- why it is needed;
- who may benefit;
- what may be shared or processed;
- how the choice can be changed.

## Rules

Consent must not be used to disguise unnecessary collection or bypass authorization.

Withdrawal or change of a choice should have defined consequences.

## Principle

User control is meaningful only when the user can understand and change the relevant behavior.
