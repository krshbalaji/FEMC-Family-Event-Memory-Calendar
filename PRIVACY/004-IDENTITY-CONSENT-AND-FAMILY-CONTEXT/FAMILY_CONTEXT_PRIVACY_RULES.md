# FEMC Family Context Privacy Rules

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Architecture
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Context Matters

The same information may have different privacy implications depending on:

- family relationship;
- event context;
- memory context;
- audience;
- time;
- communication channel;
- intended purpose.

## Rules

Avoid broad visibility simply because information is connected to the same family.

Private, shared, and public contexts must remain distinguishable.

## Principle

FEMC should preserve the natural boundaries families expect around different parts of family life.
