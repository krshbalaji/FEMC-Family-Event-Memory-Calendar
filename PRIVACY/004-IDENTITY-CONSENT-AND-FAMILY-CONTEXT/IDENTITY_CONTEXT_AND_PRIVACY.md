# FEMC Identity, Context and Privacy

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Architecture
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Purpose

Ensure privacy decisions reflect the difference between identity, family relationship, authorization, context, and purpose.

## Rules

A person being known by FEMC does not automatically justify exposing every family record associated with that person.

Privacy decisions should consider:

- who is acting;
- whose information is involved;
- family context;
- intended purpose;
- authorized scope;
- sensitivity;
- reasonable expectation.

## Principle

Family relationships provide context; they do not erase privacy boundaries.
