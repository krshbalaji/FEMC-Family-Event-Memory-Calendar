# FEMC Privacy Testing and Evidence

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Assurance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Test Areas

- family isolation;
- authorization;
- sharing;
- revocation;
- search;
- media;
- AI context;
- exports;
- deletion;
- retention;
- backups;
- operational access.

## Evidence

Privacy claims should be supported by tests, reviews, observed behavior, or other appropriate evidence.

## Principle

Privacy controls must be tested across alternate information paths, not only the primary user interface.
