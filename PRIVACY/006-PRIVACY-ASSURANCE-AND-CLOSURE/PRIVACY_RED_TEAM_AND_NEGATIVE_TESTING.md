# FEMC Privacy Red Team and Negative Testing

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Assurance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Test Deliberately

Attempt to:

- access another family context;
- discover revoked memories;
- retrieve private media through search;
- expose deleted information through caches;
- provide excessive AI context;
- misuse exports;
- exploit operational privileges;
- infer restricted information from derived results.

## Principle

Privacy is strongest when the system is tested against curious, mistaken, and malicious behavior—not only intended behavior.
