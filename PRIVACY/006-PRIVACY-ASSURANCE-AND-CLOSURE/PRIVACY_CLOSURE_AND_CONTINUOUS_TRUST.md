# FEMC Privacy Closure and Continuous Trust

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Handoff
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Closure

Privacy foundation is sufficiently defined when new privacy work can be evaluated against established principles for purpose, minimization, context, control, lifecycle, incidents, and assurance.

## Continuous Loop

```text
DESIGN
 ↓
ASSESS
 ↓
RELEASE
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
REAL-WORLD PRIVACY EVIDENCE
 ↓
IMPROVE / RESTRICT / RETIRE
 ↺
```

## Principle

Privacy is never “finished” because family expectations, technology, capabilities, and risks evolve.

Future privacy work should be driven by evidence and material change—not documentation momentum.
