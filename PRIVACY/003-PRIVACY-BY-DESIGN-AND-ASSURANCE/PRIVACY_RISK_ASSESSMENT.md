# FEMC Privacy Risk Assessment

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Assurance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Assess

Consider:

- what information is involved;
- whose information it is;
- purpose;
- access;
- inference;
- sharing;
- retention;
- potential harm;
- reversibility;
- user expectations.

## Outcomes

**LOWER RISK** — ordinary controls are sufficient.

**CONTROLLED** — additional safeguards and monitoring are required.

**ESCALATE** — material privacy risk requires broader review.

**BLOCK** — the capability creates unacceptable privacy risk under current design.

## Principle

Privacy risk should be assessed before the system makes the risk permanent.
