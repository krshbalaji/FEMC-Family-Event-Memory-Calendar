# FEMC Privacy by Design Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Architecture
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Design Sequence

```text
FAMILY VALUE
 ↓
DATA NEED
 ↓
MINIMIZE
 ↓
AUTHORIZATION
 ↓
PROCESS
 ↓
LIMIT EXPOSURE
 ↓
RETENTION / DELETION
 ↓
VERIFY
```

## Principles

- privacy before implementation;
- least necessary information;
- privacy-preserving defaults;
- contextual access;
- transparent material behavior;
- reversible choices where appropriate;
- measurable controls.

## Principle

Privacy should be designed into the capability rather than repaired after deployment.
