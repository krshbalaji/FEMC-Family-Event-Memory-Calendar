# FEMC Privacy Assurance Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Privacy Assurance
**Owner:** Privacy Office

## Conditions

- [ ] Purpose is defined.
- [ ] Data collection is minimized.
- [ ] Access boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Family-context privacy is considered.
- [ ] AI and derived data are addressed.
- [ ] Sharing behavior is understood.
- [ ] Retention is defined.
- [ ] Deletion behavior is defined.
- [ ] Export and backup implications are considered.
- [ ] Privacy risks have evidence and owners.
- [ ] Material user expectations are not violated.

## Outcomes

**READY** — privacy controls support controlled progression.

**CONDITIONAL** — bounded risks have approved mitigations.

**BLOCKED** — material privacy risk remains uncontrolled.

## Principle

Privacy readiness is demonstrated by the capability's behavior, not by the existence of a privacy document.
