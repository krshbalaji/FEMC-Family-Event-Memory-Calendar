# FEMC Family Engagement Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Strategy
**Owner:** Product Office

## Engagement Types

### Functional
Planning and coordination.

### Emotional
Celebration, remembrance, and connection.

### Historical
Discovering family stories and milestones.

### Creative
Storytelling, albums, family projects, and memory creation.

### Legacy
Preserving knowledge for future generations.

## Principle

FEMC engagement should emerge from meaningful family moments rather than engineered dependency.
