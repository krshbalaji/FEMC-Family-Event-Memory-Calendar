# FEMC Long-Term Family Value Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Strategy
**Owner:** Product Office

## Value Accumulation

```text
SMALL FAMILY MOMENTS
        ↓
CONNECTED RECORDS
        ↓
RICHER CONTEXT
        ↓
BETTER DISCOVERY
        ↓
MEANINGFUL REVISITS
        ↓
FAMILY MEMORY
        ↓
LEGACY
```

## Ten-Year Test

A capability is stronger when its value can increase as family history accumulates without creating proportional privacy, complexity, or maintenance burden.

## Principle

FEMC's strongest moat should be the meaningful family history entrusted to it—not dependence on a particular interface or technology.
