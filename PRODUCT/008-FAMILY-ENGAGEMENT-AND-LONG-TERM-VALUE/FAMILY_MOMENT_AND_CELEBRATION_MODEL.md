# FEMC Family Moment and Celebration Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Architecture
**Owner:** Product Office

## Moment Types

- birthdays;
- anniversaries;
- reunions;
- milestones;
- traditions;
- achievements;
- remembrance;
- everyday family moments.

## Product Behavior

AI and automation may assist with preparation, discovery, reminders, storytelling, or communication where authorized.

The system should avoid assuming that every date deserves equal emotional treatment.

## Principle

FEMC should help families notice meaningful moments without manufacturing meaning where none exists.
