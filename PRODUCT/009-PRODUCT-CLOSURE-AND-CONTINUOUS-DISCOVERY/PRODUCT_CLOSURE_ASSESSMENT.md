# FEMC Product Closure Assessment

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Product Office

## Coverage

Product foundations now address:

- product authority;
- family-first principles;
- family value;
- family journeys;
- memory;
- calendar and family time;
- collaboration;
- generational continuity;
- conflict/context safety;
- discovery;
- validation;
- release readiness;
- onboarding;
- adoption;
- engagement;
- celebrations;
- long-term family value.

## Closure Rule

The Product foundation is sufficiently defined when new capabilities can be evaluated against established family-value, trust, experience, evidence, and long-term principles.

## Principle

Product documentation establishes the decision framework; real families determine whether the framework produces meaningful value.
