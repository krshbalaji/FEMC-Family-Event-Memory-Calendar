# FEMC Product Continuous Discovery Loop

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Handoff
**Owner:** Product Office

## Loop

```text
FAMILY NEED
 ↓
DISCOVER
 ↓
HYPOTHESIS
 ↓
VALIDATE
 ↓
PRIORITIZE
 ↓
BUILD
 ↓
RELEASE
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
LEARN
 ↺
```

## Evidence Sources

- family feedback;
- support patterns;
- product behavior;
- privacy/security evidence;
- operational evidence;
- accessibility findings;
- AI evaluation;
- retention of meaningful family value.

## Principle

The roadmap remains alive because family needs remain alive.
