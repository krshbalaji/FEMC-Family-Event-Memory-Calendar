# FEMC Product Portfolio Review Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Handoff
**Owner:** Product Office

## Review Each Major Capability

- Does it strengthen the family core?
- Is it still useful?
- Is it trusted?
- Is it sufficiently used for meaningful reasons?
- Is it maintainable?
- Does it duplicate another capability?
- Does it create unnecessary complexity?
- Can it be simplified?
- Can it be retired safely?

## Outcomes

**INVEST** — expand.

**MAINTAIN** — preserve with controlled evolution.

**SIMPLIFY** — reduce complexity.

**REPLACE** — migrate.

**RETIRE** — remove safely.

## Principle

FEMC should continuously improve its portfolio, including by removing capabilities that no longer deserve their complexity.
