# FEMC Product Strategy Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Strategy
**Owner:** Product Office

## Strategic Horizons

### Foundation
Establish the trusted family core.

### Intelligence
Connect memories, context, search, and AI.

### Family Network
Strengthen communication, celebrations, and collaborative family life.

### Legacy
Make family history durable, portable, and meaningful across generations.

### Ecosystem
Allow carefully governed extensions without losing the family core.

## Principle

Strategy is the sequence in which FEMC creates durable family value—not a list of technologies to adopt.
