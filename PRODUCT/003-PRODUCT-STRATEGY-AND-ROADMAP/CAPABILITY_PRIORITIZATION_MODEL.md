# FEMC Capability Prioritization Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Strategy
**Owner:** Product Office

## Evaluate Each Capability

- family value;
- frequency of meaningful use;
- trust impact;
- privacy impact;
- integration value;
- complexity;
- operational cost;
- portability;
- strategic importance;
- reversibility.

## Priority Classes

**CORE** — essential to FEMC identity.

**ACCELERATE** — materially strengthens the ecosystem.

**EXPERIMENT** — promising but evidence is limited.

**DEFER** — value does not yet justify cost or risk.

**REJECT** — conflicts with FEMC principles or creates disproportionate harm.

## Principle

The best roadmap is not the longest roadmap; it is the one that creates the greatest durable family value with the least unnecessary complexity.
