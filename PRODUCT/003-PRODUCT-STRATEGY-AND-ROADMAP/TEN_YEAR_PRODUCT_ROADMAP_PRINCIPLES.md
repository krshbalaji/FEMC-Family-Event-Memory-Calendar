# FEMC Ten-Year Product Roadmap Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Strategy
**Owner:** Product Office

## Ten-Year Questions

For every major product direction ask:

- Will this still matter to families in ten years?
- Can families migrate away if needed?
- Does it preserve historical meaning?
- Can future technologies replace the implementation?
- Does it increase or decrease complexity?
- Does it strengthen family trust?
- Can future generations understand the resulting family history?

## Principle

Ten-year product thinking does not mean predicting the future. It means designing FEMC so the future can change without destroying the value already entrusted to it.
