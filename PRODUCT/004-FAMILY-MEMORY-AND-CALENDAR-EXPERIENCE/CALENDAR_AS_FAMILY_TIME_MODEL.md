# FEMC Calendar as Family Time Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Architecture
**Owner:** Product Office

## Concept

The calendar is the temporal spine of family life.

```text
TIME
 ↓
EVENT
 ↓
PEOPLE
 ↓
RELATIONSHIPS
 ↓
MEMORY
 ↓
FUTURE EXPECTATION
```

## Rules

Calendar events should be capable of connecting to relevant family context without forcing every event to become a data-heavy record.

## Principle

FEMC should evolve the calendar from a scheduling utility into an understandable view of family time.
