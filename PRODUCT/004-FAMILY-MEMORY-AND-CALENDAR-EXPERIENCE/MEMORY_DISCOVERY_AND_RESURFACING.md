# FEMC Memory Discovery and Resurfacing

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Architecture
**Owner:** Product Office

## Discovery Modes

- by person;
- by relationship;
- by date;
- by event;
- by place;
- by story;
- by media;
- by anniversary;
- by family milestone;
- through authorized AI assistance.

## Resurfacing Rules

Memory resurfacing should be:

- relevant;
- respectful;
- explainable where needed;
- privacy-aware;
- controllable.

Avoid repetitive or emotionally manipulative resurfacing.

## Principle

The right memory at the right moment can strengthen family connection; more memories are not automatically better.
