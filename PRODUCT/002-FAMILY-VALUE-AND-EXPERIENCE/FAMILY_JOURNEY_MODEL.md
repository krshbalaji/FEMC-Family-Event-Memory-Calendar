# FEMC Family Journey Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Architecture
**Owner:** Product Office

## Journey

```text
JOIN / DISCOVER
 ↓
BUILD FAMILY CONTEXT
 ↓
PLAN
 ↓
CELEBRATE
 ↓
CAPTURE
 ↓
REMEMBER
 ↓
REVISIT
 ↓
SHARE
 ↓
PRESERVE
 ↓
PASS FORWARD
```

## Rules

A capability should strengthen one or more connected journeys.

A feature that creates an isolated workflow without improving the family ecosystem requires strong justification.

## Principle

FEMC should feel like one living family space, not a collection of unrelated tools.
