# FEMC Product Experience Quality Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Architecture
**Owner:** Product Office

## Quality Dimensions

- usefulness;
- simplicity;
- warmth;
- clarity;
- accessibility;
- privacy;
- trust;
- continuity;
- responsiveness;
- recoverability;
- emotional appropriateness.

## Rules

A beautiful experience that compromises privacy is not high quality.

An intelligent experience that confuses users is not high quality.

A fast experience that loses family information is not high quality.

## Principle

FEMC experience quality is the intersection of usefulness, humanity, trust, and reliability.
