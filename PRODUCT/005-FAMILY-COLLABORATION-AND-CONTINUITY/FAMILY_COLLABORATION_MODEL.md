# FEMC Family Collaboration Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Architecture
**Owner:** Product Office

## Collaboration Areas

Families may collaborate around:

- events;
- memories;
- photographs;
- stories;
- celebrations;
- family projects;
- legacy preparation.

## Boundaries

Collaboration must preserve:

- identity;
- authorization;
- privacy context;
- ownership;
- attribution;
- conflict handling.

## Principle

Family collaboration should make shared life easier without flattening individual privacy.
