# FEMC Family Continuity and Generational Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Strategy
**Owner:** Product Office

## Generational Horizon

FEMC should support continuity across:

```text
CURRENT FAMILY
 ↓
CHILDREN
 ↓
FUTURE GENERATIONS
```

## Product Considerations

Design for:

- changing family structures;
- new members;
- departed members;
- historical records;
- evolving relationships;
- changing technology;
- future access and portability.

## Principle

Family continuity means preserving context and meaning across generations, not merely preserving storage.
