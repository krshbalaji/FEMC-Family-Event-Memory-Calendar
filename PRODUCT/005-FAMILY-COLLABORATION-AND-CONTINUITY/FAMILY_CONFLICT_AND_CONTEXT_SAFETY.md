# FEMC Family Conflict and Context Safety

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Governance
**Owner:** Product Office

## Product Risks

Family systems may contain:

- disagreement;
- separation;
- sensitive memories;
- conflicting accounts;
- changing relationships;
- different privacy expectations.

## Rules

The product must not assume that all family members share identical interests, permissions, or interpretations.

AI and automated experiences must avoid taking sides or presenting uncertain relationship context as fact.

## Principle

FEMC supports family connection without pretending that every family relationship is simple.
