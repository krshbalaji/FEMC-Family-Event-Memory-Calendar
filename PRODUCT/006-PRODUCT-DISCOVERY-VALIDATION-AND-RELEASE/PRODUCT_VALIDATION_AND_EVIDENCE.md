# FEMC Product Validation and Evidence

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Assurance
**Owner:** Product Office

## Evidence

Evaluate:

- family usefulness;
- comprehension;
- trust;
- accessibility;
- privacy expectations;
- repeat value;
- operational feasibility;
- measurable outcomes.

## Negative Evidence

A capability should be reconsidered when users:

- misunderstand it;
- avoid it;
- distrust it;
- experience unwanted exposure;
- cannot recover from mistakes;
- gain little meaningful value.

## Principle

Validation is about discovering whether FEMC should build something, not proving that something already built deserves to survive.
