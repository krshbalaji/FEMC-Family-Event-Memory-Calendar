# FEMC Product Release Readiness Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Assurance
**Owner:** Product Office

## Conditions

- [ ] Family problem is clearly defined.
- [ ] Intended value is evidenced.
- [ ] Product behavior is understandable.
- [ ] Privacy impact is addressed.
- [ ] Security impact is addressed.
- [ ] Architecture is approved.
- [ ] Engineering verification is complete.
- [ ] Operations readiness is established.
- [ ] AI risks are addressed where applicable.
- [ ] Support implications are understood.
- [ ] Rollback or safe recovery is understood.
- [ ] Material residual risks are owned.

## Outcomes

**READY** — sufficient evidence supports release.

**CONDITIONAL** — release is allowed only within explicit bounds.

**BLOCKED** — the capability is not ready for controlled release.

## Principle

A product capability is ready when the family value, trust, implementation, and operation are all sufficiently evidenced.
