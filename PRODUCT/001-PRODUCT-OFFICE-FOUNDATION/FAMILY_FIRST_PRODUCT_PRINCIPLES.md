# FEMC Family-First Product Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Foundation
**Owner:** Product Office

## Principles

1. Family value before feature volume.
2. Connected experiences before isolated modules.
3. Memory preservation before engagement optimization.
4. Privacy before convenience.
5. Trust before automation.
6. Simplicity before unnecessary complexity.
7. Portability before vendor dependence.
8. Long-term family value before short-term growth tricks.
9. Human control before artificial dependency.
10. Production readiness before public promises.

## Principle

FEMC should become more valuable to a family over time without becoming more intrusive to that family.
