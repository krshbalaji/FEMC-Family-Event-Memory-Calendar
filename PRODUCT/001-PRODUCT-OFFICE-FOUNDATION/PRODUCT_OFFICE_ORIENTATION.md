# FEMC Product Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Foundation
**Owner:** Product Office

## Mission

Define and continuously protect the product meaning of FEMC as a Living Family Digital Space and Family Memory Intelligence Platform.

## Product Owns

- product vision;
- family value;
- product principles;
- capability definition;
- family journeys;
- product priorities;
- product roadmap;
- product outcome evaluation.

## Product Does Not Own

Product does not redefine:

- constitutional non-negotiables;
- architectural implementation;
- engineering execution;
- operational control;
- security authority;
- privacy authority.

Material conflicts are routed through the appropriate governance path.

## Principle

Product decisions begin with family value and end with measurable trust, usefulness, and long-term maintainability.
