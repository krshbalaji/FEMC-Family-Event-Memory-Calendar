# FEMC Product Responsibility and Decision Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Foundation
**Owner:** Product Office

## Product Decisions

Product decides:

- what family problem should be solved;
- which capability creates sufficient value;
- which family journey is prioritized;
- what outcome defines success.

## Product Must Not Decide Alone

Material decisions involving:

- constitutional principles;
- architecture boundaries;
- security controls;
- privacy risk;
- production readiness;
- legal/compliance requirements

must involve the responsible authority.

## Principle

Product owns the **what and why** of family value; the appropriate specialist offices own the constraints and means.
