# FEMC Adoption Health and Product Learning

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Strategy
**Owner:** Product Office

## Healthy Adoption Signals

Consider:

- meaningful family participation;
- memory contributions;
- successful event coordination;
- return visits with purpose;
- family discovery;
- successful sharing;
- long-term retention of family value.

## Avoid

Do not optimize primarily for:

- notification volume;
- screen time;
- artificial streaks;
- addictive engagement;
- unnecessary data contribution.

## Principle

Healthy adoption means FEMC becomes useful to family life, not that FEMC captures more of family attention.
