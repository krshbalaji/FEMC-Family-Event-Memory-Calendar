# FEMC Family Onboarding Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Architecture
**Owner:** Product Office

## Purpose

Help a family enter FEMC with enough value to understand the platform without demanding unnecessary setup or data.

## Onboarding Principle

```text
MINIMUM EFFORT
      ↓
IMMEDIATE FAMILY VALUE
      ↓
TRUST
      ↓
OPTIONAL DEPTH
      ↓
CONNECTED FAMILY SPACE
```

## Rules

- Do not require unnecessary family information before value is demonstrated.
- Explain important privacy and sharing choices.
- Allow gradual enrichment of family context.
- Preserve the ability to correct setup mistakes.
- Do not make onboarding a disguised data-collection exercise.

## Principle

A family should be able to discover FEMC's value before being asked to build the entire family universe.
