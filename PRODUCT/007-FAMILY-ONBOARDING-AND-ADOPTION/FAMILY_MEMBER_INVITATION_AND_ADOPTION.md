# FEMC Family Member Invitation and Adoption

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Product Architecture
**Owner:** Product Office

## Objective

Enable families to grow their shared FEMC space naturally.

## Invitation Principles

Invitations should make clear:

- who is inviting;
- why the person is being invited;
- what they may gain;
- what access they may receive;
- what remains private.

## Adoption

Different family members may participate at different levels.

Do not treat low activity as failure when the member still receives meaningful family value.

## Principle

FEMC should invite participation without turning family relationships into engagement metrics.
