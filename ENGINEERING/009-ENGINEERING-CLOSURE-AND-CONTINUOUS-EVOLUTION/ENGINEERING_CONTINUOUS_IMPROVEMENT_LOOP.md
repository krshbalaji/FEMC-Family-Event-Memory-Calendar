# FEMC Engineering Continuous Improvement Loop

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Handoff
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Loop

```text
IMPLEMENT
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
RELEASE
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
OPERATE
 ↓
LEARN
 ↓
IMPROVE
 ↺
```

## Triggers

Improvement may be triggered by:

- defects;
- incidents;
- performance degradation;
- technical debt;
- dependency change;
- security findings;
- privacy findings;
- architecture change;
- product evolution.

## Principle

Engineering maturity is demonstrated by the speed and safety with which the system learns from its own behavior.
