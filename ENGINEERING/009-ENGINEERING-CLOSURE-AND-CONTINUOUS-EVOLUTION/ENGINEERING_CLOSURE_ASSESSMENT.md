## Closure Rule

The Engineering foundation is sufficiently defined when implementation teams can deliver and evolve capabilities using established principles, verification, release, reliability, and maintenance practices.

## Principle

Engineering documentation should establish the operating discipline; real software behavior supplies the continuing evidence.
