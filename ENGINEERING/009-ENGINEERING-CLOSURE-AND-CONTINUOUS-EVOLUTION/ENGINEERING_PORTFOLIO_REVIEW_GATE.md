# FEMC Engineering Portfolio Review Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Handoff
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Review

For each major engineering area ask:

- Is it maintainable?
- Is it tested?
- Is it observable?
- Is it reliable?
- Is technical debt understood?
- Are dependencies healthy?
- Can it be upgraded?
- Can it be retired?
- Does it preserve architectural boundaries?
- Does it protect family data integrity?

## Outcomes

**RETAIN** — continue.

**IMPROVE** — targeted engineering investment.

**REFACTOR** — structural correction.

**MIGRATE** — controlled replacement.

**RETIRE** — safely remove obsolete implementation.

## Principle

Engineering protects the future by continuously reducing the cost and risk of change.
