# FEMC Portability Engineering

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Design
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Portability Requirements

Engineering should support controlled:

- export;
- import;
- migration;
- validation;
- reconciliation;
- restoration.

## Rules

Portable representations should include sufficient context to reconstruct meaning.

Provider-specific exports may exist, but FEMC should maintain a provider-independent conceptual representation.

## Verification

Portability tests should prove that exported family information can be validated and meaningfully reconstructed.

## Principle

Portability is a product capability that must be engineered, not a promise written in documentation.
