# FEMC Incident and Recovery Engineering

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Design
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Incident Flow

```text
DETECT
 ↓
ASSESS
 ↓
CONTAIN
 ↓
RECOVER
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
LEARN
```

## Priorities

1. Protect family safety and privacy.
2. Protect canonical data integrity.
3. Restore trusted access.
4. Restore secondary capabilities.
5. Rebuild derived systems.

## Requirements

Recovery procedures should be tested rather than assumed.

## Principle

A recovery plan that has never been exercised is only a hypothesis.
