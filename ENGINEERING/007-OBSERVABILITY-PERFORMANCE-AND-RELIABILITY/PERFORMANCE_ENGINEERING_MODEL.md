# FEMC Performance Engineering Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Architecture
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Performance Dimensions

- latency;
- throughput;
- resource consumption;
- concurrency;
- startup time;
- media processing;
- search;
- AI inference;
- background processing.

## Rules

Performance targets should reflect meaningful family experience and architectural limits.

Optimize measured bottlenecks rather than speculative ones.

## Principle

Performance engineering exists to make family experiences responsive and sustainable, not to maximize benchmark numbers.
