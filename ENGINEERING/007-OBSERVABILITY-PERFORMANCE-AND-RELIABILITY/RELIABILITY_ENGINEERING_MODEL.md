# FEMC Reliability Engineering Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Assurance
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Reliability

Reliability includes:

- predictable behavior;
- failure containment;
- recovery;
- data integrity;
- dependency resilience;
- safe degradation.

## Rules

Reliability work should prioritize the family core and high-consequence workflows.

Repeated incidents should produce engineering learning rather than repeated manual fixes.

## Principle

Reliability is the ability to keep trusted family capabilities working and recover them safely when they fail.
