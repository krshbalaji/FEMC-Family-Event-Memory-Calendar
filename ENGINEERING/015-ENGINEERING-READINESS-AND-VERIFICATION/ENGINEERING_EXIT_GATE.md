# FEMC Engineering Exit Gate

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Verification
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Exit Conditions

- [ ] Architecture constraints are implemented.
- [ ] Canonical domain invariants are enforced.
- [ ] Authorization boundaries are verified.
- [ ] Privacy controls are verified.
- [ ] Material APIs and integrations are contract-tested.
- [ ] AI boundaries and output controls are verified.
- [ ] Media and search paths are verified.
- [ ] Family journeys pass end-to-end verification.
- [ ] Migration and recovery are tested where applicable.
- [ ] Observability is operational.
- [ ] Performance and capacity evidence exists.
- [ ] Accessibility requirements are addressed.
- [ ] Production risks are documented and owned.
- [ ] Release and rollback procedures are validated.

## Gate Outcome

**READY** — sufficient evidence exists for production progression.

**NOT READY** — material evidence or controls remain incomplete.

## Principle

Engineering exits when the system is demonstrably trustworthy, not merely feature-complete.
