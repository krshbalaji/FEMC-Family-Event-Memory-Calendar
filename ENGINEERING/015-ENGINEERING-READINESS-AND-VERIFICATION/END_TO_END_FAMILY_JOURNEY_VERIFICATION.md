# FEMC End-to-End Family Journey Verification

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Verification
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Purpose

Verify that connected FEMC journeys work across boundaries rather than only within individual components.

## Required Journey Classes

- create and participate in a family event;
- attach and discover media;
- convert event experience into memory;
- search historical family information;
- share authorized memories;
- revoke access;
- export family history;
- recover from dependency failure;
- use AI-assisted discovery safely.

## Verification

Each journey should verify:

- authorization;
- canonical integrity;
- derived processing;
- failure behavior;
- observability;
- user-visible outcome.

## Principle

FEMC must be tested as one family system, not merely as a collection of components.
