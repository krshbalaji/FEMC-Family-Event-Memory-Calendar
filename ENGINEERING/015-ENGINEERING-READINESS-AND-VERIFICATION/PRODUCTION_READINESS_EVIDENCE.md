# FEMC Production Readiness Evidence

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Verification
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Evidence Categories

- functional verification;
- security verification;
- privacy verification;
- performance evidence;
- resilience and recovery evidence;
- migration evidence;
- observability evidence;
- accessibility evidence;
- dependency review;
- operational readiness.

## Evidence Rule

Claims such as “secure”, “scalable”, or “recoverable” should be supported by tests, measurements, reviews, or documented operational evidence.

## Gaps

Known gaps must have:

- description;
- impact;
- owner;
- mitigation;
- target resolution or accepted rationale.

## Principle

Production readiness is demonstrated, not declared.
