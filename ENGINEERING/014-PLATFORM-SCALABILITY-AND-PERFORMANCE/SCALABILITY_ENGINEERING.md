# FEMC Scalability Engineering

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Design
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Purpose

Prepare FEMC to serve millions of families without designing premature complexity.

## Scaling Dimensions

- families;
- people and relationships;
- events;
- memories;
- media volume;
- search workload;
- AI workload;
- notifications;
- integrations;
- geographic distribution.

## Principles

1. Measure before optimizing.
2. Scale independent workloads independently when justified.
3. Protect canonical transactions from heavy derived workloads.
4. Design for graceful degradation.
5. Avoid architecture whose complexity exists only for hypothetical scale.

## Principle

Scale should follow real workload characteristics while preserving the family domain.
