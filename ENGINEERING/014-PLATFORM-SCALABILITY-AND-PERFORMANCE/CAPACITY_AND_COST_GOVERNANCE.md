# FEMC Capacity and Cost Governance

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Design
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Capacity

Engineering should understand growth drivers for:

- storage;
- compute;
- network;
- search;
- AI;
- media processing;
- notifications.

## Cost

Material architecture choices should consider:

- unit economics;
- growth;
- idle capacity;
- provider pricing;
- migration cost;
- operational burden.

## Rules

Do not optimize cost by weakening privacy, integrity, or recoverability.

Do not optimize scale by adding complexity without evidence.

## Principle

Sustainable scale means technical capacity and economic capacity remain aligned.
