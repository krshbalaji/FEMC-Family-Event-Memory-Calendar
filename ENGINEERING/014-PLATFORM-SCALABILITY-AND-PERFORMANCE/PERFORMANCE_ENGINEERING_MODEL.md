# FEMC Performance Engineering Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Design
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Performance Areas

### Interactive
Family-facing actions and navigation.

### Transactional
Canonical domain changes.

### Asynchronous
Media processing, notifications, imports, exports, indexing.

### Analytical
Search, reporting, recommendations, AI processing.

## Rules

Performance targets should be defined by user and business impact, not arbitrary numbers.

Measure:

- latency;
- throughput;
- resource utilization;
- queue depth;
- failure rate;
- dependency latency.

## Principle

Optimize the journeys families feel, while protecting the integrity of the operations they depend on.
