# FEMC Quality Gates and Change Risk

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Assurance
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Risk-Based Gates

Higher scrutiny applies to changes affecting:

- canonical family data;
- identity;
- authorization;
- privacy;
- security;
- AI tools;
- migrations;
- deletion;
- sharing;
- irreversible actions.

## Gate Principle

A low-risk presentation change should not require the same evidence as a canonical-data migration.

## Principle

Engineering assurance should scale with consequence, not bureaucracy.
