# FEMC Quality Engineering Constitution

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Foundation

## Purpose

Make quality an engineering property rather than a final inspection step.

## Quality Dimensions

- correctness;
- security;
- privacy;
- reliability;
- performance;
- accessibility;
- maintainability;
- portability;
- observability;
- family-experience integrity.

## Rules

1. Test important behavior at the appropriate level.
2. Protect canonical data with stronger verification.
3. Treat authorization as testable behavior.
4. Test failure paths, not only success paths.
5. Validate AI-derived behavior separately from canonical behavior.
6. Keep production readiness evidence.

## Principle

Quality means the system behaves correctly when real families depend on it, including when things go wrong.
