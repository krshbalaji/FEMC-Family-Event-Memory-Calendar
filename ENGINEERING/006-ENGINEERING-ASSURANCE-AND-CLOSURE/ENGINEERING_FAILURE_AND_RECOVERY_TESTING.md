# FEMC Engineering Failure and Recovery Testing

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Assurance
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Test Failure Paths

Verify behavior under:

- dependency failure;
- network failure;
- partial service failure;
- invalid input;
- duplicate requests;
- timeout;
- interrupted deployment;
- corrupted derived state;
- unavailable AI/provider;
- recovery from backup.

## Principle

A system that only works when everything works is not sufficiently engineered for long-lived family use.
