# FEMC Engineering Assurance Evidence

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Assurance
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Evidence

Engineering evidence may include:

- automated test results;
- code review;
- build artifacts;
- dependency checks;
- static analysis;
- integration results;
- deployment verification;
- recovery tests;
- performance evidence.

## Rule

Evidence should correspond to the actual risk of the change.

## Principle

Engineering confidence should be traceable to what was actually verified.
