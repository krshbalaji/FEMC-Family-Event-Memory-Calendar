# FEMC Engineering Closure and Continuous Improvement

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Foundation Closure
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Coverage

Engineering foundations now address:

- engineering authority;
- implementation boundaries;
- engineering principles;
- delivery lifecycle;
- quality;
- testing;
- repository structure;
- code review;
- dependencies;
- reproducibility;
- test automation;
- CI;
- risk-based quality gates;
- release engineering;
- deployment;
- rollback;
- environments;
- assurance;
- failure and recovery testing.

## Closure Rule

The engineering foundation is sufficiently defined when new engineering work can follow established delivery and assurance rules without requiring new doctrine for each implementation.

## Continuous Loop

```text
CHANGE
 ↓
BUILD
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
RELEASE
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
LEARN
 ↓
IMPROVE
 ↺
```

## Principle

Engineering maturity is the ability to make change safely, repeatedly, and measurably.
