# FEMC Engineering Delivery Lifecycle

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Delivery
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Lifecycle

```text
APPROVED INTENT
 ↓
DESIGN / PLAN
 ↓
IMPLEMENT
 ↓
TEST
 ↓
REVIEW
 ↓
INTEGRATE
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
RELEASE
 ↓
OBSERVE
 ↓
LEARN
```

## Rules

A change is not complete merely because code compiles.

Completion requires appropriate evidence that intended behavior, boundaries, tests, and operational consequences have been considered.

## Principle

Engineering delivery is an evidence-producing process, not a code-producing process.
