# FEMC Engineering Quality Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Quality
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Quality Dimensions

- correctness;
- maintainability;
- testability;
- security;
- privacy;
- performance;
- resilience;
- observability;
- accessibility;
- portability;
- cost sustainability.

## Trade-offs

Not every change maximizes every dimension.

Material trade-offs should be explicit and appropriate to the capability's consequence.

## Principle

Engineering quality is fitness for the intended family capability over its useful lifetime.
