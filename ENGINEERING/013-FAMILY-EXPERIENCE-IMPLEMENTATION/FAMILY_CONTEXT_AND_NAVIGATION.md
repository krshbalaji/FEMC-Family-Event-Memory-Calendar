# FEMC Family Context and Navigation Engineering

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Design
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Purpose

Prevent family context from being lost as users move between people, relationships, events, memories, media, and celebrations.

## Requirements

Navigation should preserve or explicitly establish:

- active family context;
- active person or relationship context where relevant;
- authorization scope;
- current workflow;
- unsaved changes where applicable.

## Rules

A route or screen must not infer sensitive family context merely from a client-controlled identifier.

Cross-family navigation requires explicit authorization.

## Principle

Context is part of a family journey and must be treated as governed state.
