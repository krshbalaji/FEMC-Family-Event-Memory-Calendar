# FEMC Accessibility and Human Control

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Design
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Accessibility

Engineering should support:

- keyboard and assistive technology access;
- readable content;
- understandable errors;
- sufficient interaction alternatives;
- media accessibility where practical;
- localization-aware layouts.

## Human Control

Users should understand:

- what will happen;
- what information will be shared;
- what AI is suggesting;
- what is being changed;
- whether an action can be reversed.

## Principle

A family platform is successful only when people can use it confidently and understand what it is doing.
