# FEMC Engineering Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Foundation
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Principles

1. Correctness before cleverness.
2. Readability before unnecessary abstraction.
3. Tests before confidence.
4. Small changes before risky rewrites.
5. Explicit dependencies before hidden coupling.
6. Secure defaults before convenience.
7. Observable behavior before silent failure.
8. Reversible change before irreversible complexity.
9. Automation before repetitive manual error.
10. Documentation at the decision boundary.
11. Technical debt is managed, not ignored.
12. Family data integrity outranks implementation convenience.

## Principle

Good engineering makes the correct behavior easier to maintain than the incorrect behavior.
