# FEMC Engineering Office Orientation

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Foundation
**Authority:** Architecture Handoff

## Mission

Implement FEMC faithfully, safely, maintainably, and production-grade from the approved Constitution and Architecture.

## Engineering Inherits

- product meaning;
- domain boundaries;
- trust boundaries;
- canonical data principles;
- AI boundaries;
- portability requirements;
- resilience requirements;
- quality expectations.

## Engineering Owns

- production implementation;
- implementation-level design;
- code quality;
- automated testing;
- technical implementation decisions;
- build and deployment implementation;
- engineering observability;
- implementation performance.

## Escalation

Engineering must escalate any issue that changes:

- family-domain meaning;
- constitutional principles;
- privacy boundaries;
- authorization semantics;
- canonical data ownership;
- AI authority.

## Principle

Engineering does not discover what FEMC means by accident. It implements what FEMC has already decided to mean.

FEMC Engineering Office Orientation
Version: 1.0.0
Status: Engineering Foundation
Owner: Engineering Office
Mission
Turn FEMC product intent and approved architecture into reliable, maintainable, testable software without silently changing family meaning or architectural boundaries.
Engineering Owns
implementation;
code quality;
engineering standards;
build and delivery practices;
automated testing;
dependency management;
technical implementation evidence;
engineering maintainability.
Engineering Does Not Own
Engineering does not redefine:
constitutional principles;
family product meaning;
architectural boundaries;
security authority;
privacy authority;
operational governance.
Principle
Engineering converts approved intent into working capability while preserving the meaning and constraints that produced that intent.
