# FEMC Legacy and Deprecation Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Governance
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Deprecation Flow

```text
IDENTIFY
 ↓
ANNOUNCE
 ↓
MIGRATE
 ↓
VERIFY
 ↓
DISABLE
 ↓
RETIRE
```

## Rules

Deprecation must account for:

- active consumers;
- stored data;
- integrations;
- operational dependencies;
- rollback;
- documentation;
- support.

## Principle

Removing old technology is successful only when the family value it carried has been safely preserved or intentionally retired.
