# FEMC Dependency Lifecycle and Upgrade Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Governance
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Lifecycle

```text
SELECT
 ↓
ASSESS
 ↓
ADOPT
 ↓
MONITOR
 ↓
UPGRADE
 ↓
REPLACE / RETIRE
```

## Review

Material dependencies should be assessed for:

- security;
- maintenance health;
- compatibility;
- license/usage implications;
- performance;
- portability;
- provider concentration.

## Principle

A dependency is part of FEMC's architecture whether or not it appears in the product UI.
