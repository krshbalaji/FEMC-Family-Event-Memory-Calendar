# FEMC Technical Debt Management Model

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Engineering Governance
**Owner:** Engineering Office

## Debt Classes

- structural;
- dependency;
- test;
- documentation;
- performance;
- security;
- operational;
- data/migration.

## Prioritization

Prioritize debt by:

- risk;
- frequency of impact;
- cost of delay;
- affected family capability;
- compounding effect;
- reversibility.

## Principle

Technical debt is acceptable when intentional, visible, and economically justified. Invisible debt is uncontrolled risk.
